# v1.0.39.0, The Design Briefing Becomes Its Own Artifact Kind

`sk-product-owner` v1.2.23.0 moves the design briefing out of Task Mode. The hub follows: its leaf map gains a lane for it.

## What's New at a Glance

- **A BRIEFING lane.** `ROUTER.md` maps `$briefing` and briefing wording to `briefing-mode.md` and `design-briefing-template.md`, and the Task lane no longer names a briefing
- **A new command.** `$briefing` has no shortcut, because `$b` is Bug
- **Own examples.** Three worked briefings sit under `sk-product-owner/assets/examples/briefing/`. They use the invented product Ordly, so they carry no Barter material

## Changed

- `leaf-manifest.json` is regenerated, and `benchmark/gates/check_leaf_map_parity.py` knows the new lane and the `$briefing` command shape
- `mode-registry.json`, `hub-router.json`, `description.json`, the hub `SKILL.md` and `README.md` carry the new version and name the briefing among the artifacts
- The hub README lists the hub and packet changelogs at their current releases
- The briefing's audience headings are H4 with a spacing rule, and the playbook gains four briefing scenarios per runtime, as `sk-product-owner/changelog/v1.2.23.0.md` records

# v1.0.35.0, FigJam Direct Edits and Bold Edge Labels

A hand-built timeline board showed what the FigJam packet could not do. The packet now edits a board in place behind its own gate, and its edge labels show their outcome in Bold.

## What's New at a Glance

- **Direct edits.** A move, a new column or a restyle is offered as a direct edit with a backup page, a gate and a read-back
- **Bold edge labels.** A short key before a colon and every decision exit are set in Bold by the composer
- **Companions stay in step.** A board's `.md` exports and A4 pages take each copy change

## Changed

- `sk-create-figjam` moves to v1.0.22.0 through v1.0.21.0, as its own notes list
- `sk-product-owner` and the Project kernel do not move

## Upgrade

The Claude Project carries the change in its six Flow files. The kernel stays at v1.2.21.

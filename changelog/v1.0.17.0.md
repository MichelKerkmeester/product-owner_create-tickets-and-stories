# v1.0.17.0, Status Lanes and Detail Cards

A spec board drawn by hand outside Flow Mode showed what the FigJam packet could not draw: a lane for the statuses a shared record moves through, and steps that carry what the source says about them. `sk-create-figjam` v1.0.16.0 adds both and two smaller rules the same board taught.

## What's New at a Glance

- **A status lane.** A laned process may name one lane as the record's statuses, drawn on top as chevrons and moved by dotted lines from the steps
- **Detail cards.** At the Faithful detail level a step may carry a body of short lines under its one-line label
- **A fresh read-back.** A view taken after a later write is read inside the write call, and a stale one is named and retaken
- **Subtitles name the start and the end.** Every shipped example now does

# v1.0.14.0, Row Decisions and Closing Questions

The v1.0.13.0 rerun of the 4 FigJam scenarios named a set of findings. `sk-create-figjam` v1.0.14.0 fixes them.

## What's New at a Glance

- **A decision beside the accent reads second.** A FigJam plan reads a decision joined to the accent on its own row right after the accent
- **Every intake round ends on one closing question.** The question comes after the round's last block, so no reason line follows it
- **Diamond labels are measured at FigJam's size.** The check traces a decision diamond at the size FigJam draws it and reads its label at the width FigJam fits

## Changed

- The Project kernel moves to v1.2.14: its Flow intake round ends on one closing question after its last block

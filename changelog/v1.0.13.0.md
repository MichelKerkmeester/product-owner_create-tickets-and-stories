# v1.0.13.0, Drawn Glance Order and Whole Sketches

The v1.0.12.0 rerun passed none of the 4 FigJam scenarios it reran and named a set of findings. `sk-create-figjam` v1.0.13.0 fixes them.

## What's New at a Glance

- **The glance lines follow the drawn board.** A FigJam plan reads the board in the order a reader meets it, the accent first and then column by column
- **The gate shows the sketch whole.** Every `%%` and `style` line of the checked model appears in the gate's sketch
- **The dials come first.** The detail level, the audience and the reading distance are asked in intake round one

## Changed

- The Project kernel moves to v1.2.13: its Flow gate shows the checked sketch in full, every `%%` and `style` line included, and its first intake round asks the three dials
- The README's version row and release links name this release

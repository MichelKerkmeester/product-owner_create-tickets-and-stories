# v1.0.18.0, Boards That Teach

The FigJam gallery boards looked good but said little beyond the request. `sk-create-figjam` v1.0.17.0 lets a board carry what its source says: notes under each zone's title, bodies under step labels at the Balanced level and a reason for every accent.

## What's New at a Glance

- **Zone and lane notes.** Up to three lines under a zone's or a lane's title, such as where it starts and ends
- **Detail cards at Balanced.** On a user flow, a process, an architecture and a state diagram
- **A reason for every accent,** drawn in the key
- **A compact key** of one band

## Changed

- `sk-create-figjam` moves to v1.0.17.0, as its own note lists
- The Project's composer file carries two code blocks, one for graph boards and one for sequence and gantt boards, and 14 Flow knowledge files change by hand in step with the skill
- The README's version row and release links name this release

## Upgrade

In the claude.ai Project, follow the `sk-create-figjam` v1.0.17.0 note. The kernel stays at v1.2.16, and `sk-product-owner` stays at v1.2.6.0.

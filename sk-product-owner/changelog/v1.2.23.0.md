# v1.2.23.0, The Design Briefing Becomes Its Own Mode

v1.2.22.0 added the design briefing as a sixth task shape. A briefing tells the Product Design team what to design and why, before any dev task exists, so it is not a task. It now stands beside Task, Bug, Doc and Story as its own artifact kind.

## What's New at a Glance

- **A new command.** `$briefing` selects Briefing Mode. It has no one-letter shortcut, because `$b` is Bug, and `$briefings` and `$brief` are ordinary text
- **Its own mode and template.** `references/briefing-mode.md` carries the signal, intake, title, sections, audiences, facets, Why facet and quality checklist. `assets/design-briefing-template.md` carries the scaffold and its Notes For Use
- **Its own examples.** The three worked briefings sit under `assets/examples/briefing/` as `briefing-example-full.md`, `briefing-example-single.md` and `briefing-example-minimal.md`. They use the invented product Ordly, so they carry no Barter material
- **Routing that no longer says Task.** "Write a design briefing", "create a briefing" and "turn this into a briefing" route to Briefing Mode by framing, and a request to create a task to write a briefing stays a task

## Changed

- Task Templates and Task Mode lose Section 7, the Design Briefings entry and every mention of a sixth task shape. The task shapes are five again
- The router contract and the route oracle gain the `BRIEFING` intent with its resource pair, its frame patterns and the `$briefing` command. The task frames drop the noun `briefing`, and the task addition frame leaves "add a briefing for the ..." to the Briefing frame
- A briefing can pair with a FigJam board like any other artifact, so the paired-output set and the board noun lists carry it
- The hub leaf map gains a `BRIEFING` lane, `leaf-manifest.json` is regenerated and the leaf map gate knows the lane
- `AGENTS.md` gains `$briefing` in the command registry, the token rules, Routing Compatibility, the loading order and the DAG, and its Artifact Titles point at Briefing Mode for the briefing form
- Interactive Mode gains a briefing entry and a Briefing Context Question, the clarification lane word `briefing` and the Quality Scoring reading for a briefing
- Route fixtures grow from 190 to 212 and the differential corpus from 283 to 305 inputs
- Audience headings under Requirements are H4, as in `#### SHOPPER`, and a spacing rule puts two blank lines before Requirements, before each audience heading after the first and before every section after Requirements. The playbook gains four briefing scenarios per runtime, `SBR-001..SBR-004` and `PBR-001..PBR-004`

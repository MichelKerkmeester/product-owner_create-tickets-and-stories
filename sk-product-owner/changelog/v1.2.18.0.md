# v1.2.18.0, Backlog Titles and a Sectioned Story Shape

Task and bug titles came out as sentences. Stories had one shape for every size. Both change here.

## What's New at a Glance

- **Titles are labels.** A Task is `{Team} - {App} - {Area} - {Detail}`. A Bug names its defect as the last segment
- **No `BUG` lead and no date.** ClickUp shows the type. The export file keeps its number
- **Stories lead with the app.** Their build tasks carry the team. An Epic keeps its `Epic - ` lead
- **The team is asked for once.** Under `$quick` the lead is left out and listed under Open items
- **Three Story options.** Problem and Solution as lists, numbered requirement sections and design links per section
- **Options, not rules.** The compact shape stays the default. Scope and length decide
- **Sibling Stories per platform.** They share one skeleton and word shared sections the same
- **A new worked example.** A sectioned Mobile Story for a fictional thermostat app

## Changed

- Eight task and bug examples carry segmented titles
- Design links inside a requirement section are plain lines under a `Design` label
- The `Checklist` label and nested checklist items stay banned
- The README counts 23 worked examples and 68 Project documents

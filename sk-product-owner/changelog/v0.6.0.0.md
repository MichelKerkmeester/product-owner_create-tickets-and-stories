# v0.6.0.0, Stories Draft in the House Format

Story Mode now drafts in the Barter ClickUp house format by default. That is the shape the team already writes. The earlier ceremony becomes optional enrichment a writer pulls in only when a story earns it.

Every tier renders that same house shape. A story is validated by coverage and requirement-tracing. Task, Bug, Doc and Interactive behavior did not change.

## Why This Release

The change came from an audit of five real house stories against the old template. The template prescribed a heavier shape than the team uses. The template moved to the team rather than the other way around.

## What's New at a Glance

- **Story Mode drafts in the Barter ClickUp house format by default.** One house shape, sized to the requirement count, replaces the three separate tier scaffolds.
- **The heavier ceremony is optional enrichment.** The per-requirement Connextra value line, the exact Rule block, the Which-means-that implications, the Definition of Ready and Definition of Done gates, the shared-mechanism section and the `← PRIO` marker are added only when a story earns them.
- **Tiers are size only.** Every tier renders the same house shape. A Simple story typically uses no enrichments and a Complex story typically uses several.
- **Acceptance criteria are numbered Given/When/Then blocks.** Named `**Scenario:**` blocks and the create-time scenario-count export gate are retired. A story is validated by coverage and requirement-tracing, not by a heading count.
- **Create-time shape checks do not fire on a source-preserving refinement.** The rule resolves the earlier collision between the export gate and refinement preservation.
- **Research and feasibility spikes have a stated route.** They go to Task or Doc Mode, or they stay on the story surface with acceptance that asserts the documented decision.
- **The worked examples were rewritten in the house shape.** Simple uses no enrichments. Medium adds a Documentation link and one Rule block, while Complex adds the shared-mechanism section, a `← PRIO`, per-requirement value lines and a Definition of Done.
- **A complete reference shows every option at once.** `story-example-complete.md` populates every section and every optional enrichment in one story.
- **The router treats Story grammar as distinct from Doc.** Story artifacts use `- [ ]` checklists, H3 section anchors, numbered acceptance criteria with a Mark-as-done checkbox and the blockquote preamble. Doc keeps `*   [ ]` checklists and H2 anchors.
- **The kernel carries the same Story rules.** `Custom Instructions.md` is now v0.5.0, aligned to Skill v0.6.0. Router token rules, tie-breaks and Quick-energy handling are untouched.

## The House Shape

#### One Template Replaces Three Scaffolds

`assets/story-templates.md` replaced the three separate tier scaffolds with one house shape sized to the requirement count. The story opens with an italic blockquote preamble. An About block carries the User Story promise plus Problem and Solution. Under Requirements, each requirement has a short prose lead and a `**Checklist**` of buildable `- [ ]` items. Acceptance criteria are numbered `1.` Given/When/Then blocks. Each block closes with `- [ ] _Mark as done, if the criteria are met_`.

&nbsp;

#### ClickUp Keeps the Headings Small

Section anchors sit at H3 because ClickUp renders H2 very large. The H1 slot is `Story - {Platform/Area} - {Feature} - {Name}`. House codes are an open set: `BO`, `BE`, `FE` or a named area. The title includes an optional version tag.

&nbsp;

#### Enrichments Live in a Catalog

Section 4 of the templates catalogs the per-requirement Connextra value line, the exact Rule block, the Which-means-that implications, the Definition of Ready and Definition of Done gates, the shared-mechanism section and the `← PRIO` marker. A writer adds one only when a story earns it.

## How Stories Are Checked

#### Tiers Only Set the Size

`references/story-mode.md` restates tiers as size only. Every tier renders the same house shape.

&nbsp;

#### Coverage Replaces the Export Gate

Acceptance criteria are numbered Given/When/Then blocks rather than named `**Scenario:**` blocks. That change retired the create-time scenario-count export gate. A story is validated by coverage and requirement-tracing, not by a heading count.

&nbsp;

#### Refinement Leaves the Source Alone

Create-time shape checks do not fire on a source-preserving refinement. The rule resolves the earlier collision between the export gate and refinement preservation.

&nbsp;

#### Spikes Stay Honest

A note routes research and feasibility spikes to Task or Doc Mode, or keeps them on the story surface with acceptance that asserts the documented decision.

## Router Grammar and the Kernel

#### Story Grammar Has Four Carve-Outs

`SKILL.md` is now v0.6.0. It gained four carve-outs so the router's grammar contract recognizes the Story house format as distinct from Doc. Story artifacts use `- [ ]` checklists and Doc keeps `*   [ ]`. Story section anchors are H3 and Doc keeps H2. Story acceptance criteria are numbered and include a Mark-as-done checkbox. Story artifacts use the blockquote preamble.

&nbsp;

#### Mode Notes Name the House Format

The Mode Selection note and the Story blocking gate now name the house format. No routing, token or energy rule changed.

&nbsp;

#### The Kernel and the README Match

`Custom Instructions.md` is now v0.5.0, aligned to Skill v0.6.0. The Story tier guard counts acceptance criteria rather than scenarios and drops the hard blocking-failure framing. A new-Story grammar rule joined the ClickUp-format section. The Story routing row names the house format and optional gates. `claude project/README.md` updated its source-to-mirror map, its aligned Skill version and its mirror counts.

# v1.2.22.0, The Design Briefing Template

A request to brief the Product Design team on what to design and why had no shape. Product Owner now writes it as a design briefing, a task shape titled `Briefing - {App or Apps} - {Area} - {Detail}`.

## What's New at a Glance

- **A sixth task shape.** About, Briefing and Requirements are required. References, Related tasks, Deliverables, Copy, Open Questions, Success Signal and Dev Handover are optional and removed whole when they do not apply
- **Audiences and facets.** Requirements groups its checklist by ALL-CAPS audience headings and bold facet lines such as `**Display**`, `**Validation**` and `**Existing Deals**`
- **A Why facet.** Under `### DESIGN SYSTEM`, a `**Why**` facet carries plain bullets for the reason behind a component trade-off
- **A third own title form.** A briefing leads with `Briefing`, joins two apps with `&` and names the area in full, such as `Barter Deals`

## Changed

- Task Templates gains Section 7 with the scaffold and its Notes For Use, and three worked examples sit under `assets/examples/task/`
- Task Mode gains a Design Briefings entry with signal, intake, title, section, audience and facet rules, and the quality checklist gains three lines
- `AGENTS.md` Artifact Titles lists three kinds that keep their own form, and File Naming gains `export/[###] - briefing-[description].md`
- The format gate accepts a plain bullet under a `**Why**` facet in Requirements, only when the document title starts `Briefing - `. A `**Why**` facet in any other document, and a plain bullet under any other facet, still fail
- The two validator copies carry the same change, and both fixture runners gain four cases for it
- "Write a design briefing" and "create a briefing" now route to Task Mode by framing. The router contract, the kernel copy, the hub artifact words and the route oracle carry the noun `briefing`, and two route fixtures pin it

# v1.2.15.0, Browser-First Doc Pages and Mark-as-Done Between Dividers

## What's New at a Glance

- **The page body is pasted in the browser.** Section 7 creates the page with `[[BODY]]` and runs the `mcp-click-up` page writer over it
- **Gray styling survives.** Numbered titles keep a gray, regular number. The Mark-as-done line reads gray italic
- **Columns come in the same paste.** The push wraps each screenshot pair in a `[[COLS]]` block
- **Markdown stays the fallback.** Without a browser the page takes markdown. The gap pass and columns routine follow
- **Mark-as-done sits between two dividers.** Under the last criterion the lower divider is the section close, written once

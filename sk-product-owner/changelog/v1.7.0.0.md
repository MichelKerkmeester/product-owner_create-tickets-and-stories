## [**1.7.0.0**] - 2026-08-13

PRD Mode is rebuilt around the two artifact kinds the Barter team actually writes in ClickUp — **Story** and **Epic** — replacing the Simple/Medium/Complex tier model. The four dated Onboarding v2 stories and the Onboarding v2 epic are the structural basis. A Story keeps requirements as bold-lead outcome bullets (no build checklist), and every artifact now closes with a `## Delivery` section (Estimation, Rabbit holes, No-gos). Epic becomes a real, supported shape (Goal + Scope of child stories, release-level acceptance criteria, no Requirements), retiring the long-standing orphan "Epic" reference the skill carried without a template.

> Scope: the PRD source pair (`assets/prd-templates.md` → v0.300, `references/prd-mode.md` → v0.300), the router and contract lines in `SKILL.md` and `AGENTS.md`, the four PRD examples plus a new Epic example, the `claude project/` kernel and README, `SYNC.md`, `claude-project.sync.json`, the router benchmark, and the regenerated knowledge mirrors and checksums. The five real Onboarding source documents govern structure only and were not modified. Implemented against packet `035-product-owner-prd-delivery`.

### Story And Epic Shapes

`assets/prd-templates.md` now teaches one Story shape and one Epic shape under shared house grammar. The Story H1 drops the `PRD -` prefix and `BO`/`BE`/`FE` short codes for the plain hyphen path (`{Persona} - {Area} - {Feature}`); the preamble is plain italic lines rather than a blockquote; `#### **Expected outcomes**` and `#### **References**` sit at H4 under About; requirements are bold-lead names with natural `*   ` outcome bullets and optional supplied screenshots, and never a `**Checklist**`. The Epic H1 is `Epic - {Persona} - {Area}`, carries `### Goal` and a `## Scope` of child stories with an optional `Added Later` group, keeps acceptance criteria at release level, and has no `## Requirements`. Both close with `## Delivery`. Connextra user-story blocks, per-requirement value lines, Rule blocks, Which-means-that and Definition of Ready/Done remain available as optional enrichments. Story and Epic are artifact kinds, not size tiers.

### Routing

`$epic` and `$e` join `$prd`/`$p`/`$story`/`$s` as exact PRD-Mode commands; `$prd`/`$p`/`$story`/`$s` select the Story shape and `$epic`/`$e` select the Epic shape, and the two families never conflict because both resolve to PRD Mode. Natural "write/create/refine an epic" framing and an `# Epic -` H1 also select the Epic shape. Embedded tokens (`$epics`, `$email`, `$e.md`) are excluded by the standing exact-token boundary rule. The zero-requirements case flips from a clarification ("an epic brief, not a PRD") to a valid Epic-shape signal. `SKILL.md` and `Custom Instructions.md` carry the same routing; the kernel's Smart Router block was re-derived from `SKILL.md` by the sync compiler.

## [**1.6.0.1**] - 2026-08-09

Rebrand release. The Story artifact intent is renamed to **PRD** (Product Requirements Document) across both packagings and the executable route contract. This is a pure rename: the Barter house-format artifact, the Simple/Medium/Complex tier intelligence, the router logic and every gate are unchanged. `SKILL.md`, `Custom Instructions.md` and `description.json` stay pinned at their current versions (`1.6.0`, `1.6.0`, `1.6.0.0`); no mode or template reference version changed. Every mirror re-derived at its existing version pin under its new filename.

> Scope: one intent renamed (`STORY` to `PRD`), two new command tokens (`$prd`/`$p`) with the old `$story`/`$s` kept as back-compat aliases, six source files and their six knowledge mirrors renamed, the router pseudocode and `route_contract.py` intent rename, three new router fixtures, and the manifest/retiredNames bookkeeping. Router fixtures ran 26/26 and all 31 mirrors verified byte-identical after the change. No artifact shape, tier logic, confidence threshold, Doc/Task/Bug behavior or delivery contract changed.

---

### Story Mode Renamed To PRD Mode

The fourth artifact intent is now **PRD**. `references/story-mode.md` became `references/prd-mode.md` and `assets/story-templates.md` became `assets/prd-templates.md`; the four worked examples moved from `assets/examples/story/` to `assets/examples/prd/` as `prd-example-{simple,medium,complex,complete}.md`. Every knowledge mirror re-derived at its existing version pin under the new title: `Product Owner - Templates - PRD Mode - v0.200.md`, `Product Owner - Assets - PRD Templates - v0.200.md` and the four `Product Owner - Examples - PRD - *` files. The six superseded Story mirror names were removed from `claude project/knowledge/` and recorded in `claude-project.sync.json`'s `retiredNames`; `expectedKnowledgeCount` stays 31.

&nbsp;

### Commands: $prd And $p Added, $story And $s Kept As Aliases

`$prd` and `$p` are the primary PRD commands. `$story` and `$s` are retained as back-compat aliases so existing muscle memory still routes, and `$prds`/`$stories` remain false-prefix non-commands. All four tokens resolve to the `PRD` intent. The command surfaces updated in lockstep: `SKILL.md`'s Command Triggers, Primary Detection Signal, Phase Detection, Mode Selection Notes and Success Criteria; `AGENTS.md`'s Command Registry and Routing Compatibility; `Custom Instructions.md`'s routing prose, Operating Model and Smart Routing Matrix; and `interactive-mode.md`'s command-detection YAML and state names (`story_intake_gate` to `prd_intake_gate`, etc.).

&nbsp;

### Internal User-Story Elements Preserved

The rebrand renames the mode, its commands, its files and the artifact-type label only. The narrative machinery a PRD carries stays a user story: the `#### **User Story**` block, the Connextra "As a {role}, I want {capability}, so that {benefit}" lines, the per-requirement `**Story:**` value-line label, the Given/When/Then acceptance criteria and the verbatim ClickUp preamble are unchanged. Natural-language triggers "write a user story", "turn this into a story" and "refine this story" still route to PRD Mode; the semantic and framing signals gained `prd` and `product requirements document` alongside them. The house-format H1 convention moved from `Story - {Platform/Area} - {Feature} - {Name}` to `PRD - {Platform/Area} - {Feature} - {Name}`, and new exports save to `export/[###] - prd-[description].md`.

### Route Contract And Fixtures

`benchmark/router/route_contract.py` renamed the `STORY` intent to `PRD` in `ARTIFACT_COMMANDS`, `INTENT_VALUES`, `RESOURCE_MAP`, `FRAME_PATTERNS` and `SEMANTIC_TOPICS`, added `$prd`/`$p` to the command table and `prd`/`product requirements document` to the framing and semantic signals, and added `prd` to the natural-quick alternation. `fixtures.json` gained three cases (`$prd`, `$p`, and a `write a prd for ...` framing case), retargeted the two existing Story command fixtures to intent `PRD`, and repointed all Story resources to `references/prd-mode.md` and `assets/prd-templates.md`. `run_fixtures.sh` prints `PASSED 26/26 fixtures` and exits 0. The `SKILL.md` Smart Router Pseudocode block carries the same PRD intent, command detection and preserved user-story synonyms, and re-derived byte-identical into `Custom Instructions.md` on `sync --write`.

`claude-project.sync.json` repointed the six renamed mirror sources and targets, keeping every version pin, and appended the six superseded Story mirror names to `retiredNames` beside the earlier v0.100/v0.102/v0.103 Story entries. `sync --write` re-rendered all mirrors, spliced the router into the kernel, and refreshed the `SYNC.md` INVENTORY region, the `claude project/README.md` CHECKSUMS region and `package-lock.json`.

# v1.1.0.0, One Outcome per Line and a Story That Opens on Its Problem

The PM who files Stories from this system edited four things by hand on every ticket. A Then line joined two outcomes with ", and", every Story opened with two intro lines, About and Problem split one problem statement across two sections, and each Delivery sub-heading carried a helper sentence.

## What's New at a Glance

- **One outcome per Then or And line.** In Story and Epic criteria, a second outcome joined to a Then line with "and" moves to its own bold `*   **And**` line, and so does each outcome after it. An "and" inside one outcome stays, as in "name and email". Task and Bug criteria are unchanged.
- **Criteria number as `1 )`.** Each Story and Epic criterion opens on its number, a space and a closing bracket, as in `1 ) **{Title}**`, in place of `1\.`.
- **A Story opens on `## Problem`.** The two intro lines are gone, and About and Problem merge into one Problem paragraph of three to five sentences on the business problem. `## Solution` moves to H2 beside it, and the nested Tasks block now sits inside Solution. That paragraph is the one exception to the three-sentence paragraph cap. Epics keep their About umbrella.
- **Delivery keeps only its placeholders.** Estimation, Rabbit holes, No-gos and External dependencies lose their helper sentences in both shapes, and `TBD...` stays where a value is unknown.
- **The format gate knows both rules.** The Problem paragraph is held to five sentences and 100 words instead of three and 60, and a Then or And line holding ", and" gets advice to split it. The same edit lands in the shared Sync Loop validator, which reference parity holds byte-identical.
- **The worked examples follow the new shape**, and the Story playbook scenarios expect it. Refinement scenarios keep the draft's own opening, as source-preserving refinement requires.
- **The kernel moves to v1.1.0.**

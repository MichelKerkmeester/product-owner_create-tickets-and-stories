# v1.2.20.0, Story Criteria and Checklist Layout

The house Story layout changes in four places. New Stories and Epics now default to it.

## What's New at a Glance

- **Mark as done closes its criterion.** It sits under the divider that closes the Given/When/Then bullets, with no divider under it
- **One empty line between criteria.** The last criterion ends on its Mark-as-done line, with no divider and no spacer after it
- **A Checklist label per section.** Checks directly under a numbered requirement section follow a bold `**Checklist**` label with a divider under it
- **User flows as steps.** `**User flow:**` stands alone on its line, with one bullet per step
- **Screenshots in columns.** On a push, a section's screenshots sit side by side, with one image left and two right for three

## Changed

- Story Mode, both templates, the six Story examples, `AGENTS.md` and `SKILL.md` state the new layout
- A task that holds columns is never rewritten through `markdown_description`, because the read-back drops the columns
- The format gate reads past a bold Part heading in Requirements, so the new rules reach a Story split into parts
- The gate keeps a User flow open across an empty line under its label, and it flags a Checklist label outside a numbered section

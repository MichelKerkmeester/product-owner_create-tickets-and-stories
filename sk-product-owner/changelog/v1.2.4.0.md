# v1.2.4.0, Open Items Name No Board Fact

The v1.0.6.0 FigJam rerun found that the command-conflict question kept to the artifact while its Open items asked the user to confirm the target FigJam file. `references/interactive-mode.md` now holds both to the same rule. No template, example or other route changed.

## What's New at a Glance

- **The conflict reply asks about the artifact alone.** When two artifact commands conflict and one of them draws a board, such as `$task $flow`, neither the question nor its Open items names a board fact, such as the target file, the type or the reader. Those wait for Flow Mode's own round

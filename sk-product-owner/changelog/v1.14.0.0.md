## [**1.14.0.0**] - 2026-09-26

Artifacts now have length caps. A bullet is one sentence of 25 words or fewer, a paragraph is at most three sentences and 60 words, and an About or Overview opening is at most two paragraphs.

> Scope: `SKILL.md`, the kernel, both READMEs, `SYNC.md` and the playbook root. The shared Conciseness card is unchanged.

### Why

The Opus 5.5 benchmark exports read bloated. Across the 98 kept exports, 109 bullets ran past 30 words and 81 paragraphs past 60. The rules said to cut what a reader could rebuild, but never said how short is short.

### What changed

- The Conciseness line in `SKILL.md` (line 276) and the kernel (line 90) now names the caps
- Code, tables, Given/When/Then lines and verbatim source copy are exempt
- A line over a cap is split or tightened, never shortened by dropping a supplied value
- The playbook root names the caps in its Advisory size row, so a miss is reported but never fails a scenario
- The format gate warns on over-cap artifact lines and still exits 0

### Why not the Conciseness card

The card lives in `z — Knowledge/` and Sales, Copywriter and Deal Templates symlink it. The parity gate holds this system's copy to the card byte for byte. The operator chose to keep the caps on this system's own lines.

### Versions

`SKILL.md` moves from 1.13.0 to 1.14.0 and `description.json` to 1.14.0.0. The kernel moves from v1.16.0 to v1.17.0, and its review by the operator is pending. The playbook root moves to 2.3.0.0. Both edits stayed on their own lines, so no line the playbook cites moved.

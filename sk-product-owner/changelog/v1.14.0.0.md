# v1.14.0.0, Artifacts Now Have Length Caps

Artifacts now have length caps. A bullet is one sentence of 25 words or fewer, a paragraph is at most three sentences and 60 words and an About or Overview opening is at most two paragraphs.

## Why This Release

The Opus 5.5 benchmark exports read bloated. Across the 98 kept exports, 109 bullets ran past 30 words and 81 paragraphs past 60. The rules said to cut what a reader could rebuild, but never said how short is short.

## What's New at a Glance

- **Artifacts now have length caps.** A bullet is one sentence of 25 words or fewer, a paragraph is at most three sentences and 60 words and an About or Overview opening is at most two paragraphs.
- **The Conciseness line names the caps.** `SKILL.md` and the kernel now state them. Both edits stayed on their own lines, so no line the playbook cites moved.
- **Code, tables, Given/When/Then lines and verbatim source copy are exempt.** The length caps do not apply to them.
- **A line over a cap is split or tightened.** It is never shortened by dropping a supplied value.
- **The playbook root names the caps in its Advisory size row.** A miss is reported but never fails a scenario.
- **The format gate warns on over-cap artifact lines.** It still exits 0.
- **The shared Conciseness card is unchanged.** Sales, Copywriter and Deal Templates symlink it. The parity gate holds this system's copy to the card byte for byte and the operator chose to keep the caps on this system's own lines.
- **The version labels moved.** `SKILL.md` moves from 1.13.0 to 1.14.0, `description.json` to 1.14.0.0 and the playbook root to 2.3.0.0. The kernel moves from v1.16.0 to v1.17.0 and its review by the operator is pending.

## Upgrade

No migration required. The shared Conciseness card is unchanged.

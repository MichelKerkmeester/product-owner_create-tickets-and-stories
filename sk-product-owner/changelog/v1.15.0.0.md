## [**1.15.0.0**] - 2026-09-26

Each artifact kind now has a word budget: a task inside a Story bundle at most 500 words, a subtask 750, any other task 900, a bug 800, an Epic 1,000, and a Story or a Doc 1,400, counted outside code blocks.

> Scope: `SKILL.md`, the kernel, both READMEs, `SYNC.md` and the playbook root. The shared Conciseness card is unchanged.

### Why

The length caps bound a line, never a whole artifact. The Opus 5.5 benchmark exports met the caps after a first edit and still read long, because that edit split lines and cut 2% of the words. A second edit cut their words by 22.7% with every supplied value kept, and the budget is taken from those cut exports.

### What changed

- The Conciseness line in `SKILL.md` (line 276) and the kernel (line 90) now names the budget after the caps
- An artifact goes over its budget only when supplied values, requirements or criteria need the room, never with restated context or background
- The playbook root names the budget in its Advisory size row, so a miss is reported but never fails a scenario

### How the numbers were set

Each budget sits in the upper range of the cut exports of its kind, rounded, so 68 of the 78 fit. Eight of the other ten run 6% or less over. The two furthest over, the partner onboarding Epic at 1,237 words and the promotion stacking Doc at 1,707, were also the longest of their kind before the cut, at 1,544 and 2,449 words.

### Versions

`SKILL.md` moves from 1.14.0 to 1.15.0 and `description.json` to 1.15.0.0. The kernel moves from v1.17.0 to v1.18.0, and its review by the operator is pending. The playbook root moves to 2.4.0.0. Every edit stayed on its own line, so no line the playbook cites moved.

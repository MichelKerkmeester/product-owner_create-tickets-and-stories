# v0.9.4.0, Unconditional Shape and Closed Field List

The shape ask is unconditional and the Doc intake field list is closed. The turn-1 question asks about shape even when the request's wording suggests one. No routing, command, confidence or delivery behavior changed.

## Why This Release

From a third re-measurement of the Doc intake scenarios, three runs per scenario on GLM 5.3 Flash through Pi were reviewed independently by DeepSeek V4.1 Flash and SWE-2 Max, and checked against the replies and exports before the repair. `SDK-001` run 1 exported a clarification asking for the notes, status, shape, scope and depth, and never asked about authority, and `PDK-001` run 2 asked for the notes, authority, status and scope, then wrote that "the document shape stays unresolved until then even though 'how it works' suggests a behavior reference" and asked nothing about shape. Both reviewers read the round-two sentence the same way: it stated that shape stays unresolved and left the ask to the sentence before it, so "until the notes arrive" could be read as permission to wait, and the run's own wording nearly repeats it. The field list was also illustrative, "such as authority, status and shape", which made dropping one look permitted.

## What's New at a Glance

- **On the operator's decision, the shape sentence carries its consequence.** Shape stays unresolved until the user states it or the notes arrive, so the turn-1 question asks about it even when the request's wording suggests one.
- **The field list is a closed minimum.** The question covers source set, authority, status, shape and scope, each unless the user has already stated it, plus any other unresolved field.
- **The Doc Mode pair carries the change.** Both system prompts and the consolidated-clarification paragraph on both sides of the Doc Mode pair carry the unconditional shape sentence and the closed field list. The pair moves to v0.109.
- **Step 4 and Section 12 are unchanged.** They already kept the ask in the same sentence.
- **Both scenarios name scope.** The overview, turn-1 chain and pass criteria of `SDK-001` and `PDK-001` now name scope beside authority, status and shape.

No migration required.

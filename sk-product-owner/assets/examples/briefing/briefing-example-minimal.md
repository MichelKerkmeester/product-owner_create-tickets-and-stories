# Briefing - Merchant - Store Setup - Welcome Checklist

## About
* * *
New merchants land on an empty home screen after signing up. Since August, about 2,300 merchants signed up and many never added a product or a payout account.

The home screen shows a short welcome checklist until the first steps are done.

## Briefing
* * *
*   The design starts from the empty home screen and adds one checklist card on top
*   The steps link to screens that already exist, so no new setup screens are needed


## Requirements
* * *
**Display**
- [] The card lists the first steps: add a product, add a payout account and share the store link
- [] Each step shows whether it is done
- [] Each step opens its existing setup screen

**Visibility**
- [] The card shows on the home screen of a new store
- [] The card hides once every step is done

# Wishlist contacts, 1 July to 20 September

Compiled by Maud, CS team lead, on 2026-09-21 from helpdesk contacts tagged wishlist, and sent to the Storefront team with a request to fix. Customer quotes are shortened, with names and order numbers removed.

## Numbers

`412` contacts were tagged wishlist between `2026-07-01` and `2026-09-20`. That makes it our third biggest tag after WISMO and returns, and it has grown every month.

| What the customer says happened | Contacts |
|---------------------------------|----------|
| Wishlist gone after a new phone or after reinstalling the app | 171 |
| Saved items in the app, cannot find them on web | 138 |
| Saved items on web, cannot find them in the app | 64 |
| Could not add any more items | 39 |
| Total | 412 |

By month: 118 in July, 139 in August and 155 in September up to the 20th.

Where the customer names an app version, the recent contacts come from iOS `4.8.0` and Android `4.8.2`, the current releases. Contacts from older versions describe the same thing.

The 39 who could not add more had reached the limit of `50 items`. Several of them had a full list on web and a second, shorter one in the app, and did not know they were two lists.

## What customers say

"I had over 40 things saved for our new kitchen, and after I switched phones it was all gone."

"I built my list in the app on the train, and when I sat down at the laptop to order, the wishlist was empty."

"Why does the website ask me to sign in to see my wishlist when I am signed in on the app?"

"Please bring back my wishlist, I did not delete anything."

## What we tell customers now

Agents explain that the wishlist in the app and the one on web are separate, and that the app one does not come back after a reinstall. Customers do not take it well: 23 of the 412 contacts also carry a complaint tag.

## Our ask

We see this as a bug. Customers lose saved items without doing anything wrong, and from where they sit the app and the website are one shop. Please fix it so the wishlist is the same everywhere.

## Reply from Storefront

Teun, iOS engineer, Storefront, replied on 2026-09-22:

"Thanks Maud, this is useful. For context, in the apps the wishlist has always been saved on the device. That is how it was built when the wishlist first shipped in the app, so it works for customers who never sign in. On web it has always been saved to the account, which is why web needs sign-in for it. Nothing about it changed in 4.8.0 or 4.8.2. Whether the apps should change is a product call, so I have added Lotte."

Maud replied the same day:

"Understood. From the customer's side it still looks broken, and it still reaches us every day."

Not decided yet: Lotte has not answered, and nobody has picked it up.

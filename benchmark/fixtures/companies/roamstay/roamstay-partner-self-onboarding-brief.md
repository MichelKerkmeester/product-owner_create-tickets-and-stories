# Partner self-onboarding in Partner Hub, strategy brief

Source: strategy brief for next year's Partner squad planning, shared in the Partner squad space
Written by Freya, Director of Partner Growth, on 2026-09-22

## Where we are

Every new property on Roamstay is set up by hand. A partner fills in a short sign-up form, then an Ops agent collects the rest by email: photos, room types, rates, policies, city tax, bank details and identity documents. The agent builds the listing in Back office and makes it live.

- Median time from sign-up to go-live is 11 business days
- 38% of partners who sign up never go live. Of 2,140 sign-ups from January to June 2026, 813 did not make it
- Each property takes an Ops agent about 4.5 hours of work, spread across the whole wait
- 640 properties are waiting in the queue today

Most of the drop-off happens while the partner waits for an agent to reply, not while they fill anything in. Partners say the same on exit calls: they signed up, heard nothing for days and listed their rooms somewhere else.

## What we want

Independent properties with up to 40 rooms set themselves up in Partner Hub and go live in a median of 3 business days, with no Ops agent doing the setup. Ops agents only check a finished listing before it goes live.

Target: 1,500 properties live through self-onboarding by 2027-06-30. For scale, Roamstay has 18,000 properties live today.

## The six stages

A partner moves through these in order and can save and come back at any point.

1. Sign-up and verification. Email and phone confirmed, business registration number checked against the country's business register
2. Property profile and photos. Address and map pin, description, facilities, and at least 8 photos, each up to 20 MB
3. Rooms and rates setup. Room types with their occupancy, a base price per night and at least one rate plan, free cancellation or non-refundable
4. Policies and city tax. Check-in and check-out times, the cancellation policy per rate plan, house rules and the city tax rule, per adult per night or per stay
5. Payout details and identity checks. Bank account and the owner's identity document, checked by the identity verification provider. No payout before both pass
6. Go-live review queue. An Ops agent checks the finished listing in Back office within 1 business day, then approves it or sends it back with a reason per stage

## Who it's for, and who it isn't

- In: independent hotels, guesthouses and apartment buildings with up to 40 rooms
- Out: properties with more than 40 rooms, and chains. They stay on the assisted path with a partner manager
- Out: any property that runs a channel manager. Connecting one is its own integration project, so these partners stay on assisted onboarding for now

## How we'll know it works

- Median time from sign-up to go-live, and time spent in each stage
- Drop-off per stage, against today's 38%
- Share of listings sent back from the Go-live review queue, and the reasons
- Guest complaints about wrong city tax or wrong photos on self-onboarded properties in their first 90 days live

## Risks I can see

- Fake listings. Identity checks and the Go-live review queue both sit before go-live, and payouts wait for both
- City tax set up wrong. It changes what a guest pays, so the Go-live review queue checks it on every listing
- Photo quality. Blurry or tiny photos cost bookings, so the photo step checks size and resolution on upload
- Queue capacity. The Go-live review queue has to hold its 1 business day as volume grows, which the Ops Tools squad will size with us

## Who else is involved

- Partner squad owns Partner Hub and partner-service, and would own this work
- Ops Tools squad builds the Go-live review queue in Back office
- Payments squad owns payout details and the handover to the payment provider for payouts
- Bram, Ops Lead, has agreed to pilot the review queue with his agents

## What I need from product

An epic for this, with the six stages as its building blocks, that the squads can size before planning closes. For the first release, a fast go-live matters more to me than covering every property type.

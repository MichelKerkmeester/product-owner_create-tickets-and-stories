# Late reminder reports after the March clock change

Compiled by Marta, Support Lead, from the support inbox on 2026-04-07.
Re-shared with the To-dos and Reminders team on 2026-09-16, because nothing has shipped for this yet and the next clock change is on 2026-10-25.

## Summary

Between 2026-03-29 and 2026-04-05 we tagged 117 tickets `reminder-late`. They fall into two groups that do not look alike.

| Group | Platform and version | Reminder | What members see | Tickets |
|-------|----------------------|----------|------------------|---------|
| A | Android 5.2.3 | One-off | Set for 09:00, arrives at 10:00 | 64 |
| B | iOS 5.2.4 | Daily | Set for 07:30, arrives at 08:30 every day | 53 |

We have no reports of late one-off reminders on iOS or late daily reminders on Android. Web and desktop users report nothing, since reminders there show in the app only.

All 117 come from members in time zones whose clocks moved forward on 2026-03-29.

Group B stays late every day until the member fixes it by editing the reminder and saving it. They do not have to change anything. Opening the reminder and tapping Save is enough, and it arrives at 07:30 again from the next day. Group A reminders fire once, so there is nothing to fix after the fact.

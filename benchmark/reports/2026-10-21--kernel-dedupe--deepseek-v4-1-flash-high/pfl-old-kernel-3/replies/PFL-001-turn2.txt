## Round two

**Question 1, where the board stops**

Reading: the spec ends at "the customer gets one email". It says nothing about what the email contains or where it sends the customer.

Recommended: the board ends on one email sent, drawn as an end shape. The mail itself and any landing page stay off this board.

Why: a step the spec does not describe would be invented, and the email is a design question in its own right.

**Question 2, what the customer sees once a guest alert is saved**

Reading: the spec says the guest alert is "saved" and stops there. A signed-in alert is saved "straight away", which implies the same moment.

Recommended: the `Notify me` button changes to its saved state on the same screen for both paths, so the flow gains no screen and the two paths meet there.

Why: the smallest change that tells the customer the alert worked, and the one the spec's "straight away" wording implies.

**Question 3, surface**

Reading: the spec names the product page and the app only to rule push out. The company context has customers buying on web, iOS and Android, with the same shopping flows.

Recommended: one board with no platform split, named for the screen rather than the platform.

Why: the screens and the one guest branch are the same on every surface, so a split would draw the same shape twice.

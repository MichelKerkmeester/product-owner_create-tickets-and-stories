## Questions

Q1: Which states end a booking?
No-show, Completed and Cancelled end a booking. Each is a grey "Final state" shape with an arrow to an end dot on its right. Sure (high). Found in the middle and lower right of the board and confirmed by the "Final state" and "End" entries in the key.

Q2: From which states can a guest cancel?
From Pending (the "guest cancels" line along the bottom left) and from Confirmed (the "guest cancels" line leaving the right edge of the Confirmed box). Because that second line leaves the whole Confirmed box it reads as covering both Upcoming and In stay. Fairly sure on Pending and Confirmed, less sure whether cancelling during In stay is really meant, since the line is drawn from the box edge and not from a named inner state.

Q3: What moves a booking from Upcoming to In stay?
"check in" moves it. The short labelled arrow between Upcoming and In stay inside the Confirmed box says so. Sure (high).

## 1 Title and the question this board answers

The title is "Booking status". It answers what statuses a Roamstay booking can be in and what event moves it from one to the next.

## 2 Main path or structure from start to end

A start dot leads to Pending (light blue, the first state). "card authorised" takes Pending into the Confirmed group. Inside Confirmed an inner start dot leads to Upcoming, then "check in" leads to In stay, then an arrow leads to an end dot inside the group. From In stay "check out [from In stay]" leads out of the group to Completed, which leads to an end dot. That is the happy path: Pending, Confirmed (Upcoming then In stay), Completed.

## 3 Every decision or branch and where each exit goes

There are no diamond decision shapes. The branches are state transitions.
Pending has two exits: "card authorised" to Confirmed and "guest cancels" to Cancelled.
Upcoming has two exits: "check in" to In stay and "missed check-in [from Upcoming]" to No-show (drawn from the bottom of the Confirmed box).
In stay has its exit "check out [from In stay]" to Completed (drawn from the left of the Confirmed box) plus an arrow to the inner end dot.
The Confirmed box as a whole has "guest cancels" to Cancelled (drawn from the right edge).
No-show, Completed and Cancelled each go to an end dot.

## 4 What the key says

Black dot means Start. Light blue rounded shape means First state. White rounded shape with a grey outline means State. Grey filled rounded shape with a thicker grey outline means Final state. Black dot with a grey ring means End. Thin solid arrow means Leads to. Grey shape with a thick pink outline means In focus.
Not every mark is in the key. The large light blue Confirmed group box (a state holding other states) has no key entry. The bracketed text on labels such as "[from Upcoming]" and "[from In stay]" is not explained. The key does not say that a start and end dot inside a group mean the start and end of that group only. The key also does not say why Cancelled is in focus.

## 5 Anything unreadable, hard to follow or ambiguous

The end dot after In stay inside the Confirmed box (top right) is confusing. It suggests the Confirmed phase can simply finish after In stay, yet the real exit to Completed is the "check out" line on the left of the box. I am not sure whether these are the same event or two different ones.
The "check out [from In stay]" line starts at the left edge of the Confirmed box, far from In stay on the right, so the eye has to trust the bracket text rather than the line. The same applies to "missed check-in [from Upcoming]" which leaves the bottom of the box between Upcoming and In stay.
The right "guest cancels" line leaves the whole Confirmed box, so it is ambiguous whether a guest can cancel while In stay.
The pink "In focus" outline on Cancelled has no stated reason on the board.
The footnote says a card that is never authorised is left out, so what happens to a Pending booking whose card fails is not shown.
All text was readable at both zoom levels.

Mostly yes for the main lifecycle and the three end states, but no for building cancellation rules, because I would need to ask whether cancelling during In stay is allowed and why Cancelled is in focus.

Q2: From which states can a guest cancel?
From Pending, and from Confirmed, which by the drawing covers both Upcoming and In stay. Sure about Pending (the "guest cancels" line from the bottom of Pending to Cancelled). Less sure about which inner states (about 65 percent): the second "guest cancels" line leaves the right edge of the Confirmed group, not a specific state, so it suggests a guest can cancel even during In stay, which I would want confirmed.

## 1 Title and the question this board answers

"Booking status". It answers which statuses a Roamstay booking can be in and what event moves it from one status to the next.

"check out [from In stay]" leaves Confirmed and leads to Completed, which leads to an end dot. That is the happy path: Pending, Upcoming, In stay, Completed.

## 1 Title and the question this board answers

The title is "Change booking dates". It answers how a Roamstay guest changes the dates of an existing booking in the app, starting on the booking page and ending at the updated booking, including what happens with availability and with the price difference.

## 2 Main path or structure from start to end

The guest starts on "Booking page" (blue start box), taps "Change dates" and lands on "Pick new dates". The flow reaches the diamond "Room free on those dates?". On Yes it jumps over to the diamond "How does the price compare?". From the price diamond one of three routes leads to "Updated booking": through "Get credit back", through "Pay difference" or directly. "Updated booking" is the end and is marked green with a pink outline.

## 3 Every decision or branch and where each exit goes

"Room free on those dates?" has two exits. "Yes" leaves from the top and runs right to "How does the price compare?". "No" leaves from the right to "See other rooms", whose only exit "Pick a room" also goes to "How does the price compare?".

"How does the price compare?" has three exits. "Lower" leaves from the top to "Get credit back", which then goes to "Updated booking". "Higher" leaves from the right to "Pay difference", which then goes to "Updated booking". "Same, nothing charged" leaves from the bottom and goes straight to "Updated booking".

## 4 What the key says and whether every mark is in it

Blue rounded box means where it starts. White rounded box with a grey outline means a screen or step. Yellow diamond means a decision. Green rounded box with a green outline means goal reached. A thin grey arrow means leads to. Green box with a thick pink outline means in focus.

Every mark on the board has a key entry, with two small gaps. The "Goal reached" style (green fill and green outline) never appears on its own because the only goal box "Updated booking" carries the pink "In focus" outline instead, so I read it as goal reached and in focus at once, but that is my inference. The text labels on connectors (Yes, No, Lower and so on) are not in the key, though they explain themselves. There is only one line style, so nothing is left unexplained there.

## 5 Anything unreadable, hard to follow or ambiguous

All text is readable at 100 percent and in the overview.

The "Yes" and "Pick a room" arrows both arrive at the left point of "How does the price compare?" with two arrowheads very close together. It is clear after a second look but slightly crowded.

"See other rooms" has only one exit. The board does not say what happens if no other room is free or the guest picks nothing, so it looks like a dead end that is not drawn. The footnote does say cancelling, payment failure, guest count changes and the host side are left out, which covers a failed payment at "Pay difference" but not the no-room case.

It is not stated why "Updated booking" is "in focus" or what that emphasis is meant to point out to the reader.

"Get credit back" does not say whether credit means a refund to the card or app credit.

## 6 Could I act on this board without asking anyone

Yes for the main flow and all three price outcomes, which are complete and labelled, but I would ask what happens when no other room is available and what kind of credit the guest gets back.

## 1 Title and the question this board answers

The title is "Start a return 2", with the subtitle "How a Fernhouse customer starts a return in the app, from the order list to a confirmed return". It answers: what steps and choices does a customer go through in the app to get a return confirmed.

## 2 What you see first, and what second

First the large bold title at the top left. Second the bright pink outline around the green "Return confirmed" box at the far right, which pulls the eye harder than anything else in the flow. After that the blue "Order list" start box on the left and the two yellow diamonds.

## 3 The main path from start to end

The board runs left to right across three tinted sections: "Find the order", "Request the return" and "Done".

- Order list (blue rounded box, the start)
- Order detail (white rounded box, a screen)
- Within 30 days? (yellow diamond, a decision)
- Pick a reason (white rounded box, on the Yes branch)
- Drop-off or pickup? (yellow diamond, a decision)
- Return confirmed (green rounded box with a pink outline, the goal and the item in focus)

## 4 Every decision and where each exit goes

- **Within 30 days?** Yes goes right to "Pick a reason". No goes down and right to "Request an exception", a dashed orange box that leaves the flow and has no outgoing arrow.
- **Drop-off or pickup?** Drop-off leaves from the top of the diamond, runs along a line above the section and drops down into "Return confirmed". Pickup leaves from the right and goes straight into "Return confirmed". Both choices end at the same box, so the board does not show any difference in what happens after each one.

## 5 What the key says

- Blue fill with blue outline: Where it starts
- White fill with grey outline: Screen or step
- Yellow diamond: Decision
- Green fill with green outline: Goal reached
- Orange fill with dashed orange outline: Leaves the flow
- Plain solid arrow: Leads to
- Green fill with thick pink outline: In focus

Almost every mark is in the key. Three things are not: the three tinted section backgrounds (peach, mint and pale yellow) with their headings, the Yes, No, Drop-off and Pickup labels on the lines (these explain themselves) and the footer text. The section tints are the only marks whose colour meaning I had to guess, and I read them as grouping only.

## 6 Anything unreadable, hard to follow or ambiguous

- The "2" in the title (top left) is unexplained. I cannot tell if it is a version number or the second of a set.
- "Request an exception" (middle bottom) is a dead end on the board. I do not know who reviews the exception, how long it takes or whether the customer can still reach "Return confirmed" from there.
- The Drop-off line (top right) leaves the diamond upward and crosses from the "Request the return" section into "Done" above the boxes. It is easy to follow but sits close to the section edge.
- Return confirmed carries both the "Goal reached" green and the "In focus" pink outline. It is clear, but nothing says why this box is the focus.
- The footer (bottom left) says free pickup over €100 and a proposal under review are left out, so the pickup branch may hide a cost rule I would need before acting.
- "Within 30 days?" does not say 30 days from what. I assumed delivery, but the board does not state it.

All text was readable at both zoom levels.

Mostly yes for the happy path, since the steps and both return methods are clear, but no for the over 30 days case and for pickup pricing, because the board stops at "Request an exception" and leaves the €100 pickup rule out by its own footer.

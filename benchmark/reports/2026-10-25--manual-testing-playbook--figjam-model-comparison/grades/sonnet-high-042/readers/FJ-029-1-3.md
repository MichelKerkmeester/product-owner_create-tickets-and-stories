Yes. After "Free or team plan?" exits on "Team" the "Add teammates' emails?" diamond has a "Skip" exit that runs under the row straight to "Name the workspace". Sure: high. Found on board 2, the line under the second diamond.

Board 1 "Sign up" comes first. Its title carries the number 1 and it ends on "Email verified" which is where board 2 "Setup" starts. Sure: high. Found in the numbered titles at the top left of each board and the matching end and start shapes.

## 1 Title and the question this board answers

The set is "1. Sign up" and "2. Setup". Together they answer how a new person gets from the landing page to a verified account and then to the editor with a welcome tip, by picking a plan, maybe adding teammates, naming a workspace and choosing a template or a blank start.

## 2 What you see first, and what second

First the big bold titles at the top left with their one line subtitle. Second the single horizontal row of shapes that starts with a blue rounded box on the left and ends with a pink outlined green box on the right. The yellow diamonds catch the eye next, then the key panel at the bottom.

## 3 The main path or structure from start to end, naming the shapes

Board 1: "Landing page" (blue start box) leads to "Sign up with email or Google" (white step) then "Enter email code" (white step) then the "Code correct?" diamond. On "Yes" it reaches "Email verified" (green goal with pink focus outline).

Board 2: "Email verified" (blue start box) leads to the "Free or team plan?" diamond. On the team path it goes to the "Add teammates' emails?" diamond then "Enter teammates' emails" (white step) then "Name the workspace" (white step). Next comes the "Template or blank?" diamond then "Choose a template" (white step) and finally "Editor with welcome tip" (green goal with pink focus outline).

Board 1 "Code correct?": Yes goes to "Email verified". No goes to "Error with resend" which then goes on "Resend" back to "Enter email code".

Board 2 "Free or team plan?": Free jumps over the top straight to "Name the workspace". Team goes to "Add teammates' emails?".

## 5 What the key says and whether every mark is in it

Both boards carry the same key. Blue rounded box means where it starts. White rounded box with grey outline means screen or step. Yellow diamond means decision. Green rounded box with green outline means goal reached. A thin grey arrow means leads to. A green box with a thick pink outline means in focus.

Almost every mark is covered. Two small gaps. The plain "Goal reached" style (green fill with a green outline) never appears on its own because both goal boxes carry the pink focus outline instead, so a reader has to infer that a pink outlined green box is a goal that is also in focus. The text labels on lines (Yes, No, Resend, Free, Team, Add emails, Skip, Template, Blank) are not in the key but they read as plain answer labels and need no explanation. The source line at the bottom of each board is a note and not a mark.

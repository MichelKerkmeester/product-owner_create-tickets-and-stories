## Quick answers

**Q1: Which states end a booking?**
Completed, No-show and Cancelled end a booking. All three are grey "Final state" shapes on the right side and each has an arrow to its own End dot at the far right. Sure: high. Found in the right column of the board and confirmed by the key ("Final state" and "End").

**Q2: From which states can a guest cancel?**
From Pending and from Confirmed. Two lines labelled "guest cancels" run to Cancelled: one leaves the right side of Pending, the other leaves the bottom edge of the Confirmed box. The pink callout in the key ("two statuses lead here") backs this up. Sure: medium. I am confident about Pending. For Confirmed I cannot tell whether a guest can cancel from both Upcoming and In stay or only one of them, because the line starts on the box edge (directly below In stay) and has no "[from ...]" note like the other two exits have.

**Q3: What moves a booking from Upcoming to In stay?**
The "check in" event. Sure: high. It is the labelled arrow between Upcoming and In stay inside the Confirmed box, top left of the diagram.

## 1 Title and the question this board answers

The title is "Booking status" with the subtitle "Every status a Roamstay booking passes through, and what moves it on". It answers: what statuses can a booking be in and which events move it from one to the next.

## 2 What you see first, and what second

First the bold title and then the large pale blue "Confirmed" box across the top with Upcoming and In stay inside it. Second the bright pink outlined Cancelled shape at the bottom right, which pulls the eye because it is the only saturated colour besides the blue Pending shape.

## 3 The main path or structure from start to end

A start dot (left, middle) leads to Pending (blue rounded rectangle, the first state). From Pending a line labelled "card authorised" runs up and left into the left edge of the Confirmed box. Inside Confirmed a second start dot leads to Upcoming (white rounded rectangle). Upcoming leads to In stay via "check in". In stay leads to an end dot inside the Confirmed box. From the top of the Confirmed box a line labelled "check out [from In stay]" goes to Completed (grey rounded rectangle), which leads to an End dot. The happy path reads: Start, Pending, card authorised, Upcoming, check in, In stay, check out, Completed, End.

## 4 Every decision or branch and where each exit goes

- Pending: "card authorised" goes into Confirmed (to Upcoming via the inner start dot). "guest cancels" goes to Cancelled.
- Upcoming: "check in" goes to In stay. "missed check-in [from Upcoming]" leaves the right edge of the Confirmed box and goes to No-show.
- In stay: an unlabelled arrow goes to the end dot inside Confirmed. "check out [from In stay]" leaves the top of the Confirmed box and goes to Completed.
- Confirmed as a whole: "guest cancels" leaves the bottom edge and goes to Cancelled.
- Completed, No-show and Cancelled: each has one arrow to its own End dot. Completed reaches its End dot by a line that drops below it and turns up, the other two go straight right.

There are no diamond decision shapes. Branching is shown by several labelled exits leaving the same state or box.

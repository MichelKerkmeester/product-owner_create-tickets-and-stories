## 1 Title and the question this board answers

The title is "Change booking dates". The subtitle says it shows how a Roamstay guest changes the dates of a booking in the app, for the product and design team. The question it answers is what steps and outcomes a guest goes through when moving their booking to new dates, and how the price difference is handled.

## 2 What you see first, and what second

First I see the large bold title at top left. Second my eye jumps to the pink outlined diamond "New price vs old?" on the right, because the thick pink outline is the loudest mark on the board. After that I read the flow from the blue "Booking page" on the left to the green "Updated booking" on the right.

## 3 The main path from start to end

The flow runs left to right in one row. It starts at "Booking page", a blue rounded rectangle. An arrow labelled "Tap Change dates" leads to "Pick new dates", a white rounded rectangle. An arrow leads to "Room free?", a yellow diamond. The "Yes" exit loops over the top to "New price vs old?", a yellow diamond with a pink outline. From there one of three exits leads to "Updated booking", a green rounded rectangle with a green outline, which is the end.

## 4 Every decision and where each exit goes

"Room free?" has two exits. "Yes" goes over the top straight to "New price vs old?". "No" goes right to "See other rooms", a white step, and from there a line labelled "Pick a room" joins "New price vs old?".

"New price vs old?" has three exits. "Lower" goes up to "Get the difference as credit" and then to "Updated booking". "Higher" goes right to "Pay the difference" and then to "Updated booking". "Same" runs along the bottom directly to "Updated booking".

## 5 What the key says

The key at the bottom lists six items. A blue rounded rectangle means "Where it starts". A white rounded rectangle with a grey outline means "Screen or step". A yellow diamond means "Decision". A green rounded rectangle with a green outline means "Goal reached". A plain arrow means "Leads to". A yellow diamond with a thick pink outline means "In focus".

Every shape and colour on the board is in the key. Only one line style is used and it is in the key. The small labels on the lines ("Tap Change dates", "Yes", "No", "Pick a room", "Lower", "Higher", "Same") are not in the key, but they read as plain labels and need no key entry. A footer under the key states the source was the product owner's description, not checked against the app, and that payment failure, host approval and cancellation rules are left out.

## 6 Anything unclear or ambiguous

The key says pink means "In focus" but the board never says why the price decision is in focus or what question about it the reader should look at. That sits on the "New price vs old?" diamond and the key entry.

"See other rooms" has only one exit. There is no path for a guest who finds no other room or gives up, so the dead end is unknown. That sits in the middle of the flow.

It is not clear whether "See other rooms" means other rooms at the same property on the new dates or other properties. Also not stated is whether the credit in "Get the difference as credit" is app credit or a refund, and where it can be spent. Both sit in the step labels on the right.

The "Pick a room" line enters the price diamond from the lower left at a slight angle next to where the "Yes" line enters, so the two arrival points are close together. It is still readable. Everything else on the board read cleanly.

## 7 Could I act on this board without asking anyone

Mostly yes for the happy path and the three price outcomes, but no for a full build, because the no room found case is missing, the meaning of credit is undefined and the footer says payment failure, host approval and cancellation rules are left out and the flow was never checked against the app.

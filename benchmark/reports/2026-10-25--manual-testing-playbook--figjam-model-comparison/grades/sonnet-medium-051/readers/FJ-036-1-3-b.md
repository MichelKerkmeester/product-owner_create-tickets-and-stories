## 1. Title and the question this board answers

The title is "Change booking dates". The subtitle reads "From the booking page to the updated booking, for the product and design team". The board answers what a guest goes through to change the dates of a booking and what they pay or get back.

## 2. Main path or structure from start to end

The flow runs left to right. "Booking page" (blue, start) leads on "Tap Change dates" to "Pick new dates", which leads to the decision "Room free?". On "Yes" the flow skips over the top to the decision "New price?". Its three exits lead to "Pay difference", "Get credit" or "Nothing charged", and all three lead to "Updated booking" (green, goal reached).

## 3. Every decision or branch and where each exit goes

- "Room free?" has two exits. "Yes" goes up and across the top straight into "New price?". "No" goes to "See other rooms", which leads on "Pick a room" into "New price?".
- "New price?" has three exits. "Higher" goes to "Pay difference". "Lower" goes to "Get credit". "Same" goes to "Nothing charged". Each of those three leads on to "Updated booking".

Nothing on the board says what happens if no other room is free, because "See other rooms" has only the one exit "Pick a room".

## 1. Title and the question this board answers

The title is "Stays model". The subtitle reads "The tables behind a stay and the keys that join them, split into the guest side and the partner side". The board answers which tables sit behind a stay, which columns join them, and which side of the business owns each table.

## 2. Main path or structure from start to end

This is an entity relationship diagram, so it has no start or end. "Booking" is the centre. On the guest side, "Guest" makes bookings, and a booking holds payments. On the partner side, "Property" offers "Room"s and publishes "Rate Plan"s. "Room" and "Rate Plan" both connect up to "Booking" across the zone border: a room "is reserved by" bookings and a rate plan "prices" bookings.

## 3. Every decision or branch and where each exit goes

There are no decisions. The six relationships are:
- "Guest" makes "Booking" (1 to 0..n).
- "Booking" holds "Payment" (1 to 1..n).
- "Room" is reserved by "Booking" (1 to 0..n).
- "Rate Plan" prices "Booking" (1 to 0..n).
- "Property" offers "Room" (1 to 1..n).
- "Property" publishes "Rate Plan" (1 to 1..n).

## 4. What the key says, and whether every mark is in the key

- Plain white box: entity. Used on all six tables.
- Plain line: identifying. Used on all six lines.
- Pink thick outline: in focus. Used on "Booking".

Not in the key: the zone boxes "Guest side" and "Partner side" (they carry their own names), the PK and FK rows inside each entity, the end marks on the lines (bars and crow's feet that show one or many) and the "(1 to 0..n)" style cardinality text in labels. The cardinality text is readable, but the key does not teach the end marks. The key says every line is identifying, yet the lines join a PK to an FK column, which I cannot confirm from the board alone.

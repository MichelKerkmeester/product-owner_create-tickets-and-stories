## 1. Title and the question this board answers

The title is "Roamstay damage claim". The subtitle says: from the hotel's damage report after check-out to a closed claim, for support and product staff. The board answers who does what in a damage claim and which status the claim shows at each point.

## 2. The main path or structure from start to end

The board has four lanes. The top lane, Claim status, is a row of four status shapes: Reported, With the guest, Under review, Closed. Solid arrows join Reported to With the guest, then With the guest over the top to Under review, then Under review to Closed. The three lower lanes show the people.

The flow starts at "Guest checks out" (blue, Hotel lane) and goes to "Report damage" (after check-out, adds photos and a repair quote). It drops into the Roamstay support lane to "Send claim to guest" (sends photos and quote, guest has 7 days to reply) and then down into the Guest lane to the decision "Accept within 7 days?". Dotted lines tie steps to statuses: Report damage to Reported, Send claim to guest to With the guest, Review dispute to Under review and Claim closed to Closed.

## 3. Every decision or branch and where each exit goes

- "Accept within 7 days?" (Guest lane) has two exits. "Accept" runs up to "Card charged", an orange dashed pill that leaves the flow. "Dispute" runs up to "Review dispute".
- "Uphold within 5 working days?" (support lane) has two exits. "Uphold" goes to "Charge the card" and then "Claim closed". "Drop" goes to "Drop the claim" and then "Claim closed".

"Claim closed" is a goal and is tied to the Closed status by a dotted line. The "Accept" branch ends at "Card charged" and does not connect to "Claim closed".

- "Accept" leads to "Card charged" and stops. The claim is then never shown reaching "Claim closed" or "Closed" on the accept route, so I cannot tell whether an accepted claim ever closes or what status it shows.
- "Card charged" (leaves the flow) and "Charge the card" (a step on the uphold route) are two near-identical names for the card charge, which is easy to confuse.
- "Accept within 7 days?" has only Accept and Dispute exits. What happens if the guest does not answer within 7 days is not shown.
- The long dotted lines to the status row cross the Hotel and support lanes and cross each other near the middle, so they take care to follow. The "With the guest" line runs up through the Hotel lane near the left.
- The solid line from With the guest to Under review loops over the top and has no label, so what triggers the move to Under review is shown only by the dotted line from "Review dispute".
- The footnote leaves out the under-25-euro waiver, as not live.

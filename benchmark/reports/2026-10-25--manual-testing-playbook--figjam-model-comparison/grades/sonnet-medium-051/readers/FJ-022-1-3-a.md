## 1. Title and the question this board answers

The title is "Start a return". The subtitle says: how a Fernhouse customer starts a return in the app, from the order list to a confirmation with a QR code. The board answers what steps and choices a customer goes through to start a return, and who gets turned away.

## 2. The main path or structure from start to end

The path runs left to right in one row. It starts at "Order list" (blue), goes to "Order detail", then to the decision "Within 30 days of delivery?". On Yes it goes to "Start a return", then "Return reasons", then the decision "Drop-off or courier pickup?". Both exits of that decision reach "Confirmation with QR code" (green), which is the goal.

## 3. Every decision or branch and where each exit goes

- "Within 30 days of delivery?" has two exits. Yes goes on to "Start a return". No goes down to "Contact support", which leaves the flow.
- "Drop-off or courier pickup?" has two exits. The one labelled "Courier pickup" goes straight right to "Confirmation with QR code". The one labelled "Drop-off point" runs up and over the top and comes down into "Confirmation with QR code" from above.

Nothing after Contact support is shown. The board does not say what support does.

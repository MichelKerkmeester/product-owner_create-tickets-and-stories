## 1. Title and the question this board answers

The title is "Roamstay damage claim". The subtitle reads "From the hotel's damage report after check-out to a closed claim, for support and product staff". The board answers who does what on a damage claim, how the claim status changes and when the guest and support have to act.

## 2. Main path or structure from start to end

There are four lanes: "Claim status" at the top, then "Hotel", "Roamstay support" and "Guest". The flow starts at "Guest checks out" (blue) in the Hotel lane and leads to "Report damage" (adds photos and a repair quote), then down to "Send claim to guest" in the Roamstay support lane (sends the photos and quote, guest has 7 days to reply). That leads down to the decision "Accept within 7 days?" in the Guest lane. On "Dispute", the flow goes up to "Review dispute" and then to "Uphold within 5 working days?". On "Uphold" it goes to "Charge the card" and then to "Claim closed" (green). On "Drop" it goes to "Drop the claim" and then to "Claim closed". The solid line in the strip runs "Reported" to "With the guest" to "Under review" to "Closed".

## 1. Title and the question this board answers

The title is "Loomlist block sync services". The subtitle reads "From a block change on any client to a woken iOS device, for Loomlist engineers". The board answers how a block change travels through the services until a sleeping iOS device is woken.

## 2. Main path or structure from start to end

Six zones from left to right. In "Clients", "Desktop client", "Web client" and "iOS client" each hold a "Live socket" (two-way) to "API gateway" in "Gateway". "API gateway" sends "Routes block changes" to "sync-service" in "Services". "sync-service" does two things. It sends "Writes blocks" to "Blocks database" in "Stores" (solid line over the top of the board) and, dashed, "Produces block changes" to "Change stream" in "Events". "Push worker" in "Services" "Consumes" from "Change stream" (dashed), then calls "Apple's push service" in "Outside" (dashed, labelled "Push service: Wakes sleeping iOS devices").

## 3. Every decision or branch and where each exit goes

There are no decision shapes. The only branching is at "sync-service", which has two outgoing lines. One goes to "Blocks database" (solid, "Writes blocks") and one goes to "Change stream" (dashed, "Produces block changes"). They are parallel, not alternatives. "API gateway" fans out to the three clients and takes one line back from "sync-service" side only through "Routes block changes", which is a one-way arrow to "sync-service".

## 4. What the key says, and whether every mark is in the key

- Cyan rounded shape: app or client. On the three clients.
- Blue rounded shape: edge or gateway. On "API gateway".
- White rounded shape: service. On "sync-service" and "Push worker".
- Orange cylinder on its side: queue or stream. On "Change stream".
- Purple upright cylinder: data store. On "Blocks database".
- Grey dashed rounded shape: outside system. On "Apple's push service".
- Solid arrow: leads to.
- Dashed arrow: async or outside call.
- Arrow with heads at both ends: both ways. On the three "Live socket" lines.
- Pink thick outline: in focus. On "Change stream".

Every shape fill and line style is in the key. The zone boxes (Clients, Gateway, Services, Events, Stores, Outside) have their own labels and no key entry, and their tint colours carry no stated meaning. Edge labels are not in the key and read as plain labels.

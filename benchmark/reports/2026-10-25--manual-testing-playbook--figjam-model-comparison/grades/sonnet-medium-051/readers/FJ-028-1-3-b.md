## 1. Title and the question this board answers

The title is "Loomlist offline mode launch plan". The subtitle reads "From conflict screen design on 2 November 2026 to the release on 21 December 2026, for product and engineering leads". The board answers what has to happen, in which order and by when, for the offline mode launch.

## 2. Main path or structure from start to end

It is a timeline with a weekly date row and three lanes. In the Design lane, "Conflict screen design" runs 2 Nov to 16 Nov (2 weeks). In the Build lane, "Sync engine" runs 2 Nov to 23 Nov (3 weeks), "Conflict screen build" runs 16 Nov to 30 Nov (2 weeks) and "Desktop and iOS integration" runs 30 Nov to 7 Dec (1 week, critical). In the Launch lane, "Beta to 5 percent of workspaces" runs 7 Dec to 21 Dec (2 weeks), and "Release" is a milestone on 21 Dec.

The arrows give the order. "Conflict screen design" leads to "Conflict screen build". "Sync engine" and "Conflict screen build" both lead to "Desktop and iOS integration". Integration leads to the beta, and the beta leads to "Release".

## 3. Every decision or branch and where each exit goes

There are no decisions. The only join is at "Desktop and iOS integration", which waits for two bars, "Sync engine" and "Conflict screen build". "Conflict screen design" and "Sync engine" both start on 2 Nov and run in parallel.

## 1. Title and the question this board answers

Two linked diagrams. Diagram 1 is "Checkout payment, happy path": the calls a Roamstay checkout makes, from the booking request to the 201, for the payments team. Diagram 2 is "Checkout payment, declined card": the same calls when the card is declined, from the booking request to the 402. Together they answer what calls happen at checkout and how the outcome differs when the card is declined.

## 2. The main path or structure from start to end

These are sequence diagrams. Participants stand in columns with dashed vertical lines and time runs downward.

Diagram 1, four participants (GuestApp, BookingApi, PaymentProvider, PartnerHub):
1. GuestApp sends "Post the booking" to BookingApi.
2. BookingApi calls itself: "Hold the room".
3. BookingApi sends "Authorise card" to PaymentProvider (pink, in focus).
4. PaymentProvider replies "Authorised" (dashed).
5. BookingApi calls itself: "Confirm the hold".
6. BookingApi sends "Tell the partner hub" to PartnerHub (blue, async).
7. BookingApi replies "201" to GuestApp (dashed).

Diagram 2, three participants (no PartnerHub):
1. "Post the booking", 2. "Hold the room", 3. "Authorise card" (pink), 4. "Declined" reply, 5. "Release the hold" (self call), 6. "402" reply to GuestApp.

## 3. Every decision or branch and where each exit goes

There are no diamonds or branches inside a diagram. The branch is between the two diagrams and comes from the PaymentProvider reply at step 4. "Authorised" leads to diagram 1: confirm the hold, tell the partner hub, return 201. "Declined" leads to diagram 2: release the hold, return 402, and the partner hub is not told. The footnotes point each diagram at the other.

## 4. What the key says and whether every mark is in the key

- Rounded box: participant.
- Solid grey arrow: request.
- Dashed grey arrow: reply.
- Thin blue arrow: async message (only in the key of diagram 1, which is the only one that uses it).
- Purple numbered circle: step number.
- Thick pink arrow: in focus. Used on "Authorise card" in both diagrams.

The self calls (Hold the room, Confirm the hold, Release the hold) use the solid request style bending back to the same line, which fits "Request". The dashed vertical lines under each participant are not in the key but are the usual lifelines. The two key boxes differ because diagram 2 has no async message. Every other mark is in the key.

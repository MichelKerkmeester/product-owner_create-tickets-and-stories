## 1. Title and the question this board answers

The title is "Change booking dates". The subtitle says: from the booking page to the updated booking, for the product and design team. The board answers what a guest goes through to change the dates of a booking and what happens to the money.

## 2. The main path or structure from start to end

The flow runs left to right. It starts at "Booking page" (blue). The label "Tap Change dates" leads to "Pick new dates", then "Room free?". On Yes the line goes over the top to "New price?". From New price? three branches run to "Pay difference", "Get credit" and "Nothing charged", and all three lead to "Updated booking" (green), the goal.

## 3. Every decision or branch and where each exit goes

- "Room free?" has two exits. Yes goes along the top straight to "New price?". No goes to "See other rooms", and then "Pick a room" goes into "New price?" as well.
- "New price?" has three exits. "Higher" goes to "Pay difference". "Lower" goes to "Get credit". "Same" goes to "Nothing charged". All three then lead to "Updated booking".

Nothing leaves the flow. If the guest does not pick a room after "See other rooms" there is no exit shown.

- Blue rounded pill: where it starts. Used on "Booking page".
- White rounded pill: screen or step. Used on Pick new dates, See other rooms, Pay difference, Get credit and Nothing charged.
- Yellow diamond: decision. Used on "Room free?" and "New price?".
- Green rounded pill: goal reached. Used on "Updated booking".

Every colour, shape and outline on the board is in the key. The edge labels (Tap Change dates, Yes, No, Pick a room, Higher, Lower, Same) are plain text and not in the key.

## 5. Anything I could not read, could not follow or found ambiguous

- The "Pick a room" line leaves the bottom of "See other rooms" and joins the "Yes" line before entering "New price?" on its left side. The two lines meet near the diamond, so it takes a second look to see that both end at the same diamond.
- "Nothing charged" and "Get credit" are shown as steps, but whether they are screens the guest sees or things that happen in the background is not stated.
- The price compared in "New price?" is not named. I take it as the new total against the old total.
- The footnote leaves out failed payment, change-window rules, host approval and cancellation, so a guest who cannot pay the difference has no path here.
- "Get credit" does not say whether the credit is a refund or app credit.

## 6. Could I act on this board without asking anyone

Yes, for the happy flow and the three price outcomes, because every step and exit is labelled and the footnote names what is left out, though I would ask what happens when payment of the difference fails.

## 1. Title and the question this board answers

The title is "Loomlist block sync services". The subtitle says: from a block change on any client to a woken iOS device, for Loomlist engineers. The board answers how a block change travels through the services and ends up waking an iOS device.

## 2. The main path or structure from start to end

Left to right. The three clients in the Clients zone connect over "Live socket" lines to the "API gateway" in the Gateway zone. The gateway sends "Routes block changes" to "sync-service" in the Services zone. From sync-service two things happen. It sends "Writes blocks" over the top to "Blocks database" in Stores. It sends "Produces block changes" (dashed) to "Change stream" in Events. The Change stream is then read by "Push worker" ("Consumes", dashed), which calls "Apple's push service" in Outside (dashed) to wake sleeping iOS devices.

## 3. Every decision or branch and where each exit goes

There are no decision diamonds. The only branch is at sync-service, which has two outgoing lines: a solid one to Blocks database and a dashed one to Change stream. The gateway fans in from three clients. The Change stream has one reader shown, the Push worker.

## 4. What the key says and whether every mark is in the key

- Teal rounded pill: app or client. Used on the three clients.
- Blue pill with a bold blue outline: edge or gateway. Used on "API gateway".
- White rounded pill: service. Used on sync-service and Push worker.
- Orange cylinder lying on its side: queue or stream. Used on "Change stream".
- Purple upright cylinder: data store. Used on "Blocks database".
- Grey pill with a dashed outline: outside system. Used on "Apple's push service".
- Solid arrow: leads to.
- Dashed arrow: async or outside call.
- Arrow with heads on both ends: both ways. Used on the three Live socket lines.
- Thick magenta outline on the stream: in focus.

Every node and line style is in the key. The zone bands are labelled in their own corners but not in the key. The edge labels are plain text.

## 5. Anything I could not read, could not follow or found ambiguous

- The "Writes blocks" line runs a long way over the top of the board and down into Blocks database, so it is easy to lose. It starts at the top of sync-service.
- The Desktop client line bends at the top and joins the gateway from above, while the iOS client line joins from below. They read as both ways but the bends take a second look.
- The label on the Push worker line is long ("Push service: Wakes sleeping iOS devices") and runs across most of the lower board. It is readable.
- Nothing shows how a client learns of a change while it is awake and live. The sockets are both ways so I assume it is through them, but the board does not say.
- Only the Push worker is shown reading the Change stream. Whether other consumers exist is not stated, and the footnote leaves out file storage and the caller.

Q2: From which states can a guest cancel? From Pending (arrow "guest cancels") and from Upcoming (arrow "guest cancels [from Upcoming]"). Not from In stay. Sure, found at the bottom of the board. The footnote also says cancellation after check-in is left out.

## 1. Title and the question this board answers

The title is "Booking status". The subtitle says: every status a Roamstay booking passes through, from the pending request to a completed, cancelled or missed stay, and what moves it on. The board answers which statuses a booking can be in and what triggers each move.

## 2. The main path or structure from start to end

A start dot leads into "Pending" (blue, first state). On "card authorised" a long line along the top goes into the "Confirmed" box. Inside the box a start dot leads to "Upcoming", then "check in" leads to "In stay", then an end dot. Out of the Confirmed box three lines leave to the final states: "check out [from In stay]" to Completed, "missed check-in [from Upcoming]" to No-show, and "guest cancels [from Upcoming]" to Cancelled. From Pending a separate line "guest cancels" goes to Cancelled. Each final state then leads to an end dot.

So the happy path is Pending, Confirmed (Upcoming, In stay), Completed.

## 3. Every decision or branch and where each exit goes

There are no diamonds. The branching is on the arrows.

- Pending has two exits: "card authorised" into Confirmed and "guest cancels" to Cancelled.
- Upcoming has three ways on: "check in" to In stay, "missed check-in" to No-show and "guest cancels" to Cancelled.
- In stay has one way on: "check out" to Completed.
- No-show, Completed and Cancelled have one exit each, to an end dot.

## 4. What the key says and whether every mark is in the key

- Black dot: start.
- Blue rounded pill: first state. Used on Pending.
- White rounded pill: state. Used on Upcoming and In stay.
- Grey rounded pill with a heavier outline: final state. Used on No-show, Completed and Cancelled.
- Black dot with a grey ring: end. Used after each final state and after In stay inside the box.
- Plain arrow: leads to.
- Thick magenta outline: in focus. Used on Cancelled.

The pale blue Confirmed box is not in the key, but it is labelled in its own corner. Edge labels with square brackets such as [from Upcoming] are plain text and not in the key. Every state shape is in the key.

## 5. Anything I could not read, could not follow or found ambiguous

- The three lines to the final states start at the edge of the Confirmed box, not at a state. The square bracket in each label ("from In stay", "from Upcoming") says which state they really leave from. This is readable but I had to rely on the label rather than the line.
- The end dot inside the Confirmed box, to the right of In stay, has no label. It reads as In stay ending, yet the real exit from In stay is the "check out" line, so the meaning of that dot is unclear.
- The "guest cancels [from Upcoming]" line runs down the whole right side and enters Cancelled from above, and the "check out" line runs down the left side. They are long but can be followed.
- The "card authorised" arrow lands on the top edge of the Confirmed box, not directly on Upcoming. I take it as entering at the inner start dot.
- Why Cancelled is in focus is not stated.
- The footnote leaves out payment failure, expiry, refunds, booking edits and cancellation after check-in, so a Pending booking whose card fails has no exit on this board.

## 6. Could I act on this board without asking anyone

Yes, for the statuses and what moves each one, because every state and transition is labelled and the footnote names what is left out, though I would ask what happens to a Pending booking when the payment fails.

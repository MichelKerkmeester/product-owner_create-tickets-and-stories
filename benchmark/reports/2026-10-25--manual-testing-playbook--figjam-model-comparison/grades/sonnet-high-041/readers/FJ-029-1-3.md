# Fresh reader notes for FJ-029-1-3

## 1 Title and the question this board answers

The title is "1. Sign up". The subtitle states the question: how does a new person sign up with email or Google and verify the email code, from the landing page to a verified email.

## 2 What you see first, and what second

First I see the large bold title in the top left and the pink outlined diamond "Code correct?" in the middle right, which is the strongest colour on the board. Second I see the left to right row of shapes starting at the blue "Landing page" and ending at the green "Email verified". The key panel along the bottom comes after that.

## 3 The main path or structure from start to end

The flow runs left to right on one row. It starts at "Landing page", a blue rounded rectangle. An arrow leads to the yellow diamond "Sign up with?". Both of its exits meet at a small dark grey dot. From the dot an arrow leads to "Enter email code", a white rounded rectangle with a grey outline. An arrow leads on to the yellow diamond "Code correct?", which carries a thick pink outline. Its Yes exit goes to "Email verified", a green rounded rectangle with a green outline, which is the end of the flow.

## 4 Every decision or branch and where each exit goes

"Sign up with?" has two exits. "Email" leaves from the top of the diamond, runs along a line above it and drops down into the grey join dot. "Google" leaves from the right point straight into the same dot. After the dot both paths continue to "Enter email code".

"Code correct?" has two exits. "Yes" leaves from the right point and goes to "Email verified". "No" leaves from the bottom point, runs right and drops into "Error with resend option", a white rounded rectangle under the goal. From that error box a line labelled "Resend" runs left along the bottom and turns up with an arrowhead into the bottom of "Enter email code". So a wrong code loops back to entering the code.

## 5 What the key says

The key lists seven entries. A blue rounded rectangle means where it starts. A white rounded rectangle with a grey outline means screen or step. A yellow diamond means decision. A dark grey dot means paths join. A green rounded rectangle with a green outline means goal reached. A thin grey line with an open arrowhead means leads to. A yellow diamond with a thick pink outline means in focus.

Every shape on the board matches a key entry. The error box uses the screen or step style and has no style of its own. The text labels on the lines (Email, Google, Yes, No and Resend) are not in the key but read as plain exit labels. All connectors are solid lines, so there is no dashed or dotted style to explain. A footer line under the key names the source and says nothing was left out.

## 6 Anything I could not read, follow or found ambiguous

All text is legible at both zoom levels. Three things left me unsure.

The Google exit joins the Email exit before "Enter email code", so the board says a person who signs up with Google must also enter an email code. That is unusual for a Google sign up and I could not tell whether it is intended or a drawing shortcut. It sits at the grey dot between the first diamond and "Enter email code".

The pink "in focus" outline on "Code correct?" is explained only as "In focus". The board does not say why this step is the focus, for example whether it is the step under discussion or a known problem.

The Resend loop has no limit and no exit. The board does not say what happens after repeated wrong codes, whether the code expires or whether a person can go back and change the email. This sits at "Error with resend option" and the Resend line back to "Enter email code".

## 7 Could a product manager act on this board without asking anyone

Mostly yes for the happy path and the wrong code loop, but no for building it as drawn, because a product manager would need to ask whether Google sign up really requires the email code step and why "Code correct?" is marked in focus.

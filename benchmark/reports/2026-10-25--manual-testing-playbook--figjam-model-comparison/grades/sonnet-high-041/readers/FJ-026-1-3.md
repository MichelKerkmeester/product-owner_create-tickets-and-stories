# FJ-026-1-3 fresh reader

## 1 Title and the question this board answers

The title is "Roamstay booking status". The subtitle says it shows every status a Roamstay booking can hold, what moves it on and which statuses end it, written for support. The question is: what status can a booking be in, what event moves it to the next one and where does it stop? It is a state diagram.

## 2 What you see first, and what second

First the large bold title and then the wide pale blue "Confirmed" container across the top with Upcoming and In stay inside it. Second the bright pink outlined "Cancelled" state at the bottom right, which pulls the eye because it is the only saturated outline on the board. The light blue "Pending" state on the left comes after that.

## 3 The main path or structure from start to end

A black start dot leads to Pending (light blue rounded rectangle, the first state). From Pending a line labelled "card authorised" goes up and enters the left edge of the Confirmed container. Inside Confirmed a second start dot leads to Upcoming (white rounded rectangle), then "check-in" leads to In stay (white rounded rectangle), then an unlabelled arrow leads to an end dot inside the container. From the container a line labelled "check-out [from In stay]" runs over the top and down into Completed (grey rounded rectangle, a final state). Completed then leads to an end dot on the far right. The happy path is therefore Pending, Upcoming, In stay, Completed.

## 4 Every branch and where each exit goes

Pending has two exits: "card authorised" into Confirmed (landing at Upcoming via the inner start dot) and "guest cancels" along the bottom into Cancelled.
Upcoming has two exits: "check-in" to In stay and "missed check-in [from Upcoming]", drawn from the right edge of the Confirmed container down into No-show (grey final state).
In stay exits by "check-out [from In stay]" to Completed and by an unlabelled arrow to the inner end dot.
The Confirmed container as a whole has a "guest cancels" line leaving its bottom edge (directly below In stay) that drops down and enters Cancelled from above.
Completed, No-show and Cancelled each lead to their own end dot (black dot with a grey ring). Nothing leaves a final state for anything other than its end dot.

## 5 What the key says

Black dot means Start. Light blue fill with a blue outline means First state. White fill with a grey outline means State. Grey fill with a thicker grey outline means Final state. A black dot with a grey ring means End. A thin solid arrow means Leads to. A grey fill with a thick pink outline means "In focus: two statuses lead here".
Most marks are covered. Not in the key: the large pale blue Confirmed container (a grouping or parent state) and its title, the bracketed "[from ...]" qualifiers on two labels and the source note at the bottom. The key does not say that a line drawn from a container edge applies to any status inside it.

## 6 What I could not read, follow or found ambiguous

The inner end dot after In stay (top right of the Confirmed container) is unclear. In stay already leaves by check-out to Completed, so I cannot tell what this unlabelled exit means or whether a booking can end there without being Completed.
The "guest cancels" line from Confirmed leaves the container bottom under In stay. I cannot tell whether a guest can cancel only from Upcoming, also during In stay, or from either. The label has no "[from ...]" qualifier unlike the two other container exits.
The "missed check-in [from Upcoming]" line starts at the container's right edge level with In stay rather than at Upcoming, so the qualifier is the only thing telling me its source.
The start dot and end dot look very alike at small size. Only the grey ring separates them.
The key says the pink outline marks a status that two statuses lead into. Cancelled is reached from Pending and from Confirmed (a group, not a single status), so "two statuses" is only roughly right.
Pending has no exit for a failed or declined card and no timeout, so I cannot tell what happens to a Pending booking that is never authorised.
No timing is given for "missed check-in", for example how long after the check-in date it fires.
The Completed exit line takes an odd route, leaving the bottom of Completed and turning up into the end dot, but it is readable.

## 7 Could a product manager act on this board without asking anyone

No, because the core lifecycle is clear but a PM would still need to ask whether cancelling during a stay is allowed, what the unlabelled end inside Confirmed means and what happens when a card is never authorised.

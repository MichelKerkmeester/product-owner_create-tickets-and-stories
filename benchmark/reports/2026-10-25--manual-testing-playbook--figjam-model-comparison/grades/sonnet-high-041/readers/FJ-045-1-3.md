# Reader notes: FJ-045-1-3

## 1 Title and the question this board answers

The title is "Start a return". The subtitle says the board shows how a customer starts a return in the app, from the order list to the QR confirmation. It is meant for Post-purchase and CS to check against the returns doc. The question it answers is: what steps and checks does a customer pass through in the app to start a return, and where can they drop out?

## 2 What you see first, and what second

First I see the large bold title at top left. Then my eye goes to the big diamond with the thick pink outline in the middle of the flow, "Within 30 days of delivery?", because it is the heaviest and brightest mark on the board. Second I read the row left to right starting at the blue "Order list" box, and I notice the green "Confirmation with QR code" box at the far right as the end point.

## 3 The main path from start to end

The flow runs in one horizontal row from left to right.

- "Order list" is a blue rounded rectangle. It is the start.
- A solid arrow labelled "Opens an order" leads to "Order detail", a white rounded rectangle.
- An unlabelled arrow leads to the decision diamond "Within 30 days of delivery?", which is yellow with a thick pink outline.
- The arrow labelled "Yes: Start a return" leads to "Return reasons", a white rounded rectangle.
- An unlabelled arrow leads to a second yellow diamond, "Drop-off or courier?".
- From there both exits end at "Confirmation with QR code", a green rounded rectangle with a green outline. This is the goal.

## 4 Every decision and where each exit goes

There are two decisions.

- "Within 30 days of delivery?" has two exits. "Yes: Start a return" goes right to "Return reasons". "No" leaves from the bottom of the diamond, bends right and ends at "Contact support", an orange rounded rectangle with a dashed orange outline. That box has no outgoing arrow, so the customer leaves the flow there.
- "Drop-off or courier?" has two exits. "Courier pickup" goes straight right to "Confirmation with QR code". "Drop-off point" leaves from the top of the diamond, runs right above the row and comes down into the same "Confirmation with QR code" box. Both choices reach the same end.

## 5 What the key says

The key sits in a grey panel under the flow. It lists:

- Blue rounded rectangle: Where it starts
- White rounded rectangle with grey outline: Screen or step
- Yellow diamond: Decision
- Green rounded rectangle with green outline: Goal reached
- Orange rounded rectangle with dashed orange outline: Leaves the flow
- Solid grey arrow: Leads to
- Yellow diamond with thick pink outline: In focus

Every mark on the board is in the key. All connectors are the same solid grey arrow, so there is only one line style to explain and the key covers it. The arrow labels ("Opens an order", "Yes: Start a return", "No", "Drop-off point", "Courier pickup") are plain text and need no key entry. A small footnote at the bottom names the sources (the app, the returns doc and a context doc for the 30-day window) and lists what is left out: everything after the confirmation, the screens inside each method, item selection, orders not yet delivered and guest customers.

## 6 Anything unreadable, hard to follow or ambiguous

- Nothing was unreadable. The arrow labels and the footnote are small but legible at 100%.
- The key explains what "In focus" looks like but not why the 30-day check is in focus. I can guess it is the check the reviewers should verify against the returns doc, but the board does not say so.
- Both return methods end at "Confirmation with QR code". It is not clear whether a courier pickup really produces a QR code too, or whether the two methods get different confirmations. Because the footnote says the screens inside each method are left out, I cannot tell.
- The two unlabelled arrows (out of "Order detail" and out of "Return reasons") are fine to follow, but it is not shown what the customer does on "Order detail" to reach the 30-day check, for example tapping a return button.
- "Contact support" is a dead end on the board. The footnote does not say what happens next, so the outcome for a late return is open.

## 7 Could a product manager act on this board without asking anyone?

Yes, mostly, because the path, both decisions and every exit are labelled and fully covered by the key, though they would still need to confirm whether courier pickup really ends in a QR code and why the 30-day check is marked in focus.

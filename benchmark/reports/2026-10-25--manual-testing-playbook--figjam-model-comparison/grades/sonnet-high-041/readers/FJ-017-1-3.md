# FJ-017-1-3 fresh reader notes

## 1 Title and the question this board answers

The title is "Booking status". The subtitle says it shows every status a Roamstay booking passes through and what moves it on, so that support can follow a booking to its end. The question it answers is: what state can a booking be in, and what event moves it from one state to the next until it finishes?

## 2 What you see first, and what second

First I see the large bold title and then the big pale blue container labelled "Confirmed" across the top, with Upcoming and In stay inside it. Second my eye goes to the Cancelled state at the lower right, because it is the only shape with a thick pink outline. After that I notice the pale blue Pending state on the left and the grey final states stacked on the right.

## 3 The main path or structure from start to end

This is a state diagram. A black start dot on the left leads to Pending, a light blue rounded rectangle (the first state). From Pending an arrow labelled "card authorised" goes up and enters the Confirmed container on its left edge. Inside Confirmed a second black start dot leads to Upcoming (white rounded rectangle), then "check in" leads to In stay (white rounded rectangle), then an unlabelled arrow leads to an end dot inside the container. From the top of the Confirmed container a line labelled "check out [from In stay]" runs round to Completed, a grey rounded rectangle, which then leads to an end dot on the right. So the happy path reads Pending, Confirmed (Upcoming then In stay), Completed, end.

## 4 Every decision or branch and where each exit goes

- Pending has two exits: "card authorised" goes to Confirmed, and "guest cancels" goes along the bottom to Cancelled.
- Inside Confirmed, Upcoming has one exit, "check in", to In stay. In stay has one unlabelled exit to the inner end dot.
- Confirmed as a whole has three exits. "check out [from In stay]" leaves the top edge and arrives at Completed. "missed check-in [from Upcoming]" leaves the right edge and arrives at No-show. "guest cancels" leaves the bottom edge and arrives at the top of Cancelled.
- Completed, No-show and Cancelled each lead to their own end dot. There are no exits back from them.

## 5 What the key says

The key lists: black dot means Start, light blue rounded rectangle means First state, white rounded rectangle means State, grey rounded rectangle means Final state, black dot with a grey ring means End, a thin arrow means Leads to, and a grey shape with a pink outline means In focus.

Marks on the board that are not in the key: the pale blue Confirmed container (a grouping or parent state is never explained), the square bracket notes on line labels such as "[from In stay]" and the two blue sticky notes on the far right reading "Set by the hotel" and "Set by the guest". The sticky notes are close in colour to the First state fill, so they could be misread as states.

## 6 What I could not read, follow or found ambiguous

- The two sticky notes at the far right are not attached to anything. I cannot tell which states or transitions are set by the hotel and which by the guest.
- The "guest cancels" line from Confirmed leaves the container edge, so I cannot tell whether a guest can cancel from In stay or only from Upcoming. The other two container exits carry a bracket note saying which inner state they come from, but this one does not.
- The inner end dot after In stay is unlabelled. It is unclear whether reaching it is what triggers check out, or whether it is a separate ending.
- Two start dots appear (one before Pending and one inside Confirmed). This is readable once you understand nested states, but a first reader may wonder which one is the real start.
- Around Completed the lines are crowded: the check out line and the missed check-in line both run down the right side near Completed, and the Completed exit loops down and back up to its end dot. I had to trace them carefully.
- Why Cancelled is "In focus" is not explained anywhere on the board.
- The footnote says what happens when card authorisation fails was left out, so Pending has no failure exit.

## 7 Could a product manager act on this board without asking anyone

No, because although the status flow itself is clear, the floating "Set by the hotel" and "Set by the guest" notes are unexplained, cancellation from In stay is ambiguous and the card authorisation failure path is openly missing.

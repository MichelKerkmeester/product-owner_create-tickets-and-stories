## 1 Title and the question this board answers

The title is "Damaged-item refund". The board answers who handles a damaged-item claim at each step, from the customer reporting damage to the refund being released, as the subtitle says.

## 2 Main path from start to end

The customer starts at "Damage reported" (blue start shape, Customer lane). The line goes down to the CS agent lane into the diamond "Damage clear in the photo?". On "Clear" it goes to "Approve the claim" (pink step marked in focus). From there "Logs" goes to the "Claims table" data store and "Handoff to warehouse" goes down to the "Warehouse queue" data store in the Warehouse operator lane. The warehouse path then runs "Book a courier" to "Inspect the item" to "Refund released" (green goal).

## 3 Every decision or branch and where each exit goes

There is one decision, the diamond "Damage clear in the photo?" in the CS agent lane. Exit "Clear" goes right to "Approve the claim". Exit "Unclear" goes up to "Send the second photo" in the Customer lane, which loops back into the diamond. "Approve the claim" also splits into two outgoing lines without a diamond: "Logs" to the Claims table and "Handoff to warehouse" to the Warehouse queue. I read that as both happening, not a choice.

## 4 What the key says

Blue rounded shape means where it starts. White rounded box means step. Teal parallelogram means input or output. Yellow diamond means decision. Green rounded shape with green outline means goal reached. Purple cylinder means data store. A thin solid arrow means leads to. Pink fill with a thick pink outline means in focus. Every shape on the board matches one key entry. The lane background tints (peach, pale teal, pale yellow) are not in the key but each lane carries its own name, so I did not need them. The labels written on lines (Clear, Unclear, Logs, Handoff to warehouse) are not in the key, they explain themselves. There is only one line style on the board, so nothing is missing there.

## 5 What I could not read, follow or found ambiguous

- There is no reject path. The diamond only has Clear and Unclear, so a claim where the photo clearly shows no damage or the damage is not covered has nowhere to go (CS agent lane, diamond).
- The Unclear loop has no stop. If the second photo is still unclear the board just sends it round again (between the Customer and CS agent lanes).
- "Inspect the item" leads straight to "Refund released" with no decision, so I cannot tell what happens if the inspection finds the damage is not real or not as reported (Warehouse operator lane, right side).
- Why "Approve the claim" is the one shape in focus is not explained anywhere. I assume it is the step under discussion but that is a guess.
- The footnote says every decision is logged in the claims table, but only Approve the claim has a Logs line. The Unclear outcome is not shown being logged.
- The footnote mentions a pickup skip under 20 euros and a pilot that is not live, both left out, so the board is incomplete for low-value items and I do not know what the pilot is.
- Two lines enter the diamond at slightly different points on its left side (one from Damage reported, one from Send the second photo). It reads fine but the arrowheads are close together.
- Where the refund is actually paid and who presses the button is not shown. "Refund released" sits in the Warehouse operator lane, which is surprising if finance or CS normally releases money.

## 6 Could I act on this board without asking anyone

No, because I could follow the happy path and the unclear-photo loop but I would have to ask what happens on a rejected claim or a failed inspection and who really releases the refund.

## 1 Title and the question this board answers

The title is "Damaged-item refund". The board answers who handles a damaged-item claim at each step, from the customer reporting damage to the refund being released.

## 2 Main path from start to end

The customer starts at "Damage reported" (blue start shape). A line runs down to the CS agent's decision "Damage clear in the photo?" (yellow diamond). On "Clear" the agent does "Approve the claim" (pink in-focus shape), which "Logs" to the "Claims table" (purple data store). From Approve the claim the "Handoff to warehouse" line goes to the "Warehouse queue" (purple data store) in the Warehouse operator lane. The operator then does "Book a courier" and "Inspect the item" (white steps) and the flow ends at "Refund released" (green goal shape).

## 3 Every decision or branch and where each exit goes

There is one decision, "Damage clear in the photo?".
- "Clear" goes right to "Approve the claim".
- "Unclear" goes up to "Send the second photo" in the Customer lane, and from there a line returns into the left side of the same diamond.

Approve the claim also splits two ways but this is not a decision: "Logs" goes to the Claims table and "Handoff to warehouse" goes to the Warehouse queue. Both happen.

## 4 What the key says

The key lists: light blue rounded shape is "Where it starts". White rounded shape is "Step". Cyan parallelogram is "Input or output". Yellow diamond is "Decision". Green rounded shape with a green outline is "Goal reached". Purple cylinder is "Data store". A thin solid arrow is "Leads to". A pink shape with a heavy pink outline is "In focus".

Every shape on the board matches a key entry. Not in the key: the three lane background colours (peach, mint and cream), though each lane carries its own name so they read fine. The text labels on lines ("Clear", "Unclear", "Logs" and "Handoff to warehouse") are not explained either, but they read as plain labels. All lines look like the same solid style, so there is no dashed line left unexplained.

## 5 What I could not read, follow or found ambiguous

- "In focus" on "Approve the claim" (CS agent lane, centre) is not explained. I do not know why this step is highlighted or what I should take from it.
- There is no reject exit. The diamond only has Clear and Unclear, and the Unclear loop back to the diamond has no limit, so I cannot tell what happens if the second photo is still unclear or the damage is clearly not covered.
- "Inspect the item" (Warehouse operator lane) has no branch. The refund is released whatever the inspection finds, which seems unlikely, and the board does not say what a failed inspection does.
- "Refund released" sits in the Warehouse operator lane, which suggests the warehouse operator releases the money. I would expect finance or the CS agent to do that, so I am not sure the lane is right or just a layout choice.
- The footnote says every decision is logged in the claims table, but only Approve the claim draws a line to it. The diamond's own outcome is not drawn as logged.
- The footnote says pickup is skipped under 20 euros but this is left out of the flow, so Book a courier looks like it always happens.
- The two arrows entering the diamond's left side (from Damage reported and from the photo loop) land very close together and take a moment to separate.

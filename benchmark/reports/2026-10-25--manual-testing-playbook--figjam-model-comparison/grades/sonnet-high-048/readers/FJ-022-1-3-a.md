## 1 Title and the question this board answers

The title is "Start a return". It answers how a Fernhouse customer starts a return in the app, going from the order list to a confirmed return.

## 2 Main path or structure from start to end

The board runs left to right through three sections. In "Find the order" the customer starts at "Order list" (blue, where it starts) and goes to "Order detail" (white step). Then in "Request the return" they hit the diamond "Within 30 days after delivery?". On Yes they go to "Pick a reason" and then to the diamond "Drop-off or courier pickup?". ## 3 Every decision or branch and where each exit goes

"Within 30 days after delivery?" has two exits. Yes goes right to "Pick a reason". No goes down and right to "Contact support", which leaves the flow.

"Drop-off or courier pickup?" has two exits. "Drop-off point" goes up over the top and down into "Confirmation with QR code". "Courier pickup" goes straight right into the same "Confirmation with QR code". So both choices end at the same confirmation.

Peach fill with dashed orange outline means leaves the flow. Thin grey arrow means leads to. Thick pink outline on a diamond means in focus.

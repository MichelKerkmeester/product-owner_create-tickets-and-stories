## 1 Title and the question this board answers

The title is "Damaged-item refund". The board answers how a damaged-item claim moves between the customer, the CS agent and the warehouse operator until the refund is released, and where it is handed to the warehouse.

## 2 Main path from start to end

The customer reports damage with a photo (blue start pill "Damage reported with a photo"). The CS agent checks the "Damage clear?" diamond. On "Clear" the CS agent does "Approve the claim" (pink, in focus), which "Logs" into the "Claims table" data store. Approval then sends the claim on as "Claim queued" to the warehouse operator, who does "Book a courier pickup", then "Inspect the item on arrival", and finally reaches the green goal "Refund released".

## 3 Every decision or branch and where each exit goes

There is one decision, the "Damage clear?" diamond in the CS agent lane.
- "Clear" goes down and right to "Approve the claim".
- "Unclear" goes up to "Send a second photo" in the Customer lane, which loops back into "Damage clear?".

"Approve the claim" also has two outgoing lines, but they are not a decision. "Logs" goes to "Claims table" and "Claim queued" goes to "Book a courier pickup". No exit exists for a rejected claim, a second unclear photo or a failed inspection. The footnote admits the denied claim and failed inspection are left out.

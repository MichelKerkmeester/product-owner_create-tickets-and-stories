## 1 Title and the question this board answers

The title is "Change booking dates". It answers how a Roamstay guest changes the dates of an existing booking in the app, from the booking page through to an updated booking, including what happens with availability and price.

## 2 Main path or structure from start to end

The guest starts on "Booking page" (blue start shape), taps Change dates (label on the arrow) and lands on "Pick new dates". Next comes the decision "Room free?". On Yes the line jumps over the middle steps straight to the decision "New price?". From "New price?" the flow ends in the green goal shape "Updated booking" by one of three routes: via "Get credit", via "Pay difference" or directly with no charge.

## 3 Every decision or branch and where each exit goes

"Room free?" has two exits. "Yes" runs along a line above the row to "New price?". "No" goes right to "See other rooms", then "Pick a room (assumed)", then into "New price?".

"New price?" has three exits. "Lower" goes up to "Get credit" and then to "Updated booking". "Higher" goes right to "Pay difference" and then to "Updated booking". "Same: no charge" runs below the row straight to "Updated booking".

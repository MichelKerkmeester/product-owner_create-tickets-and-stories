## 1 Title and the question this board answers

The title is "Damaged-item refund". It answers how a damaged-item claim travels from the customer report through CS review to the warehouse and ends in a refund, and which role owns each step.

## 2 Main path from start to end

The customer reports damage with a photo (blue start shape). The CS agent checks "Damage clear?" (yellow diamond). On "Clear" the agent does "Approve the claim" (pink in-focus step), which also logs to the "Claims table" (purple data store). The claim is then queued to the warehouse operator, who does "Book a courier pickup" and then "Inspect the item on arrival" (white steps). The path ends at "Refund released" (green goal).

## 3 Every decision and its exits

There is one decision, "Damage clear?". Its "Clear" exit goes down and right to "Approve the claim". Its "Unclear" exit goes up to "Send a second photo" in the Customer lane, which loops back into "Damage clear?". No other shape branches. "Approve the claim" has two outgoing lines but they are not a choice: "Logs" to the Claims table and "Claim queued" to the courier pickup.

## 4 What the key says and whether everything is in it

The key lists: light blue rounded shape is where it starts. White rounded shape is a step. Teal parallelogram is input or output. Yellow diamond is a decision. Green rounded shape with a green outline is goal reached. Purple cylinder is a data store. Thin grey arrow means leads to. Pink fill with a thick pink outline means in focus.

Every shape on the board matches a key entry. Not in the key: the lane background tints (peach, mint and cream), though each lane is labelled so this does not block reading. The words on the lines (Unclear, Clear, Logs, Claim queued) are not explained by the key either but read as plain labels. All lines are the same solid style, so there is nothing else to decode.

## 1 Title and the question this board answers

The title is "Booking status". It answers what statuses a Roamstay booking can be in and what event moves it from one status to the next.

## 2 The main path or structure from start to end

A booking starts at the black Start dot on the far left and becomes Pending. When the card is authorised ("card authorised" line across the top) it enters the Confirmed container. Inside Confirmed an inner start dot leads to Upcoming, then "check in" leads to In stay, then an arrow runs to an inner end dot at the right of the container. On check out ("check out [from In stay]", a line leaving the left side of the container) the booking goes to Completed, which ends at an End dot. That is the happy path: Pending, Upcoming, In stay, Completed.

## 3 Every decision or branch and where each exit goes

There are no diamonds. The branches are events leaving states.
- Pending: "card authorised" goes to Confirmed (into Upcoming). "guest cancels" goes to Cancelled.
- Upcoming: "check in" goes to In stay. "missed check-in [from Upcoming]" leaves the bottom of the Confirmed container and goes to No-show.
- In stay: an arrow goes to the inner end dot. "check out [from In stay]" leaves the left of the container and goes to Completed.
- Confirmed as a whole: "guest cancels" leaves the right edge and goes to Cancelled.
- No-show, Completed and Cancelled each go to their own End dot.

## 4 The key

The key lists: black dot = Start, blue rounded box = First state, white rounded box = State, grey rounded box with a thicker grey outline = Final state, black dot with a grey ring = End, plain arrow = Leads to, grey box with a thick pink outline = In focus.
Not every mark is in the key. The light blue Confirmed container (a state that holds other states) has no key entry. The bracketed text on edge labels such as "[from Upcoming]" is not explained. The inner start dot and inner end dot inside Confirmed use the Start and End marks, but the key does not say they mean entering and leaving the container. All lines are solid with one style, which matches "Leads to". The key does not say why Cancelled is "In focus".

## The three questions

Q1: Which states end a booking?
No-show, Completed and Cancelled. They are the three grey states that the key calls Final state and each leads to an end dot on its right. Sure, from the middle and lower right of the board plus the key.

Q2: From which states can a guest cancel?
From Pending, along the bottom "guest cancels" line, and from Confirmed, along the "guest cancels" line that leaves the right edge of the Confirmed box. Because that line leaves the box and not a single state, I read it as Upcoming or In stay. Fairly sure about Pending and Confirmed. Q3: What moves a booking from Upcoming to In stay?
The "check in" event, on the short arrow between Upcoming and In stay inside the Confirmed box. Sure.

## 1 Title and the question this board answers

"Booking status", with the subtitle "Every status a Roamstay booking passes through, and what moves it on". It answers which statuses a booking can have and which event moves it from one to the next.

## 2 Main path from start to end

A booking starts at the black dot on the left and enters Pending (the first state, light blue). "card authorised" moves it into Confirmed, the light blue box. Inside Confirmed it starts at Upcoming. "check in" moves it to In stay. From In stay, "check out [from In stay]" moves it to Completed, which leads to an end dot. That is the happy path: Pending, Upcoming, In stay, Completed.

## 3 Decisions and branches

- Pending has two exits: "card authorised" goes up and into Confirmed (landing on Upcoming through the inner start dot), and "guest cancels" goes down and right into Cancelled.
- Upcoming has two exits: "check in" goes to In stay, and "missed check-in [from Upcoming]" leaves the bottom of the Confirmed box and goes to No-show.
- In stay exits by "check out [from In stay]", which leaves the left side of the Confirmed box and goes round to Completed. In stay also has an arrow to an end dot inside the Confirmed box.
- Confirmed as a whole has "guest cancels" leaving its right side and dropping into Cancelled from above.
- No-show, Completed and Cancelled each go straight to an end dot. None of them has another exit.

There are no diamonds. The branches are all shown as several labelled arrows leaving a state or the box.

## 5 Unclear or ambiguous

- The end dot inside the Confirmed box to the right of In stay. It is not clear what reaching it means, since leaving In stay already goes to Completed by "check out". It reads like a second way out of In stay with no label.
- The "guest cancels" line on the right leaves the edge of the whole Confirmed box, so it seems to allow a cancel during In stay. I am not sure a guest can cancel a stay already in progress. The board does not say.
- "missed check-in" and "check out" also leave the box edge and rely on the bracket text to say which inner state they come from. That works but takes a second look, and the bracket notation is not in the key.
- The "check out" line starts at the left edge of the Confirmed box near Pending height and loops down to Completed. On first glance it looks like it might come from Pending.
- Pink "In focus" on Cancelled has no stated reason.
- The source line "the booking status lifecycle described in the request" points to something a reader of the board cannot see.
- No timing or actor is given for "missed check-in" (who decides it and when).

## 6 Could I act on it

Yes for the main lifecycle and the final states, but I would ask whether a guest can cancel during In stay and what the unlabelled end of In stay inside Confirmed means before building on it.

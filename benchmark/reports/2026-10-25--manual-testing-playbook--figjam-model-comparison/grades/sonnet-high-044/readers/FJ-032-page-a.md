## 1 Title and the question this board answers

The title is "Booking status". The board answers which statuses a Roamstay booking passes through and what event moves it from one status to the next.

## 2 Main path or structure from start to end

A booking starts at the start dot and enters Pending (blue, the first state). On "card authorised" it moves into the Confirmed group. Inside Confirmed it starts at Upcoming, moves on "check in" to In stay and then reaches the group's end dot. From In stay, "check out" leads to Completed, which ends at an end dot. That is the happy path: start, Pending, Confirmed (Upcoming then In stay), Completed, end.

## 3 Every decision or branch and where each exit goes

There are no diamond decisions. The branches are events leaving a state.
- Pending: "card authorised" goes to Confirmed. "guest cancels" goes down and along the bottom to Cancelled.
- Upcoming (inside Confirmed): "check in" goes to In stay. "missed check-in [from Upcoming]" goes to No-show.
- In stay (inside Confirmed): "check out [from In stay]" goes to Completed.
- Confirmed as a whole: "guest cancels" leaves its right edge and goes to Cancelled.
- No-show, Completed and Cancelled each go to an end dot.

## 4 What the key says

The key lists: black dot is Start, blue rounded box is First state, white rounded box is State, grey rounded box is Final state, black dot with a grey ring is End and a plain arrow is "Leads to". The colours on the states all match. Not in the key: the light blue Confirmed group itself (a container with its own start and end), the line labels in square brackets such as "[from Upcoming]" and "[from In stay]" and the fact that lines can leave the edge of the group. The retro sticky notes are not in the key, which is fine because they are not part of the diagram. All lines are the same solid style, so there is no line style left unexplained.

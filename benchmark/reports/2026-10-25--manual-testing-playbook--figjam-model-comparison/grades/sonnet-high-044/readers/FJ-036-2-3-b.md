## 1 Title and the question this board answers

The title is "Change booking dates". It answers how a Roamstay guest changes the dates of an existing booking in the app and what happens to the price, from the booking page to the updated booking.

## 2 Main path from start to end

The guest starts on the blue "Booking page", taps "Change dates" (label on the arrow) and lands on "Pick new dates". Next is the diamond "Room free on new dates?". If yes the guest goes straight to the price check. If no the guest goes to "Choose another room" and then to the price check. The price check is the pink outlined diamond "How does the new price compare?" in the "Settle the price" zone. It splits three ways into "Pay the difference", "Get the difference as credit" or "Nothing charged" and all three join at the green "Updated booking", which is the goal.

## 3 Every decision and where each exit goes

"Room free on new dates?" has two exits. "Yes" runs up and over "Choose another room" into the left side of "How does the new price compare?". "No" goes to "Choose another room", which then flows into the same price diamond.

"How does the new price compare?" has three exits. "Higher" goes to "Pay the difference". "Lower" goes to "Get the difference as credit". "Same" goes to "Nothing charged". Each of the three then leads to "Updated booking".

## 4 What the key says and whether every mark is in it

The key says a blue rounded shape is where it starts, a white rounded shape is a screen or step, a yellow diamond is a decision, a green rounded shape is goal reached, a thin arrow means leads to and a yellow diamond with a thick pink outline is in focus.

Almost every mark is in the key. The two coloured zones (peach "Find a room" and mint "Settle the price") are not in the key but they carry their own names so I did not need them explained. The text labels on arrows (Tap Change dates, Yes, No, Higher, Lower, Same) are also not keyed but read plainly as conditions or actions. The key does not say why the price diamond is in focus.

## 5 Anything unreadable, hard to follow or ambiguous

All text is readable at both sizes.

The "Yes" line from "Room free on new dates?" travels up, across and then down into the upper left edge of the pink diamond, just above where the arrow from "Choose another room" lands. Two arrows meet the diamond's left side very close together, which takes a second look to sort out.

"Choose another room" goes straight to the price check with no check that the other room is free. The note under the key says the case with no other room free is left out, so this is a known gap rather than a drawing error.

"In focus" is keyed but nothing says what the focus is for or why the price decision is singled out.

"Get the difference as credit" does not say where the credit goes. The note under the key says this is left out on purpose, along with payment failures, cancellation and change fees and deadlines.

## 6 Could I act on this board without asking anyone

Yes for the happy path and the three price outcomes, since every branch is labelled and ends at "Updated booking", but I would still need to ask about the gaps the note lists (no other room free, payment failure, fees and where the credit goes) before building the full feature.

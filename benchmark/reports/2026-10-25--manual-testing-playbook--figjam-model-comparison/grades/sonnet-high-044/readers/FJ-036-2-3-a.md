## 1 Title and the question this board answers

The title is "Change booking dates". The board answers how a Roamstay guest changes the dates of an existing booking in the app and what happens to the price when they do, from the booking page through to the updated booking.

## 2 Main path from start to end

The guest starts on the blue "Booking page", taps "Change dates" (label on the arrow) and lands on "Pick new dates". The yellow diamond "Room free on new dates?" checks availability. On Yes the line runs over the top straight into the pink diamond "How does the new price compare?". On No the guest goes through "Choose another room" and then into the same pink diamond. The pink diamond splits into three outcomes: "Pay the difference", "Get the difference as credit" or "Nothing charged". All three join at the green "Updated booking", which is the goal.

## 3 Every decision and where each exit goes

- "Room free on new dates?" (yellow diamond, Find a room). Yes goes over the top to "How does the new price compare?". No goes to "Choose another room", which then leads to "How does the new price compare?".
- "How does the new price compare?" (yellow diamond with pink outline, Settle the price). Higher goes to "Pay the difference". Lower goes to "Get the difference as credit". Same goes to "Nothing charged". Each of those three then leads to "Updated booking".

# Fresh reader: Booking status board

## 1 Title and the question this board answers

The title is "Booking status" with the subtitle "Every status a Roamstay booking passes through, and what moves it on". It answers the question: what statuses can a booking be in, and which event moves it from one status to the next until it ends?

## 2 What you see first, and what second

First I see the large bold title and then the pale blue "Confirmed" container across the top, with the bright pink outlined "In stay" state inside it pulling the eye. Second I see the blue "Pending" state on the left and the column of grey final states on the right (Completed, No-show and Cancelled), then the Key box along the bottom and the source note below it.

## 3 The main path or structure from start to end

This is a state diagram. A start dot leads to Pending, the blue rounded rectangle marked as the first state. From Pending a "card authorised" line climbs up and enters the Confirmed container on its left edge. Confirmed is a composite state with its own start dot, which leads to Upcoming (a white rounded rectangle). Upcoming moves on "check-in" to In stay (white with the pink in focus outline). In stay then runs to an end dot inside the Confirmed container. From the top of the Confirmed container a "check-out [from In stay]" line runs right and down into Completed, a grey final state, and Completed goes on to an end dot. So the happy path is Pending, card authorised, Confirmed (Upcoming, check-in, In stay), check-out, Completed, End.

## 4 Every decision or branch and where each exit goes

There are no diamonds. The branches are the several transitions out of each state.

- Pending has two exits: "card authorised" into Confirmed, and "guest cancels" along the bottom into Cancelled.
- Upcoming has two exits: "check-in" into In stay, and "missed check-in [from Upcoming]" which leaves the right edge of Confirmed and ends in No-show.
- In stay exits through "check-out [from In stay]" into Completed.
- The Confirmed container as a whole has a "guest cancels" exit from its bottom edge into Cancelled.
- Completed, No-show and Cancelled each lead only to their own end dot.

## 5 What the key says, and whether every mark is in the key

The key lists: plain black dot means Start. Light blue filled rounded rectangle means First state. White rounded rectangle means State. Grey filled rounded rectangle means Final state. Black dot with a grey ring means End. Pink thick outline means In focus.

Not every mark is in the key. The pale blue Confirmed container (a composite or grouping state) has no key entry. Connector lines have no key entry either. They are all thin solid grey with arrowheads so there is only one line style, which is readable without a key, but the convention that a bracketed note such as "[from In stay]" names the inner source state is not explained anywhere.

## 6 Anything I could not read, could not follow or found ambiguous

- Inside Confirmed, In stay runs straight to an end dot with no label. I could not tell what that internal end means, or whether it is the same moment as check-out or something separate.
- The check-out line leaves the top edge of Confirmed above In stay, not from In stay itself. Only the bracketed text tells me the true source.
- The "missed check-in [from Upcoming]" route is broken. A line leaves the right edge of Confirmed and stops above the label near Completed, and a second line starts below the label further left and runs down to No-show. They do not visibly join, and the label sits crowded against the top of Completed, so at first glance it reads as if the line belongs to Completed.
- The "guest cancels" line from Confirmed leaves the bottom edge directly under In stay. It is not clear whether a guest can cancel only while Upcoming or also while already In stay.
- Completed reaches its end dot by a detour that drops below the box and climbs back up, which looks odd next to the straight lines used by No-show and Cancelled.
- Why In stay is "in focus" is never said. The board gives no reason for the highlight.
- The source note says payment failure, expiry on Pending and refunds are left out, so those paths are known gaps.

## 7 Could a product manager act on this board without asking anyone

No, because although the statuses and main transitions are clear, the unlabelled end inside Confirmed, the broken missed check-in line, the unclear scope of cancelling from Confirmed and the unexplained focus on In stay would each need a question before building or speccing anything.

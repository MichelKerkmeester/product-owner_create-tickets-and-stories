# Fresh reader notes: Booking status 2

## 1 Title and the question this board answers

The title is "Booking status 2". The subtitle reads "Every status a Roamstay booking passes through, and what moves it on". So the board answers two questions. Which statuses can a booking be in? Which event moves it from one status to the next?

## 2 What you see first, and what second

First I see the large title and then the wide pale blue box labelled "Confirmed" across the top, with three white or dark shapes inside it. Second my eye drops to the blue "Pending" pill on the left, which stands out as the only filled blue state. After that I see the column of three grey pills on the right (Completed, No-show and Cancelled), each with a ringed dark dot beside it.

## 3 The main path or structure from start to end

This is a state diagram. Rounded pills are states, solid dark dots are starts and dark dots with a grey ring are ends.

The main path runs as follows. A start dot on the left points to Pending (blue pill, the first state). From the top of Pending a line labelled "card authorised" runs left, up and into the left edge of the Confirmed box. Confirmed is a parent state that holds its own small flow: an inner start dot points to Upcoming (white pill), "check in" moves Upcoming to In stay (white pill) and an unlabelled arrow runs from In stay to a ringed end dot inside the Confirmed box. From the top of the Confirmed box a line labelled "check out [from In stay]" runs right and down into Completed (grey pill). Completed then connects to its own end dot on the far right through a line that leaves its bottom edge and climbs back up to the dot.

## 4 Every decision or branch and where each exit goes

There are no diamonds. The branches are the several transitions out of each state.

- Pending has two exits. "card authorised" goes into Confirmed (landing on the inner start, so Upcoming). "guest cancels" runs from the right side of Pending down and across to Cancelled.
- Upcoming has one inner exit, "check in", to In stay. It also seems to own "missed check-in [from Upcoming]", which leaves the right edge of the Confirmed box and should reach No-show.
- In stay has an unlabelled exit to the inner end dot. The "check out [from In stay]" line leaves the top of the box to Completed.
- A second "guest cancels" line leaves the bottom of the Confirmed box (below In stay) and drops into the top of Cancelled. It carries no "[from ...]" note, so I read it as applying to the whole Confirmed status, meaning a guest can cancel from Upcoming or from In stay.
- Completed, No-show and Cancelled each lead to their own ringed end dot and nothing else.

## 5 What the key says, and whether every mark is in it

The key at the bottom lists five marks: Start (solid dark dot), First state (blue filled pill), State (white pill), Final state (grey pill) and End (dark dot with grey ring).

Marks on the board that the key does not explain:

- The pale blue Confirmed container. Nothing says it is a parent or grouping state.
- Line style. Every line is a thin solid grey arrow and the key has no entry for lines or arrows at all.
- The square bracket notes such as "[from In stay]" and "[from Upcoming]". Their meaning (which inner state the exit belongs to) is guessable but not stated.
- The ringed end dot inside Confirmed looks identical to the End mark, yet it sits inside a parent state rather than ending the booking. The key does not separate the two uses.

A footer line reads "Source: the request. Left out: nothing."

## 6 Anything I could not read, follow or found ambiguous

- Right side, between the Confirmed box and No-show: the "missed check-in [from Upcoming]" line looks broken. One line drops from the right edge of the Confirmed box and stops just above the label. A separate line starts below the label further left and runs down to No-show. I assume they are one connector but they do not visibly join.
- Inside Confirmed, top right: the arrow from In stay to the inner end dot has no label. I cannot tell if reaching that dot is the same event as "check out" or something else, and the check out line leaves the top of the box rather than that dot.
- Far right, next to Completed: the end dot sits level with Completed but the connector leaves Completed's bottom edge, runs right and climbs back up to the dot. It reads as a detour and at first glance the dot looks unconnected.
- The top "check out" line and the bottom "guest cancels" line both leave the Confirmed box at the same horizontal position under In stay, which made me wonder briefly if the cancel exit belongs only to In stay.
- The title ends in "2". Nothing on the board says what version 1 was or how this one differs.
- It is not stated whether Pending can time out or fail authorisation. Only card authorised and guest cancels leave it.

## 7 Could a product manager act on this board without asking anyone

Mostly yes, because every status and every named trigger is readable and the terminal outcomes are clear, but they would likely need to ask whether "guest cancels" applies during In stay and what the unlabelled inner end inside Confirmed means before writing rules.

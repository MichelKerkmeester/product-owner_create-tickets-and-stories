# FJ-017-1-3 fresh reader notes

## 1 Title and the question this board answers

The title is "Booking status". The subtitle says "Every status a Roamstay booking passes through and what moves it on, for support". The board answers this question: what statuses can a booking be in, and which event moves it from one status to the next? It is a state diagram.

## 2 What you see first, and what second

First I see the large bold title and then the big pale blue "Confirmed" container across the top, with Upcoming and In stay inside it. Second my eye goes to the bright pink outlined "Cancelled" box at the lower right, because it is the only strongly coloured outline on the board. After that I notice the light blue "Pending" box on the left and the two blue sticky notes on the far right.

## 3 The main path or structure from start to end, naming the shapes

The board starts at a black start dot on the left that points into "Pending" (a light blue rounded box with a blue border). From Pending a line labelled "card authorised" runs up and left and enters the left edge of the "Confirmed" container. Inside Confirmed there is its own start dot, which points to "Upcoming" (a white rounded box). Upcoming goes to "In stay" (another white rounded box) on the line labelled "check in". In stay then runs on an unlabelled line to an end dot inside the Confirmed container.

From the Confirmed container a line labelled "check out [from In stay]" leaves the top edge, runs across the top of the board and comes down into "Completed" (a grey rounded box). Completed then goes to an end dot (a black dot with a grey ring) to its right. That is the happy path: Pending, Confirmed (Upcoming then In stay), Completed, end.

## 4 Every decision or branch and where each exit goes

There are no diamond decisions. Branching is shown by several labelled transitions leaving the same state.

- Pending has two exits. "card authorised" goes to the Confirmed container. "guest cancels" goes along the bottom to Cancelled.
- Confirmed has three exits leaving its edges. "check out [from In stay]" leaves the top and goes to Completed. "missed check-in [from Upcoming]" leaves the right edge and goes to No-show. "guest cancels" leaves the bottom edge, directly under In stay, and goes to Cancelled.
- Inside Confirmed, Upcoming goes to In stay on "check in" and In stay goes to the inner end dot with no label.
- Completed, No-show and Cancelled each go to their own end dot on the right, so all three are final.

Two sticky notes add who triggers a state. "No-show: set by the hotel" sits next to No-show. "Cancelled: set by the guest" sits next to Cancelled.

## 5 What the key says, and whether every mark is in the key

The key at the bottom lists six marks. A plain black dot is "Start". A light blue rounded box with a blue border is "First state". A white rounded box is "State". A grey rounded box is "Final state". A black dot with a grey ring is "End". A grey box with a thick pink border is "In focus".

Not everything on the board is in the key. The large pale blue Confirmed container (a state that holds other states) has no key entry. The two blue sticky notes have no key entry. There is only one line style on the board (thin grey solid arrows) and the key does not mention lines at all, nor the bracketed text such as "[from In stay]" on the labels. The key also does not say why Cancelled is in focus or what "in focus" is meant to draw attention to.

## 6 Anything I could not read, could not follow or found ambiguous

- The "guest cancels" line from Confirmed leaves the bottom edge right under In stay. I cannot tell whether a guest can cancel during In stay or only while Upcoming. The label has no bracket to say which, unlike the other two exits from Confirmed.
- The "missed check-in [from Upcoming]" line looks broken. A line drops from the right edge of Confirmed and stops at the label, then a separate line starts left of Completed and runs down into No-show. I had to infer that these two pieces are one transition.
- The "check out [from In stay]" line leaves the top edge of the container above In stay, while In stay also has its own unlabelled arrow to the inner end dot. It is not clear whether these are the same event drawn twice or two different things.
- The pink "In focus" highlight on Cancelled is not explained, so I do not know why this state matters more than the others.
- The footer says a failed card authorisation is left out because no source says where Pending goes then. So the board admits a known gap at Pending.
- Both sticky notes have a lot of empty space and only one line of text each, which reads as unfinished but is fully legible.

All text on the board was readable at full size.

## 7 Could a product manager act on this board without asking anyone

No, because the board is clear for the main statuses but the footer leaves the failed card authorisation path open and it is ambiguous whether a guest can still cancel once the booking is In stay, so a product manager would need to ask about both before acting.

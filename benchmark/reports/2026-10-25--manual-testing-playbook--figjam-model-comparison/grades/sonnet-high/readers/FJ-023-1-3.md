# Reader notes: FJ-023-1-3

## 1 Title and the question this board answers

The title is "Damaged-item refund". The subtitle states the question: who handles a damaged-item claim, from the customer's report to the released refund. It is a swimlane flowchart with three lanes, one per role.

## 2 What you see first, and what second

First the large bold title and then the bright pink "Approve the claim" box in the middle lane, which has a thick pink border and is the loudest shape on the board. Second the three coloured horizontal lanes (Customer at the top, CS agent in the middle, Warehouse operator at the bottom) and the yellow diamond "Damage clear?" that sits left of the pink box.

## 3 The main path or structure from start to end

The flow starts in the Customer lane at the light blue rounded box "Damage reported". A solid line drops down into the CS agent lane and enters the yellow diamond "Damage clear?" from the left. On the Clear exit a solid arrow runs right to the pink rounded box "Approve the claim". From there a solid line labelled "To the warehouse queue" runs right and then down across the lane border into the Warehouse operator lane, where it reaches the white rounded step "Book a courier pickup". Solid arrows then lead to the white rounded step "Inspect the item" and finally to the green rounded box "Refund released", which is the end goal at the far right.

## 4 Every decision or branch and where each exit goes

There is one decision, the diamond "Damage clear?" in the CS agent lane.

- Clear: exits to the right with a solid arrow to "Approve the claim", which continues to the warehouse lane as described above.
- Unclear: exits from the top of the diamond, runs right and then up into the Customer lane to the teal parallelogram "Send a second photo". From the left side of that parallelogram a solid line runs left and then down, joining the incoming line from "Damage reported" just before the diamond. This forms a loop back to the decision.
- Logs: a dashed arrow leaves the bottom of the diamond and runs right to the purple cylinder "Claims table" in the CS agent lane. It is not a decision answer but a side record.

The warehouse lane has no branches. Every step there leads straight to the next.

## 5 What the key says and whether every mark is in it

The key at the bottom lists:

- Light blue rounded box: where it starts.
- White rounded box: step.
- Teal parallelogram: input or output.
- Yellow diamond: decision.
- Green rounded box: goal reached.
- Purple cylinder: data store.
- Dashed arrow: optional or proposed.
- Pink box with a thick pink border: in focus.

Every shape on the board matches a key entry. Two kinds of mark are not in the key. The plain solid arrow is not listed, though it reads naturally as the normal flow. The three lane background colours (peach, pale teal, pale yellow) are not in the key, though the lane titles name them. A footnote under the key gives the source (the support runbook) and says what was left out: pickup skipped under 20 euros, which is a pilot that is not live. It also says the runbook gives no path for a failed inspection and no limit on second photos.

## 6 Anything unreadable, hard to follow or ambiguous

- The return line from "Send a second photo" (top centre, Customer lane) merges into the line from "Damage reported" right at the diamond's left arrowhead, around the left edge of the CS agent lane. The two lines share one arrowhead, so at a glance it is hard to tell the loop returns to the decision and not to some other point. It also has no label.
- The dashed "Logs" arrow (CS agent lane, below the diamond) leaves the decision itself rather than a step. It is unclear what is logged, at what moment (on every check, only on Clear or also on Unclear) and who does it. Under the key the dashed style means optional or proposed, so I cannot tell whether logging to the "Claims table" happens today or is only suggested.
- "Approve the claim" is marked in focus but the board does not say why it is in focus, for example whether it is the step under discussion or a step that is changing.
- The label "To the warehouse queue" sits on the line just above the border between the CS agent and Warehouse operator lanes (centre right). It reads fine, but it implies a queue that is not drawn as a shape or data store.
- "Refund released" sits in the Warehouse operator lane, which suggests the warehouse operator releases the money. That may be correct but it is unusual and not stated.
- Gaps are disclosed only in the footnote and not on the flow itself: "Inspect the item" has a single outgoing arrow, so a failed inspection has no drawn exit, and the photo loop has no limit.
- All text was readable at 100 percent.

## 7 Could a product manager act on this board without asking anyone

No, because although the happy path and the photo loop are clear, the PM would still need to ask what happens after a failed inspection, whether there is a cap on second photo requests, whether the dashed logging is live or proposed and why the approval step is in focus.

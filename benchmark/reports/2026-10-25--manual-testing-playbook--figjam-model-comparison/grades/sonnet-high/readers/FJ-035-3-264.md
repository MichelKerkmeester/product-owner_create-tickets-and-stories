# Reader notes for FJ-035-3-264

## 1 Title and the question this board answers

The title is "Start a return". The subtitle reads "How a Fernhouse customer starts a return in the app, from the order list to a confirmed return". The board answers the question: what steps and choices does a customer go through in the app to get from their order list to a confirmed return?

## 2 What you see first, and what second

First I see the large bold title and then the bright pink outlined green box at the far right, "Confirmation with QR code", because the pink outline is the loudest mark on the board. Second I see the two yellow diamonds in the middle band, which tell me the flow branches twice.

## 3 The main path or structure from start to end

The board is a left to right flowchart split into three tinted sections. The "Find the order" section (peach tint) holds "Order list", a blue rounded rectangle marked as the start, which leads to "Order detail", a white rounded rectangle. An arrow then crosses into the "Request the return" section (mint tint) and reaches the yellow diamond "Within 30 days of delivery?". The Yes exit leads to "Pick a reason" (white rounded rectangle), which leads to the second yellow diamond "Drop-off or pickup?". The Drop-off exit leads to "Choose a drop-off point" (white rounded rectangle) and on into the "Done" section (cream tint) to "Confirmation with QR code", a green rounded rectangle with a thick pink outline. That is the happy path.

## 4 Every decision or branch and where each exit goes

There are two decisions.

"Within 30 days of delivery?" has two exits. Yes goes right to "Pick a reason". No drops down and right to "Contact support", an orange rounded rectangle that the key calls "Leaves the flow". Nothing leaves "Contact support", so it is an end point.

"Drop-off or pickup?" has two exits. Drop-off goes right to "Choose a drop-off point", which then goes to "Confirmation with QR code". Pickup drops down and right to "Book a courier pickup", which then runs right and turns up to enter "Confirmation with QR code" from below. Both exits of this second decision end at the same confirmation.

## 5 What the key says and whether every mark is in it

The key at the bottom lists six entries: blue rounded rectangle means "Where it starts", white rounded rectangle means "Screen or step", yellow diamond means "Decision", green rounded rectangle means "Goal reached", orange rounded rectangle means "Leaves the flow" and a green box with a thick pink outline means "In focus".

Every node on the board matches a key entry. Three kinds of mark are not in the key. The tinted section backgrounds (peach, mint and cream) have titles but no key entry, so I read them as stages rather than as coded meanings. The connectors are all one style (thin grey solid line with an arrowhead) and the key does not mention them, though they read plainly as "goes to". The small branch labels Yes, No, Drop-off and Pickup are also not in the key but are self explanatory.

## 6 What I could not read, follow or found ambiguous

All text is legible at 100 percent. The ambiguities are these.

The "In focus" outline sits on the confirmation box at the far right, but nothing on the board says why that step is in focus or what a reader should do about it.

"Contact support" at the bottom of the middle section is a dead end. The board does not say what happens after support is contacted or whether the customer can rejoin the flow.

Between "Order detail" and the first diamond there is no step showing how the customer actually starts the return (for example a button). It is not clear whether the 30 day check is something the customer sees or a check the app makes silently.

The footer note under the key says the sources (the request's description of the app screens and the returns doc) are not attached and that the status is unconfirmed because the Fernhouse context dated 2026-09-18 records no self-serve return. So I cannot tell whether this flow exists today or is a proposal.

## 7 Could a product manager act on this board without asking anyone

No, because although the flow itself is clear and complete on its happy paths, the footer says the status is unconfirmed and the sources are missing, and the board leaves open why the confirmation is in focus and what happens after "Contact support".

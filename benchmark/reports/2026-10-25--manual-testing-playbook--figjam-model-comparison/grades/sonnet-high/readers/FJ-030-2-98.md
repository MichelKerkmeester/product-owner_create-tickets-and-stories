# Reader notes: FJ-030-2-98

## 1 Title and the question this board answers

The title is "Start a return". The subtitle reads "How a Fernhouse customer starts a return in the app, and the three steps where support says they give up". The board answers two questions. How does a customer get from their order to a confirmed return? And at which three steps do customers abandon the return, according to support?

## 2 What you see first, and what second

First I see the large bold title and then the horizontal flow across three tinted sections. The node that stands out most is "Return confirmed, QR code" on the right because of its thick pink outline. Second I notice the three purple numbered badges (1, 2 and 3) sitting on steps in the middle section. After that I see the three blue sticky notes in a column on the far right. The key below the flow comes last.

## 3 The main path or structure from start to end

The flow runs left to right through three sections.

Section "Find the order" (peach tint): "Order list" (blue rounded rectangle, the start) leads to "Order detail" (white rounded rectangle, a screen).

Section "Request the return" (mint tint): "Order detail" leads into the yellow diamond "Within 30 days of delivery?" (badge 1). The Yes exit goes to "Reason list" (white rounded rectangle, badge 2). "Reason list" leads to the yellow diamond "Drop-off point or courier pickup?" (badge 3).

Section "Done" (mint tint): both exits of the second diamond end at "Return confirmed, QR code" (green rounded rectangle with a thick pink outline).

## 4 Every decision or branch and where each exit goes

Decision 1, "Within 30 days of delivery?": the right exit is labelled "Yes, Start a return" and goes to "Reason list". The bottom exit is labelled "No, Contact support" and goes to "Contact support", an orange rounded rectangle. Nothing leaves "Contact support", so it is a dead end for this flow.

Decision 2, "Drop-off point or courier pickup?": the right exit is labelled "Courier pickup" and goes straight to "Return confirmed, QR code". The top exit is labelled "Drop-off point" and loops over the top, then drops down into the same "Return confirmed, QR code" node. Both choices therefore reach the same end.

The numbered badges tie to the sticky notes on the right. Note 1: "Support says customers give up here: nothing warns them before day 30" (on the 30 day decision). Note 2: "Support says customers give up here: too many reasons to choose from" (on Reason list). Note 3: "Support says customers give up here: the pickup fee is unclear" (on the drop-off or pickup decision).

## 5 What the key says, and whether every mark is in the key

The key lists six entries. Blue rounded rectangle means "Where it starts". White rounded rectangle means "Screen or step". Yellow diamond means "Decision". Green rounded rectangle means "Goal reached". Orange rounded rectangle means "Leaves the flow". Green fill with a thick pink outline means "In focus".

Not every mark is in the key. The purple numbered badges are not explained there, though the sticky notes make their meaning clear by number. The blue sticky notes are not in the key. The peach and mint section tints are not in the key and I cannot tell whether the colour difference carries meaning or is just grouping. Connector lines are all thin grey solid arrows and the key says nothing about lines, which is fine since there is only one line style.

## 6 Anything I could not read, could not follow or found ambiguous

The "In focus" highlight sits on "Return confirmed, QR code", the goal node, while the subtitle and notes put the problem at steps 1, 2 and 3. I could not tell why the end state is the one in focus rather than the three drop off points.

The sticky notes sit far to the right of the flow and are linked to their steps only by number, not by position or connector. That works but needs a back and forth glance.

Note 1, "nothing warns them before day 30", is ambiguous. I read it as customers not being reminded that the return window is closing, but it could also mean the app gives no warning on the order screens.

The courier pickup path mentions a fee only in note 3. The board does not show what the fee is or where in the flow it appears. The footer says free pickup over 100 euros was left out as a proposal under review, which hints at the fee rules without stating them.

The drop-off point path goes straight to confirmation with no step for picking a location, so I am not sure whether a screen is skipped or simply not drawn.

What happens after "Contact support" is not shown.

All text was readable at full size, including the small connector labels and the footer.

## 7 Could a product manager act on this board without asking anyone

Yes, mostly: the flow and the three support reported drop off points are clear enough to start work on the 30 day warning, the reason list and the pickup fee clarity, though they would likely ask what the current pickup fee is and why the goal node rather than the problem steps is marked in focus.

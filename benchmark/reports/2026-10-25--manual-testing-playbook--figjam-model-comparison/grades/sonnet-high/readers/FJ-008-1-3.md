# Reader notes: FJ-008-1-3

## 1 Title and the question this board answers

The title is "Stays model". The subtitle reads "The six Roamstay stay entities and the keys that join them, for the team working on stays". The board answers the question: what are the six data entities behind a stay and which keys connect them, with what cardinality?

## 2 What you see first, and what second

First the large bold title "Stays model" at top left. Second the Booking box in the middle right, because it is the only box with a thick magenta border and it carries the most rows (one PK and three FK lines). After that the eye follows the grey connector lines outward to the other five boxes.

## 3 The main path or structure from start to end, naming the shapes

This is an entity relationship diagram, not a flow, so there is no start or end. There are six white rectangles with a thin grey border, each holding a bold entity name and its key columns:

- Guest (top left): PK id uuid.
- Property (middle left): PK id uuid.
- Room (middle centre): PK id uuid and FK propertyId uuid.
- Rate Plan (bottom centre): PK id uuid and FK propertyId uuid.
- Booking (middle right, magenta border): PK id uuid then FK guestId uuid then FK roomId uuid then FK ratePlanId uuid.
- Payment (far right): PK id uuid and FK bookingId uuid.

Reading left to right the structure is Property feeding Room and Rate Plan, those two plus Guest all feeding Booking, and Booking feeding Payment. Booking is the hub.

## 4 Relationships it shows

All six connectors are solid grey lines with crow's foot ends. Each has a verb label and "(1 to 0..n)":

- Guest "makes (1 to 0..n)" Booking. Double bar at Guest, circle plus crow's foot at Booking. Matches Booking FK guestId.
- Property "offers (1 to 0..n)" Room. Double bar at Property, circle plus crow's foot at Room. Matches Room FK propertyId.
- Property "publishes (1 to 0..n)" Rate Plan. The line leaves the bottom of Property, runs down and turns right into Rate Plan. Double bar at Property, circle plus crow's foot at Rate Plan. Matches Rate Plan FK propertyId.
- Room "is reserved by (1 to 0..n)" Booking. Double bar at Room, circle plus crow's foot at Booking. Matches Booking FK roomId.
- Rate Plan "prices (1 to 0..n)" Booking. The line leaves Rate Plan to the right and turns up into the bottom of Booking. Double bar at Rate Plan, circle plus crow's foot at Booking. Matches Booking FK ratePlanId.
- Booking "holds (1 to 0..n)" Payment. Double bar at Booking, circle plus crow's foot at Payment. Matches Payment FK bookingId.

Every FK on the board has exactly one matching line and every line has a matching FK, so the keys and the lines agree. No timing or messages are shown.

## 5 What the key says, and whether every mark is in the key

The key box at the bottom lists three items:

- A plain white rectangle with a grey border: "Entity".
- A solid grey line: "Identifying".
- A rectangle with a thick magenta border: "In focus".

Not every mark is in the key. The crow's foot end symbols (double bar, circle, crow's foot) are not explained, so a reader must already know crow's foot notation. The text "(1 to 0..n)" on each line does spell the cardinality out in words, which partly covers this. The abbreviations PK and FK and the type "uuid" are not explained either. The key offers only one line style, so there is no non-identifying style to compare against.

A footer line reads "Source: the stays model request. Keys and cardinalities derived from it. Left out: every non-key column."

## 6 Anything I could not read, could not follow or found ambiguous

- Everything is legible at both zoom levels. I found no unreadable text.
- The key calls every line "Identifying", yet in each child box the FK is a separate column and the PK is just id. In standard notation that makes these non-identifying relationships. It is unclear whether "Identifying" is meant in the technical sense or simply means "a relationship line".
- "In focus" for Booking is not explained. I cannot tell whether it means Booking is the subject of the current change, the most important entity or simply the hub.
- The label "is reserved by" between Room and Booking reads Room to Booking, while the other labels read parent then verb then child in an active voice. It still parses as one Room is reserved by zero or many Bookings, but the voice switch is a small wobble.
- Room and Rate Plan both carry propertyId and Booking points at both. The board does not say whether a Booking's room and rate plan must belong to the same Property. A reader cannot tell if that rule exists.
- "Roamstay" is not explained, I assume it is the product name.

## 7 Could a product manager act on this board without asking anyone?

Yes for understanding which entities exist and how they join, because every entity, key and cardinality is written out in plain words, but they would need to ask what "In focus" means for Booking and whether a booking's room and rate plan must share a property before acting on any change.

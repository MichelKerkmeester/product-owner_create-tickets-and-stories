# Fresh reader notes: Start a return

## 1 Title and the question this board answers

The title is "Start a return". The subtitle reads "How a Fernhouse customer starts a return in the app, from the order list to a confirmed return". The question it answers: what steps and checks does a customer pass through in the app to go from their order list to a confirmed return?

## 2 What you see first, and what second

First the large bold title at top left and then the subtitle under it. Second my eye goes to the pink outlined green pill at the far right, "Confirmation with QR code", because the thick magenta border is the strongest mark on the board. After that I read left to right across the three tinted section frames.

## 3 The main path or structure from start to end

The board runs left to right in three labelled frames.

- Frame "Find the order" (light peach): blue rounded pill "Order list" (the start) with an arrow to the white rounded pill "Order detail".
- Frame "Request the return" (light teal): an arrow from Order detail into the yellow diamond "Within 30 days of delivery?". Its Yes exit goes to the white pill "Pick a reason", which leads to a second yellow diamond "Drop-off or pickup?".
- Frame "Done" (light teal or green): the green pill with a magenta border "Confirmation with QR code", which is the end of the flow.

So the happy path is Order list, Order detail, Within 30 days of delivery?, Pick a reason, Drop-off or pickup? and then Confirmation with QR code.

## 4 Every decision or branch and where each exit goes

- "Within 30 days of delivery?": the exit labelled "Yes: Start a return" goes right to "Pick a reason". The exit labelled "No" leaves from the bottom of the diamond and goes to the orange pill "Contact support", which sits below the main row inside the Request the return frame. Nothing leaves Contact support, so it is an end point.
- "Drop-off or pickup?": the exit labelled "Courier pickup" goes straight right to "Confirmation with QR code". The exit labelled "Drop-off point" leaves the top of the diamond, runs along above the frames and drops down into the top of the same "Confirmation with QR code" pill. Both exits end at the same place.

## 5 What the key says

The Key panel lists six entries:

- Blue rounded pill: Where it starts
- White rounded pill with a grey border: Screen or step
- Yellow diamond: Decision
- Green rounded pill: Goal reached
- Orange or peach rounded pill: Leaves the flow
- Green pill with a thick magenta border: In focus

Every node on the board matches a key entry. Marks not in the key: the connector lines (all thin grey solid arrows, so there is only one line style and it is not explained, though it reads naturally as "next step") and the tinted background frames. The frame colours are not explained. The "Find the order" frame is a light peach that is close to the orange "Leaves the flow" colour, which could suggest a meaning it does not have.

## 6 Anything I could not read, follow or found ambiguous

- The "Drop-off or pickup?" diamond sends both exits to the same confirmation pill, so on the board the choice changes nothing. The footer says screens for picking a drop-off point or booking a pickup slot were left out, which explains it, but on the canvas itself the branch looks pointless unless you read the small footer.
- The "Drop-off point" connector runs above the frames and crosses over the top edge of the "Done" frame, so for a moment it is unclear whether it belongs to the Request the return frame or the Done frame.
- The footer (small text at bottom left) notes a clash: a Fernhouse context dated 2026-09-18 says there is no self-serve return today, while the app and returns doc are said to govern here. This is important for whether the flow exists at all, yet it is in the smallest type on the board.
- It is not stated what "In focus" means in practice. Only the confirmation pill carries it, and the board does not say why that step is the focus.
- "Contact support" has no outcome after it, so I cannot tell what happens to a customer outside the 30 day window beyond reaching support.
- All text was readable at 100%.

## 7 Could a product manager act on this board without asking anyone

No, because although the path and the 30 day rule are clear, the footer clash about whether self-serve returns exist today and the missing drop-off and pickup screens leave open questions a product manager would need answered first.

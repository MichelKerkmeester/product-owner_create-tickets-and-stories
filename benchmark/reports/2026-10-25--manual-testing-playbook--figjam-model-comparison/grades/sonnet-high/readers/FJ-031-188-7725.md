# Reader notes: Start a return

## 1 Title and the question this board answers

The title is "Start a return". The subtitle reads "How a Fernhouse customer starts a return in the app, from the order list to a confirmed return". The question it answers is: what steps and choices does a customer go through in the app to get from their order list to a confirmed return?

## 2 What you see first, and what second

First I see the large bold title at the top left. Second my eye goes to the bright pink outlined green pill on the far right ("Return confirmed with QR code") because the pink border is the strongest colour on the board. After that I notice the blue starting pill on the left and the two yellow diamonds in the middle.

## 3 The main path or structure from start to end, naming the shapes

The board reads left to right across three tinted sections: "Find the order" (peach tint), "Request the return" (pale teal tint) and "Done" (pale teal tint).

- "Order list" is a blue rounded pill, the start.
- An arrow leads to "Order detail", a white rounded pill (a screen or step).
- An arrow labelled "Start a return" crosses into the second section and reaches a yellow diamond "Within 30 days of delivery?".
- The "Yes" exit leads to "Return reasons", a white rounded pill.
- An unlabelled arrow leads to a second yellow diamond "Drop-off or pickup?".
- Both exits of that diamond end at "Return confirmed with QR code", a green pill with a thick pink outline inside the "Done" section.

## 4 Every decision or branch and where each exit goes

- "Within 30 days of delivery?": "Yes" goes right to "Return reasons". "No" drops down and right to "Request an exception", an orange pill. Nothing leaves "Request an exception", so that branch ends there.
- "Drop-off or pickup?": "Courier pickup" goes straight right to "Return confirmed with QR code". "Drop-off point" leaves from the top of the diamond, runs along the top of the board and comes down into the top of the same "Return confirmed with QR code" pill. Both exits reach the same end state, so the board shows no difference in outcome between the two choices.

## 5 What the key says, and whether every mark is in the key

The key at the bottom lists six entries:

- Blue rounded pill: "Where it starts".
- White rounded pill with grey border: "Screen or step".
- Yellow diamond: "Decision".
- Green rounded pill: "Goal reached".
- Orange rounded pill: "Leaves the flow".
- Green pill with thick pink border: "In focus".

Every node on the board matches a key entry. A few marks are not in the key: the tinted section backgrounds (peach for "Find the order" and pale teal for the other two) have no entry and I cannot tell if the colour difference means anything. The connector lines are all one style (thin grey solid with arrowheads) and the key does not describe them, though their meaning is obvious. The text labels on connectors ("Start a return", "Yes", "No", "Drop-off point", "Courier pickup") are not keyed but read as plain labels. The footnote names sources and says "free pickup over 100 euros" was left out as a proposal under review.

## 6 Anything I could not read, could not follow or found ambiguous

- "In focus" on the final pill (far right) is not explained. The key says what the pink border means as a label but not why this node is in focus or what a reader should do with it.
- "Request an exception" (bottom middle) is a dead end. The board does not say what happens next, whether the exception can be granted, or whether it rejoins the flow.
- "Drop-off or pickup?" (middle right) sends both exits to the same end node. It is unclear whether the QR code or the confirmation differs between drop-off and courier pickup.
- The arrow from "Return reasons" to the second diamond has no label, so I cannot tell whether choosing reasons is required or can fail.
- The two section tints differ (peach versus teal) with no explanation, and "Done" uses the same teal as "Request the return".
- All text is legible at both zoom levels and I found nothing I could not read.

## 7 Could a product manager act on this board without asking anyone

Yes for the happy path, since the steps, the 30 day rule and the two return methods are clear, but they would need to ask what happens after "Request an exception" and why the final step is marked "In focus" before acting on those parts.

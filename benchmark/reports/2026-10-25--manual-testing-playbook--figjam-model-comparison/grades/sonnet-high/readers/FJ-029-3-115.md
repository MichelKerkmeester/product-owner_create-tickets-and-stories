# Fresh reader notes: Workspace setup board

## 1 Title and the question this board answers

The title is "2. Workspace setup". The subtitle reads "How a verified Loomlist user picks a plan and a workspace and reaches the editor, for the product and design teams". The question it answers is this: once a user has verified their email, what steps do they take to get into the editor, and how does the plan choice change that path?

## 2 What you see first, and what second

First I see the large bold title at the top left. Second my eye goes to the decision diamond "Free or team plan?" because it is the biggest shape and has a thick pink outline that makes it stand out from everything else. After that I read the row of steps left to right and then the key below.

## 3 The main path or structure from start to end

The flow runs in one horizontal row from left to right.

1. "Email verified" is a blue rounded rectangle. It is the start.
2. An arrow leads to "Free or team plan?" which is a yellow diamond with a pink outline. It is the decision.
3. On the Team exit the path goes to "Invite teammates by email, or skip" which is a white rounded rectangle.
4. Then "Name the workspace" which is a white rounded rectangle.
5. Then "Pick a template or start blank" which is a white rounded rectangle.
6. Then "Editor opens with welcome tip" which is a green rounded rectangle. It is the end goal.

## 4 Every decision or branch and where each exit goes

There is one decision: "Free or team plan?".

- The exit labelled "Team" leaves the right point of the diamond and goes to "Invite teammates by email, or skip".
- The exit labelled "Free" leaves the top of the diamond, runs along a line above the row and drops down into "Name the workspace". This skips the invite step.

Both exits rejoin at "Name the workspace" and from there the path is shared all the way to the editor. The invite step says "or skip" in its label but no separate skip arrow is drawn, so skipping just continues to the same next step.

## 5 What the key says and whether every mark is in it

The key, in a grey panel under the flow, lists five entries.

- Blue rounded rectangle means "Where it starts".
- White rounded rectangle means "Screen or step".
- Yellow diamond means "Decision".
- Green rounded rectangle means "Goal reached".
- Yellow diamond with a thick pink outline means "In focus".

Every shape and colour on the board appears in the key. The connectors are all the same thin grey solid arrow, and the key does not list a line style. Because there is only one line style this does not cause confusion. The small text labels "Team" and "Free" on the connectors are not in the key but they read clearly as branch names. A footer line says the source was the Loomlist onboarding doc and that team billing and what invited teammates see were left out.

## 6 Anything unreadable, hard to follow or ambiguous

- Everything is legible at both zoom levels.
- The "In focus" mark on the decision diamond is not explained beyond the key label. I cannot tell why this decision is in focus or what I am meant to do about it. This sits at the diamond, second shape from the left.
- The step "Invite teammates by email, or skip" folds a choice into a single box. It is a little unclear whether skipping is a real branch that behaves differently later. This sits in the third shape.
- The "2." in the title suggests this is one part of a larger series, but no other parts are visible, so I do not know what comes before or after.
- No error or drop-off paths are shown, such as a failed invite or leaving before the editor. The footer says some areas were left out on purpose but does not mention failure paths.

## 7 Could a product manager act on this board without asking anyone

Yes, because the single decision and both of its exits are labelled and the key covers every shape, though they might still ask why the decision is marked in focus and whether failure paths matter.

# Fresh reader notes: Change booking dates

## 1 Title and the question this board answers

The title is "Change booking dates". The subtitle says it shows how a Roamstay guest changes the dates of a booking in the app, from the booking page to the updated booking. The question it answers is: what happens step by step when a guest moves their booking to new dates, and how is the price difference handled?

## 2 What you see first, and what second

First I see the large bold title at top left. Second my eye goes to the pink outlined diamond "New price vs old?" on the right of centre, because its thick pink border is the loudest mark on the board. After that I read the single row left to right starting from the blue "Booking page" pill.

## 3 The main path or structure from start to end

The board is a left to right flowchart on one main row.

- "Booking page" (blue rounded pill, the start) leads by an arrow labelled "Change dates" to
- "Pick new dates" (white rounded box, a screen or step), which leads to
- "Room free?" (yellow diamond, a decision).
- On Yes the flow goes to "New price vs old?" (yellow diamond with a thick pink border, the decision in focus).
- From there one of three white steps follows: "Pay the difference" (top), "Difference returned as credit" (middle) or "Nothing charged" (bottom).
- All three join into "Updated booking" (green rounded pill, goal reached), the single end.

A side step "See other rooms" (white rounded box) sits between the two diamonds on the No branch.

## 4 Every decision or branch and where each exit goes

- "Room free?" has two exits. Yes leaves from the top of the diamond, runs right along a line above the row and drops into the left point of "New price vs old?". No leaves from the right point and goes to "See other rooms". From "See other rooms" a line labelled "Pick a room" leaves its bottom edge, runs right and joins the same vertical line that feeds "New price vs old?". So both exits of "Room free?" end at the price decision.
- "New price vs old?" has three exits. Higher goes up and right to "Pay the difference". Lower goes straight right to "Difference returned as credit". Same goes down and right to "Nothing charged". Each of those three steps then has one arrow into "Updated booking" (from above, from the left and from below).

There are no loops back and no failure or exit paths. The flow always ends at "Updated booking".

## 5 What the key says and whether every mark is in it

The key at the bottom lists five entries:

- Blue rounded pill: Where it starts
- White rounded box: Screen or step
- Yellow diamond: Decision
- Green rounded pill: Goal reached
- Yellow diamond with thick pink border: In focus

Every shape and colour on the board is covered by the key. The lines are not in the key, but every connector is the same thin grey solid line with a small arrowhead, so there is only one line style and nothing is left unexplained by its absence. The small edge labels (Change dates, Yes, No, Pick a room, Higher, Lower, Same) are plain text on the lines and read clearly without a key entry.

A footnote below the key says the source is the product owner's description of the date change flow treated as current behaviour. It states one assumption (after seeing other rooms the guest picks one) and says payment failure, change deadlines, fees and the host's side are left out.

## 6 Anything I could not read, could not follow or found ambiguous

- The key says pink means "In focus" but nothing on the board says why the price decision is the focus or what a reader should do about it. I could guess it is the part under discussion but that is inference.
- The Yes line and the "Pick a room" line merge into one vertical line just left of "New price vs old?" and share one arrowhead. It is readable but at first glance the Yes line looks like it might be heading somewhere other than the pink diamond, since it has no arrowhead of its own.
- "See other rooms" has only the assumed "Pick a room" exit. What happens if no other room suits the guest is not shown, and the footnote confirms this was assumed rather than described.
- "Difference returned as credit" does not say where the credit goes (wallet, original card or future stay).
- "Pay the difference" has no failure path, which the footnote names as left out.
- The edge labels and the footnote are small at overview size but fully legible at 100%. Nothing on the board was unreadable.

## 7 Could a product manager act on this board without asking anyone

Yes for understanding the happy path, because every step, decision exit and shape is labelled and keyed, but they would still need to ask why the price decision is marked in focus and how the left out cases such as payment failure and no suitable room should behave before turning it into work.

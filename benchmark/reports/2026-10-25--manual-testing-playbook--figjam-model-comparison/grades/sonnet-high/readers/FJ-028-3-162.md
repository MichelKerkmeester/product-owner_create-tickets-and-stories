# Fresh reader: FJ-028-3-162

## 1 Title and the question this board answers

The title is "Offline mode launch". The subtitle asks which work runs when before the offline mode release and what holds it. It is a timeline (Gantt style) board with weekly columns from 2 Nov to 21 Dec.

## 2 What you see first, and what second

First the large bold title. Second the one bar with a thick magenta outline and a pink fill in the Build lane, labelled "1 week, critical". After that the eye moves to the yellow diamond at the far right end of the Launch lane.

## 3 The main path or structure from start to end

Three coloured swim lanes run top to bottom: Design (light blue), Build (light orange) and Launch (light teal). Each lane holds rounded bars on a shared week grid.

- Design: "Conflict screen design" bar from 2 Nov to 16 Nov, labelled 2 weeks.
- Build: "Sync engine" bar from 2 Nov to 23 Nov, labelled 3 weeks. "Conflict screen build" bar from 16 Nov to 30 Nov, labelled 2 weeks. "Desktop and iOS integration" bar from 30 Nov to 7 Dec, labelled "1 week, critical", with the heavy magenta outline.
- Launch: "Beta for 5 percent of workspaces" bar from 7 Dec to 21 Dec, labelled 2 weeks. "Release" is a yellow diamond milestone at 21 Dec.

The path reads: design and sync engine start together, conflict screen build follows design, integration waits on both sync engine and conflict screen build, beta follows integration and release closes the chart.

## 4 Relationships and timing

There are no decisions or branches. The relationships are dependency arrows (thin grey lines with arrowheads):

- Conflict screen design to Conflict screen build. The line leaves the end of the design bar at 16 Nov, runs right to 23 Nov and drops down onto the build bar.
- Sync engine to Desktop and iOS integration. The line leaves the end of the sync bar at 23 Nov and drops onto the integration bar.
- Conflict screen build to Desktop and iOS integration. Joins the same arrowhead as the sync engine line.
- Desktop and iOS integration to Beta. The line leaves the end of integration at 7 Dec and drops onto the beta bar at 14 Dec.
- Beta to Release. The line leaves the beta bar near 14 Dec and runs right into the diamond at 21 Dec.

So integration is the one item that holds the release, as the only bar marked critical. Android is named in the footer as left out, planned for next quarter and not yet approved.

## 5 What the key says and whether every mark is in it

The key has four entries:

- White bar with grey outline: Planned.
- Pink fill with a coral or orange outline: "Critical, heavy outline".
- Yellow diamond: Milestone.
- Pink fill with a heavy magenta outline: In focus.

Not every mark matches the key. The only critical bar on the board ("1 week, critical") wears the magenta "In focus" style and not the coral "Critical" style, so the Critical key entry is used by nothing on the board. The grey dependency arrows are not in the key. The three lane background colours are not in the key, though the lane titles name them. The duration text inside bars is self explaining.

## 6 Anything unreadable, hard to follow or ambiguous

- Critical versus in focus, in the Build lane at 30 Nov to 7 Dec: the bar text says critical but its outline matches In focus. I cannot tell whether it is critical, in focus or both, and the coral Critical style appears nowhere.
- Arrow landing points: arrowheads land in the middle of the target bars and not at their start. The design arrow lands on Conflict screen build at 23 Nov although that bar starts 16 Nov. The integration arrow lands on Beta at 14 Dec although beta starts 7 Dec. The Beta to Release line starts from the middle of the beta bar (around 14 Dec) and not its end. This blurs whether work truly waits for the predecessor to finish.
- Overlap at 23 Nov in the Build lane: the vertical drop of the design arrow passes right at the end of the Sync engine bar, so it can read as Sync engine feeding Conflict screen build.
- Gap in timing: Sync engine ends 23 Nov but integration does not start until 30 Nov. The board does not say why the week of slack exists or whether conflict screen build alone sets the start.
- Critical path: only integration is marked critical, though design, conflict screen build, beta and release all sit in one unbroken chain to the release date. It is unclear why the rest of that chain is not critical.
- No owners, no status (done, in progress) and no "today" marker are shown.

## 7 Could a product manager act on this board without asking anyone

Mostly yes for reading the order and dates to a 21 Dec release, but no for acting on risk, because the critical versus in focus styling conflicts with the key and the mid-bar arrow landings leave real doubt about what the critical path is.

## 1. Title and the question this board answers

"Damaged-item refund". The subtitle says it answers: who handles a damaged-item claim, from the customer's report to the released refund. So it is an ownership question across three lanes.

## 2. Main path or structure

The board is three horizontal swimlanes (Customer, CS agent, Warehouse operator) plus a Key and a source footnote. The path starts at "Damage reported" (blue pill, Customer lane). It goes right, then down into the diamond "Damage clear in the photo?" (CS agent lane). On "Clear" it leaves the bottom of the diamond, runs along the bottom edge of the CS agent lane and up into "Approve the claim" (pink, thick outline). From there one line goes right, labelled "Logs", into the "Claims table" cylinder. Another line goes down, labelled "Claim queued", into "Book a courier" (Warehouse operator lane). Then "Book a courier" leads to "Inspect the item", which leads to "Refund released" (green pill, the end).

## 3. Decisions and branches

There is one decision, "Damage clear in the photo?". Its "Clear" exit goes to "Approve the claim". Its "Unclear" exit goes up to "Send the second photo", which comes back down and across into the diamond's right side. So the unclear branch is a loop back to the same question. There is no stated limit on how many times it can loop, and no exit to a rejection. "Approve the claim" has two outgoing lines (to "Claims table" and to "Book a courier"), which is a split, not a decision. "Inspect the item" has one exit only, with no pass or fail.

## 4. What the key says

The Key lists seven marks:

- Blue rounded pill: where it starts.
- White rounded pill with grey outline: step.
- Teal parallelogram: input or output.
- Yellow diamond: decision.
- Green rounded pill with green outline: goal reached.
- Purple cylinder: data store.
- Thin arrow: leads to.
- Pink pill with thick pink outline: in focus.

Every node on the board matches one of these. The "Approve the claim" pink pill is not a step in the key's terms; the key only says "in focus", so its role as a step is implied. Not in the key: the lane colours (peach, mint, cream) and the lane titles, the text labels on lines ("Unclear", "Clear", "Logs", "Claim queued"), and the footnote. All lines are one solid style, so there is no dashed or other line meaning. The "In focus" mark is in the key but the board does not say what it is in focus for.

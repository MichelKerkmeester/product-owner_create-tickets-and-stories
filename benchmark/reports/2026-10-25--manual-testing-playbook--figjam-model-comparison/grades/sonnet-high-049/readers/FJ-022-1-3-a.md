## 1. Title and the question this board answers

The title is "Start a return" and the subtitle reads "How a customer starts a return in the app, from the order list to the QR code confirmation". The board answers: what does a customer go through in the app to start a return, and who can do it at all? It covers one flow, from opening the order list to getting a QR code, plus the one case that falls out (too late to return in the app).

## 2. The main path or structure from start to end

The board has two zones. "Check the order" (peach) holds "Order list", "Order detail" and the diamond "Within 30 days of delivery?". "Request the return" (mint) holds "Start a return", "Pick a reason", the diamond "Return method?", "Confirmation with QR code" and "Contact support". The main path runs left to right: "Order list" (blue, start) to "Order detail" to "Within 30 days of delivery?", then "Yes" to "Start a return" to "Pick a reason" to "Return method?", and from there to "Confirmation with QR code" (green, goal).

## 3. Every decision or branch and where each exit goes

- "Within 30 days of delivery?": "Yes" goes right to "Start a return". "No" goes down and right along a bent line to "Contact support".
- "Return method?": two exits. "Courier pickup" goes straight right to "Confirmation with QR code". "Drop-off point" leaves from the top of the diamond, runs along the top and drops down into the top of "Confirmation with QR code". Both reach the same end shape.
- No other branches. "Contact support" has no outgoing arrow.

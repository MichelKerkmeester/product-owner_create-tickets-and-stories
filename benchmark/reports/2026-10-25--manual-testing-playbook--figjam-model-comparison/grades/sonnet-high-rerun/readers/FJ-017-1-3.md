# Fresh reader: FJ-017-1-3

## 1 Title and the question this board answers

The title is "Booking status". The subtitle says the board shows every status a Roamstay booking passes through, what moves it on and who sets Cancelled and No-show. So the question is: what states can a booking be in, what event moves it between them and who is responsible for the two unhappy endings.

## 2 What you see first, and what second

First I see the large pale blue "Confirmed" container across the top with its row of states inside. Second my eye goes to the Cancelled state, because it is the only shape with a thick pink outline and it sits bottom right. After that I notice the light blue Pending pill on the left and then the column of grey final states on the right.

## 3 The main path or structure from start to end

This is a state diagram. A start dot on the left leads into Pending (a light blue rounded pill). From Pending a line labelled "card authorised" runs up and left and enters the Confirmed container. Confirmed is a composite state holding its own start dot, then Upcoming (white pill), then a "check in" arrow to In stay (white pill), then an arrow to an inner end dot at the right of the container. The happy path leaves the top of Confirmed on a line labelled "check out [from In stay]" and lands on Completed (grey pill), which then leads to an end dot.

## 4 Every decision or branch and where each exit goes

Pending has two exits. "card authorised" goes to Confirmed. "guest cancels" runs down and right along the bottom to Cancelled.

Confirmed has three exits, all drawn from the container edge rather than from a single inner state. "check out [from In stay]" leaves the top edge and goes to Completed. "missed check-in [from Upcoming]" leaves the right edge and goes down to No-show. "guest cancels" leaves the bottom edge and goes to Cancelled from above.

Each of the three final states (Completed, No-show and Cancelled) has its own arrow to an end dot on the far right. Two sticky notes explain responsibility: "Set by the hotel, after a missed check-in" (meant for No-show) and "Set by the guest, from Pending or Confirmed" (meant for Cancelled).

## 5 What the key says and whether every mark is in it

The key lists: black dot is Start. Light blue pill with blue outline is First state. White pill with grey outline is State. Grey pill with grey outline is Final state. Black dot with a grey ring is End. Solid grey line with an open arrowhead is Leads to. Grey pill with a thick pink outline is "In focus: guest sets it".

Not in the key: the pale blue Confirmed container (a composite or grouping state) and the two blue sticky notes. The text in square brackets on edge labels, such as "[from In stay]", is also not explained, though it reads as a guard naming the inner source state. The inner end dot inside Confirmed matches the End mark but its meaning there (Confirmed is finished) is not spelled out.

## 6 Anything unreadable, hard to follow or ambiguous

The "Set by the hotel, after a missed check-in" sticky sits in the middle of the board, left of No-show and just above the two "guest cancels" lines. It is not attached to No-show and sits closer to the Cancelled lines, so a reader could pair it with the wrong state.

The "Set by the guest" sticky floats outside the frame to the right of Cancelled, overlapping nothing but also outside the key area, so it looks like a loose comment.

The guest cancels line from Confirmed leaves the bottom of the whole container, which suggests a guest can cancel during In stay as well as Upcoming. The sticky says "from Pending or Confirmed", which agrees, but it is unclear whether cancelling mid stay is really intended.

In stay has an arrow to the inner end dot, and separately the "check out" line leaves the container top edge with a "[from In stay]" guard. Both seem to describe the same move, so it is unclear whether the inner end dot is check out or something else.

On the right the "missed check-in" line and the "check out" line run as two close parallel verticals near the container corner, and the missed check-in line crosses over the check out route. Tracing which one reaches Completed and which reaches No-show takes care.

The board does not say who triggers check in and check out, only who sets Cancelled and No-show. The footer says the source was only the request with no bookings doc and that payment failures, refunds and booking changes are left out.

## 7 Could a product manager act on this board without asking anyone

Mostly yes, because the states, triggers and owners of Cancelled and No-show are all labelled, but they would likely confirm whether a guest may cancel during In stay and that the loose hotel sticky belongs to No-show.

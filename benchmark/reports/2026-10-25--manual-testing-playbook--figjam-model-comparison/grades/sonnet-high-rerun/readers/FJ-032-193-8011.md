# Reader notes: FJ-032-193-8011

## 1 Title and the question this board answers

The title is "Roamstay booking status lifecycle". The subtitle says it shows every status a Roamstay booking passes through and what moves it on, for the product and support team. The question it answers is: what statuses can a booking be in, and which event moves it from one status to the next?

## 2 What you see first, and what second

First I see the large bold title and then the wide pale blue box labelled "Confirmed" across the top, with Upcoming and In stay inside it. Second I see the blue Pending pill on the left and the three grey pills stacked on the right (Completed, No-show and Cancelled), each followed by a ringed black dot. The key strip at the bottom and the source note under it come last.

## 3 The main path or structure from start to end

A black start dot leads to the blue Pending pill. From Pending a line labelled "card authorised" runs up and left and enters the Confirmed box on its left edge. Inside Confirmed a second start dot leads to the white Upcoming pill. Upcoming leads to the white In stay pill through "check in". In stay leads to a ringed end dot inside the right side of the Confirmed box. A line labelled "check out [from InStay]" leaves the top edge of the Confirmed box and comes down the right side into the grey Completed pill. Completed leads to a ringed end dot to its right. So the happy path reads Pending, Confirmed (Upcoming then In stay), Completed, end.

## 4 Every decision or branch and where each exit goes

This is a state diagram, so the branches are the transitions out of each status.

- Pending has two exits. "card authorised" goes into Confirmed (where it starts at Upcoming). "guest cancels" runs down and right along the bottom to Cancelled.
- Upcoming has two exits. "check in" goes to In stay. "missed check-in [from Upcoming]" leaves the right edge of the Confirmed box and goes down to No-show.
- In stay has one drawn exit inside the box to the inner end dot. The "check out [from InStay]" line leaves the Confirmed box top and goes to Completed.
- The Confirmed box as a whole has a "guest cancels" line leaving its bottom edge (below In stay) and going to Cancelled.
- Completed, No-show and Cancelled each lead only to their own end dot. There are no exits back from any of them.

## 5 What the key says, and whether every mark is in the key

The key lists six marks. A solid black dot is Start. A light blue pill with a blue outline is First state. A white pill with a grey outline is State. A grey filled pill with a thicker grey outline is Final state. A black dot with a grey ring is End. A thin arrow is Leads to.

Not every mark is in the key. The large pale blue Confirmed container (a status that holds other statuses) has no key entry. The bracketed text on lines such as "[from InStay]" and "[from Upcoming]" is not explained. The plain text labels on the arrows (the triggering events) are not explained as such either, though they read naturally. Every other mark I found matches a key entry.

## 6 Anything I could not read, could not follow or found ambiguous

- Top right, the "check out [from InStay]" line leaves the top edge of the Confirmed box above the gap between Upcoming and In stay, not from In stay itself. The bracket says it only applies from In stay, but the drawing makes it look like it could fire from any state inside Confirmed. The spelling "InStay" also differs from the pill label "In stay".
- Right side, the "missed check-in [from Upcoming]" line leaves the right edge of the Confirmed box beside the inner end dot, far from Upcoming. I had to rely on the bracket text to know it starts at Upcoming.
- Centre, the "guest cancels" line leaves the bottom of the Confirmed box directly under In stay. I cannot tell whether a guest can cancel while In stay or only while Upcoming.
- Inside Confirmed, the arrow from In stay to the inner end dot has no label. I cannot tell what event ends the Confirmed stage or how it relates to the separate "check out" line.
- Right of Completed, its end arrow leaves the bottom of the pill and turns up into the end dot, which is a small detour but still readable.
- There is no path for a card that is not authorised, so I cannot tell what happens to a Pending booking that never gets approved. The source note says refunds, payment steps and when No-show is marked are left out on purpose.

All text on the board was legible at both sizes.

## 7 Could a product manager act on this board without asking anyone?

Mostly yes for the overall lifecycle and the five end statuses, but no for implementation, because they would need to ask whether a guest can cancel during In stay, what ends the Confirmed stage and what happens when card authorisation fails.

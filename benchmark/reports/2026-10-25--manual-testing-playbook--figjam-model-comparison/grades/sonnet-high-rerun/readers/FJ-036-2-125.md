# Fresh reader notes: Change booking dates

## 1 Title and the question this board answers

The title is "Change booking dates". The subtitle says it shows how a Roamstay guest changes the dates of a booking in the app, from the booking page to the updated booking. The question it answers is: what steps and choices does a guest go through to move a booking to new dates, and how is the price settled?

## 2 What you see first, and what second

First I see the large bold title at top left and the bright pink outlined diamond "New price?" in the right half, which is the loudest mark on the board. Second I see the two tinted lanes, an orange one labelled "Find a room" on the left and a teal one labelled "Settle the price" on the right, and the green end box "Updated booking shown" at the far right.

## 3 The main path from start to end

The flow reads left to right. It starts at the blue rounded rectangle "Booking page". An arrow labelled "Taps Change dates" leads to the white rounded rectangle "Pick new dates". An arrow labelled "Dates picked" leads to the yellow diamond "Room free?". On the Yes exit a line leaves the top of that diamond, runs across the gap between the lanes and enters the pink outlined yellow diamond "New price?" in the "Settle the price" lane. From there one of three white rounded rectangles follows ("Pay the difference", "Difference added as credit" or "Nothing charged") and all three lead to the green rounded rectangle "Updated booking shown", which is the end.

## 4 Every decision and where each exit goes

"Room free?" has two exits. Yes goes straight to "New price?". No goes to the white box "See other rooms", and from there an arrow labelled "Picks a room" also goes to "New price?".

"New price?" has three exits. Higher goes to "Pay the difference". Lower goes to "Difference added as credit". Same goes to "Nothing charged". Each of those three boxes then leads to "Updated booking shown" (the top one enters it from above, the middle one from the left and the bottom one from below).

## 5 What the key says

The key at the bottom lists six marks. A blue rounded rectangle means where it starts. A white rounded rectangle with a grey outline means a screen or step. A yellow diamond means a decision. A green rounded rectangle with a green outline means goal reached. A thin solid arrow means leads to. A yellow diamond with a thick pink outline means in focus, the only three-way choice.

Every shape and line on the board matches a key entry. All connectors are the same thin solid grey arrow, so there is only one line style. The two tinted lane backgrounds (orange for "Find a room" and teal for "Settle the price") are not in the key, though their titles explain them well enough. Below the key a footer says the source is the product brief taken as current behavior and that cancelling, a failed card payment and host approval are left out.

## 6 Anything unreadable, hard to follow or ambiguous

All text is readable at 100 percent. A few points are unclear.

The "Picks a room" path from "See other rooms" skips any check that the newly picked room is free for the new dates. It also gives no exit if the guest finds no room they like, so the No branch has no dead end or way back to "Pick new dates".

The Yes line leaves "Room free?" from the top and runs a long way round before entering "New price?", while the "Picks a room" line enters the same diamond just below it at the lower left edge. The two arrows land very close together, which takes a moment to separate.

"Pay the difference" has no failure path shown. The footer says a failed card payment is left out on purpose, so this is a stated gap rather than a mistake, but the reader cannot tell what happens then.

The "Higher", "Lower" and "Same" labels do not say compared with what. I read them as the new price compared with the original booking price, which is the natural reading but is not written down.

## 7 Could a product manager act on this board without asking anyone?

Yes for the happy path and the three price outcomes, because every step, decision and exit is labelled and keyed, but they would still need to ask what happens when no other room suits the guest and whether a picked alternative room is checked for availability.

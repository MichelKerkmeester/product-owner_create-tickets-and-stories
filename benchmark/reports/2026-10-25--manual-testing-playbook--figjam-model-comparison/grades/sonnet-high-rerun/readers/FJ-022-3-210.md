# Reader notes: Start a return

## 1 Title and the question this board answers

The title is "Start a return". The subtitle reads "How a Fernhouse customer starts a return in the app, from the order list to a confirmed return". The board answers the question: what steps and checks does a customer go through in the app to get from an order to a confirmed return?

## 2 What you see first, and what second

First I see the large bold title at top left and the row of three tinted stage panels running left to right. Inside them the eye lands first on the thick pink outlined green node at the far right, "Confirmation with QR code", because it is the loudest mark on the board. Second I see the two yellow diamonds in the middle panel and the blue "Order list" start node on the left, which together show this is a flow with two decisions.

## 3 The main path from start to end

The flow runs left to right across three labelled stages.

Stage "Find the order" (peach panel): the blue rounded rectangle "Order list" leads to the white rounded rectangle "Order detail (Start a return)".

Stage "Request the return" (mint panel): Order detail leads to the yellow diamond "Within 30 days of delivery?". On Yes it leads to the white rounded rectangle "Pick a reason", which leads to the second yellow diamond "Drop-off or pickup?".

Stage "Done" (cream panel): both exits of the second diamond lead to the green rounded rectangle "Confirmation with QR code", which has a thick pink outline. This is where the flow ends.

## 4 Every decision and where each exit goes

Decision 1, "Within 30 days of delivery?":
- Yes exits right to "Pick a reason".
- No exits from the bottom of the diamond, turns right and goes to "Contact support", an orange rounded rectangle with a dashed outline. Contact support has no outgoing line, so the flow stops there.

Decision 2, "Drop-off or pickup?":
- "Courier pickup" exits right straight to "Confirmation with QR code".
- "Drop-off point" exits from the top of the diamond, runs along above the panels and drops down into the top of "Confirmation with QR code".

Both options end at the same confirmation node. The board does not show any step that differs between drop-off and pickup before confirmation.

## 5 What the key says, and whether every mark is in it

The key at the bottom lists:
- Blue filled rounded rectangle: Where it starts.
- White rounded rectangle with grey outline: Screen or step.
- Yellow diamond: Decision.
- Green filled rounded rectangle with green outline: Goal reached.
- Orange filled rounded rectangle with dashed orange outline: Leaves the flow.
- Thin grey arrow: Leads to.
- Thick pink outline around a green shape: In focus.

Almost every mark is covered. The node shapes, the arrows and the pink focus outline all match key entries. Marks not in the key are the three tinted background panels (peach, mint and cream), though each carries its own stage heading so they read as stage groupings without needing a key entry. The text labels on lines (Yes, No, Drop-off point, Courier pickup) are also not in the key, but they read naturally as branch labels. The confirmation node shows a pink outline instead of the green outline shown for "Goal reached" in the key, so a reader has to combine two key entries to read it, which works but is worth noticing.

## 6 Anything unreadable, hard to follow or ambiguous

- Everything is legible at 100 percent. I found no text I could not read.
- "In focus" is not explained. The key says the pink outline means "In focus" but the board never says why the confirmation step is the focus, for example whether it is the screen being designed or changed.
- "Contact support" (bottom middle of the Request the return panel) is a dead end. It is unclear whether the customer can still return the item after contacting support or whether the return attempt simply ends.
- "Confirmation with QR code" (Done panel, right) is shared by both the drop-off and courier pickup exits. It is unclear whether a courier pickup also produces a QR code, or what the QR code is used for in the pickup case.
- "Order detail (Start a return)" (Find the order panel) implies a "Start a return" button on the order detail screen, but the board does not say so directly.
- "Pick a reason" does not list the reasons or say whether any reason changes the outcome.
- The footnote at the bottom says "Sources: the app and the returns doc, as described in the request. Left out: free pickup for orders over €100, a proposal under review." So courier pickup cost rules are deliberately missing.
- The "Drop-off point" line leaves the top of the decision diamond and runs above the panel headings. It is easy to follow but it crosses the gap between the mint and cream panels above the "Done" heading.

## 7 Could a product manager act on this board without asking anyone?

Yes for understanding the current happy path and the 30 day rule, because every step, decision and exit is labelled and keyed, but they would still need to ask what "In focus" is meant to prompt and what happens after "Contact support" before acting on those parts.

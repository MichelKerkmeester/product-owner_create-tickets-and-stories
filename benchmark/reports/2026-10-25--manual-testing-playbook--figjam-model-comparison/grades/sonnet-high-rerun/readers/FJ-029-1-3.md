# Fresh reader: FJ-029-1-3

## 1 Title and the question this board answers

The title is "Loomlist signup and workspace setup". The subtitle says it shows how a new person goes from the landing page to the first editor session, with the code, plan, skip and blank choices. The question it answers is: what steps and choices does a new user pass through from first landing to working in the editor?

## 2 What you see first, and what second

First the large bold title at top left. Second the two tinted section frames side by side: a peach frame on the left called "Create the account" and a pale teal frame on the right called "Set up the workspace". Inside those the eye lands on the blue start pill at far left and the green pill with a bright pink outline at far right, which pull attention to the two ends of the flow. The yellow diamond is the next thing that stands out.

## 3 The main path or structure from start to end

The flow runs left to right in one row.

In "Create the account": a blue rounded pill "Landing page" leads to a white rounded pill "Sign up (email or Google)" which leads to a white rounded pill "Enter email code".

An arrow labelled "Right code" crosses the gap between the frames into "Set up the workspace" and reaches a yellow diamond "Free or team plan?".

From there the Team path goes to a white pill "Invite teammates" then by an arrow labelled "Send invites or skip" to a white pill "Name the workspace". Then a plain arrow to a white pill "Pick a template or blank" and a plain arrow to the end, a green pill "Editor with welcome tip" with a thick pink outline.

## 4 Every decision or branch and where each exit goes

- "Free or team plan?" (yellow diamond) has two exits. "Team" goes right to "Invite teammates". "Free" leaves the top of the diamond, runs along a line above the row and drops down into "Name the workspace", skipping the invite step.
- "Enter email code" is an implied check. Its labelled exit "Right code" goes forward to the plan decision. The wrong-code case is not drawn as an exit. It appears only as a text note "Wrong code: error with resend" sitting just above the node.
- "Send invites or skip" is a label on one arrow, so both choices take the same route to "Name the workspace".
- "Sign up (email or Google)" and "Pick a template or blank" name choices inside the step text but have one outgoing arrow each, so the choice does not change the path.

The footnote says the wrong-code error was folded into a label and that a separate Google path and anything after the welcome tip were left out on purpose.

## 5 What the key says, and whether every mark is in the key

The key at the bottom lists six items:

- Blue rounded pill: Where it starts.
- White rounded pill with grey outline: Screen or step.
- Yellow diamond: Decision.
- Green rounded pill with green outline: Goal reached.
- Grey solid arrow: Leads to.
- Green pill with thick pink outline: In focus.

Every shape on the board matches a key entry. Marks not in the key: the two tinted section frames (peach and teal) are not explained, though their titles make them clear. Arrow labels such as "Right code", "Team", "Free" and "Send invites or skip" are not explained, though they read as the condition for that arrow. The floating "Wrong code" text note has no key entry. One small point is that the goal node is drawn with the pink "In focus" outline rather than the plain green outline shown for "Goal reached", so the reader has to combine the two entries to understand it. Only one line style (solid grey arrow) is used, which matches the key.

## 6 Anything unreadable, hard to follow or ambiguous

- "Wrong code: error with resend" above "Enter email code" is small and sits tight against the top of the node. At overview zoom it looks as if the node outline strikes through it, which made it read like crossed-out text until I zoomed in. It is also a free label with no arrow, so it is unclear where the user goes after the error (back to the same screen is the natural guess).
- The "Free" connector leaves from the top point of the diamond and joins a long line across the top of the frame, so at first glance it is not obvious which end it starts from. The arrowhead into "Name the workspace" settles it.
- "Sign up (email or Google)" mentions Google but the footnote says the Google path was left out, so it is unclear whether a Google signup also goes through "Enter email code".
- "In focus" is not explained: why the final step is in focus is not stated.
- The footnote is small grey text at the bottom left and easy to miss.

## 7 Could a product manager act on this board without asking anyone

Yes, mostly, because the main path, the free versus team branch and the optional invite step are all clear and keyed, though they would need to ask what happens after a wrong code and whether Google signups skip the email code step.

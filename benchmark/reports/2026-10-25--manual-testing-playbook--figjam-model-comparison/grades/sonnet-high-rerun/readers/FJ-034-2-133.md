# Reader notes for board FJ-034-2-133

## 1 Title and the question this board answers

The title is "Fernhouse damaged-item refund". The subtitle reads "Who does what from a damage report to a released refund, for new CS and warehouse staff". So the board answers who does each step between a customer reporting a damaged item and the refund being released, split across three roles.

## 2 What you see first, and what second

First I see the large bold title and then the pink-outlined yellow diamond "Photo approved?" in the middle of the CS lane, which is the strongest mark on the board. Second I see the three coloured horizontal lanes (Customer in pale orange, CS in pale cyan, Warehouse in pale yellow) and the green rounded box "Refund released" at the far right of the Warehouse lane.

## 3 The main path or structure from start to end

The board is a swimlane flowchart with three lanes stacked top to bottom: Customer, CS and Warehouse. The path starts in the Customer lane at the blue rounded box "Report damage with a photo". An arrow runs right and down into the CS lane and enters the left point of the yellow diamond "Photo approved?". From the bottom point of the diamond an arrow runs down into the Warehouse lane and right, labelled "Approved", to the white rounded box "Book a pickup". From there arrows run right to the white rounded box "Inspect the item" and then to the green rounded box "Refund released", which is the end.

## 4 Every decision or branch and where each exit goes

There is one decision, "Photo approved?", in the CS lane.

- The bottom exit is labelled "Approved" and goes to "Book a pickup" in the Warehouse lane, then on through "Inspect the item" to "Refund released".
- The right exit is labelled "Second photo" and goes to the white box "Ask for a second photo" in the CS lane. From there an arrow runs right and up into the Customer lane to the white box "Send a second photo". From that box an arrow runs left along the top of the Customer lane and down into the top point of the diamond, so the flow loops back to the same decision.

No other step branches. "Book a pickup" and "Inspect the item" each have a single exit.

## 5 What the key says and whether every mark is in it

The key at the bottom lists six entries.

- Blue rounded box with a blue outline: Where it starts.
- White rounded box with a grey outline: Step.
- Yellow diamond with a thin yellow outline: Decision.
- Green rounded box with a thicker green outline: Goal reached.
- Thin grey solid line with an open arrowhead: Leads to.
- Yellow diamond with a thick pink outline: In focus.

Every node and connector on the board matches a key entry. All lines are solid with no dashed or dotted style. The lane background colours (orange, cyan and yellow) are not in the key, but each lane carries its own text label so they read without one. The small edge labels "Approved" and "Second photo" are text, not keyed marks. A footnote under the key reads "Source: the request description. Left out: claim rejection, failed inspection and a limit on second photos, none of them stated".

## 6 Anything unreadable, hard to follow or ambiguous

- Everything is legible at both zoom levels. Both images show the same board with nothing cut off.
- The right exit of the diamond is labelled "Second photo" rather than a plain answer to "Photo approved?" such as No or Not yet. It reads as an action rather than an outcome, so I had to infer that it means the photo was not good enough.
- The "In focus" pink outline on the diamond is explained in the key only as "In focus". The board does not say why this decision is singled out or what a reader should do about it.
- The second-photo loop has no exit other than going back to the decision. The footnote confirms there is no limit and no rejection path, so a claim that never gets approved has nowhere to end.
- "Inspect the item" has one exit straight to "Refund released". What happens on a failed inspection is not shown, which the footnote also admits.
- "Refund released" sits in the Warehouse lane. It is unclear whether the warehouse really releases money or whether CS or finance does that and it was placed there for layout.
- The arrow from "Send a second photo" back to the diamond runs a long way left across the Customer lane and passes close to the line from "Report damage with a photo" before dropping into the diamond top. It is followable but the two entries into the diamond (left side and top) could be mistaken for different meanings.

## 7 Could a product manager act on this board without asking anyone

No, because the happy path and the second-photo loop are clear but the board itself states that claim rejection, failed inspection and any limit on second photos are missing while it leaves open who releases the refund, so a product manager would need to ask about those before acting.

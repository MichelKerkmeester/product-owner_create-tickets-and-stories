# Fresh reader notes: FJ-026-1-3

## 1 Title and the question this board answers

The title is "Booking status". The subtitle reads "Every status a Roamstay booking passes through, and what moves it on". The board answers the question: what statuses can a booking be in, and which event moves it from one status to the next? It is a state diagram, not a step flow.

## 2 What you see first, and what second

First the large bold title at top left, then the light blue rounded "Pending" box on the left, which is the only coloured state. Second the big pale blue container labelled "Confirmed" across the top right, holding two white states. After that the eye drops to the column of three grey boxes (Completed, No-show and Cancelled), each with a black end dot to its right. The key sits in a grey panel along the bottom with a one line source note under it.

## 3 The main path or structure from start to end

A black start dot on the far left points to Pending (light blue rounded rectangle, the first state). From Pending a line labelled "card authorised" runs up and across to the top edge of the Confirmed container. Inside Confirmed there is its own black start dot that points to Upcoming (white rounded rectangle). Upcoming goes to In stay (white rounded rectangle) on "check in". In stay points to an end dot inside the Confirmed container. Below the container a line labelled "check out [from InStay]" leads down to Completed (grey rounded rectangle with a thicker grey outline, a final state), which points to an end dot. So the happy path is start, Pending, Confirmed (Upcoming then In stay), Completed, end.

## 4 Every transition and where each one goes

- Start dot to Pending, unlabelled.
- Pending to Confirmed on "card authorised". The arrow lands on the container, and the inner start dot then puts the booking in Upcoming.
- Upcoming to In stay on "check in", inside Confirmed.
- In stay to the inner end dot of Confirmed, unlabelled.
- Confirmed to Completed on "check out [from InStay]". The line leaves the bottom edge of the container, and the bracket says it only applies from In stay.
- Confirmed to No-show on "missed check-in [from Upcoming]". The line leaves the left edge of the container, and the bracket says it only applies from Upcoming.
- Confirmed to Cancelled on "guest cancels". The line leaves the right edge of the container and loops down to the top of Cancelled. No bracket is given, so as drawn it applies from any status inside Confirmed.
- Pending to Cancelled on "guest cancels". The line drops from the bottom of Pending and runs right into Cancelled.
- Completed, No-show and Cancelled each point to their own end dot, unlabelled.

There is no exit from Pending other than card authorised and guest cancels, so nothing shows what happens if the card is declined or the booking is never paid.

## 5 What the key says, and whether every mark is in it

The key lists: solid black dot is Start. Light blue rounded rectangle with a blue outline is First state. White rounded rectangle with a thin grey outline is State. Grey filled rounded rectangle with a thicker grey outline is Final state. Black dot inside a grey ring is End. A plain grey line with an open arrowhead is Leads to.

Not every mark is in the key. The large pale blue container with a blue outline that groups Upcoming and In stay (the Confirmed box) has no key entry, and nothing explains that an arrow into the container means entering at its inner start dot. The inner start and end dots inside Confirmed reuse the Start and End symbols, but the key does not say they mean the start and end of a sub status rather than of the whole booking. The square bracket notation such as "[from Upcoming]" on a label is also not explained in the key. All lines are the same solid style, so there is no line style the key misses.

## 6 What I could not read, follow or found ambiguous

- The inner end dot after In stay (top right inside Confirmed). It is unclear what reaching it means, since leaving In stay to Completed is already drawn as the "check out" line from the container. It reads as a duplicate or a second unexplained exit.
- The "check out [from InStay]" label below the container spells the state "InStay" while the box says "In stay". Small, but it reads as a different name.
- The "missed check-in" and "check out" lines start at the container edges rather than at Upcoming and In stay themselves, so the reader has to trust the bracket text to know which sub status they leave from.
- "guest cancels" from the right edge of Confirmed has no bracket, so it is ambiguous whether a guest can cancel while already In stay.
- The footnote at the bottom says the timing of the No-show mark is left out because the source brief does not give it, so when a missed check-in turns into No-show is unknown.
- No path for a failed card authorisation out of Pending.
- Everything else was legible at 100 percent zoom.

## 7 Could a product manager act on this board without asking anyone

Mostly yes for the core lifecycle, because every status and trigger is labelled and the key covers the shapes, but no for edge cases, since they would still need to ask whether cancelling is allowed during In stay, what the inner end dot means, what happens on a failed card and when No-show is set.

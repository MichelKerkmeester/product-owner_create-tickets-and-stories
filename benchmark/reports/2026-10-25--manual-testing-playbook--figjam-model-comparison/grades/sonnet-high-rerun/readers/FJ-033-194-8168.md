## 1 Title and the question this board answers

The title is "Block sync 2". The subtitle asks how a block change reaches every Loomlist app, including a sleeping iPhone. The board is an architecture view of where a change goes after an app sends it. The "2" in the title is not explained anywhere on the board.

First I see the large bold title and then the thick pink outline around Sync Platform in the middle of the row. It is the heaviest mark on the board. Second I see the left to right row of tinted group frames: Clients, Edge, Services, Stores and External. The Key panel along the bottom comes after that.

## 3 The main path or structure from start to end

The board reads left to right. The Clients frame holds three teal rounded rectangles: Desktop App, Web App and iOS App. Each one has a solid arrow labelled "Sends changes" into Entry point, a light blue rounded rectangle in the Edge frame. A solid arrow labelled "Routes changes" goes from Entry point to Sync Platform, a white rounded rectangle with a pink outline in the Services frame. A solid arrow labelled "Stores every change" goes from Sync Platform to Change Store, a purple cylinder in the Stores frame. A dashed arrow labelled "Apple Push: Wakes iPhones" leaves the bottom of Sync Platform and ends at Apple Push, a grey rounded rectangle with a dashed outline in the External frame. The path ends at Change Store and at Apple Push.

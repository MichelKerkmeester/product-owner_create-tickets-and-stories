## 1 Title and the question this board answers

The title is "Damaged-item refund". It answers who handles a damaged-item claim at each step, from the moment the customer reports damage to the refund being released.

## 2 Main path from start to end

The customer starts at "Damage reported" (blue pill). That leads to the CS agent decision "Damage clear in the photo?" (yellow diamond). On "Clear" it goes to "Approve the claim" (pink, in focus), which "Logs" to the "Claims table" (purple cylinder). From "Approve the claim" the line "Claim queued" goes to the warehouse operator's "Book a courier", then "Inspect the item", then "Refund released" (green goal).

## 3 Every decision and where each exit goes

There is one decision, "Damage clear in the photo?". The exit "Clear" leaves the bottom of the diamond and goes to "Approve the claim". The exit "Unclear" leaves the top and goes to "Send the second photo" in the Customer lane, which loops back into the right side of the same diamond. Nothing else branches. The footnote says rejection, a failed inspection and an end for a still-unclear claim are left out because the runbook states none.

## 4 What the key says

The key lists: light blue rounded pill for where it starts, white rounded box with grey outline for a step, teal parallelogram for input or output, yellow diamond for a decision, green box with green outline for goal reached, purple cylinder for a data store, a thin grey arrow for leads to and a pink box with thick pink outline for in focus. Every shape on the board matches a key entry. All lines are the same solid grey arrow, so one line style covers them. The lane background colours (peach, mint, cream) are not in the key, but each lane carries its own name, so they need no key entry. The small line labels ("Unclear", "Clear", "Logs", "Claim queued") are plain text and read on their own.

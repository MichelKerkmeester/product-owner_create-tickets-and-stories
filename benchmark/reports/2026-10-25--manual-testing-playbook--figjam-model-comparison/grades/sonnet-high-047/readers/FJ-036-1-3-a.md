## 1 Title and the question this board answers

The title is "Change booking dates". It answers how a Roamstay guest changes the dates on an existing booking and what happens to the money when the price for the new dates differs.

## 2 Main path from start to end

The guest starts on "Booking page" (blue start shape), taps "Change dates" (line label) and lands on "Pick new dates" (white step). Then the yellow decision "Room free on those dates?" checks availability. From there the flow reaches the pink outlined decision "New price?", which splits three ways. All three branches meet at a grey join dot and end at the green goal shape "Updated booking".

## 3 Every decision and where each exit goes

"Room free on those dates?": Yes goes over the top straight to "New price?". No goes to "See other rooms", and from there "Pick a room" goes to "New price?".

"New price?": Lower goes to "Credit difference" and then to the join dot. Higher goes to "Pay difference" and then to the join dot. "Same, nothing charged" goes straight to the join dot. The join dot leads to "Updated booking".

## 4 What the key says

Blue rounded shape means where it starts. White rounded shape means a screen or step. Yellow diamond means a decision. Grey dot means paths join. Green rounded shape means the goal is reached. A plain arrow line means leads to. A diamond with a thick pink outline means in focus. Every mark on the board appears in the key. The line labels (Tap Change dates, Yes, No, Pick a room, Lower, Higher, Same, nothing charged) are plain text on lines and need no key entry. There is only one line style, so nothing is left unexplained there.

## 5 Unclear or ambiguous

"Pay difference" does not say when or how the guest pays, and the footer says payment failure is left out. "See other rooms" has only one exit, so it is not shown what happens if the guest finds no room they like or backs out. The footer also leaves out change window and fee rules, so I cannot tell whether a change fee applies on top of the price difference.

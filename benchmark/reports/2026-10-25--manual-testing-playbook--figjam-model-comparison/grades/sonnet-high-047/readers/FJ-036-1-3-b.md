## 1 Title and the question this board answers

The title is "Change booking dates". It answers how a Roamstay guest changes the dates on an existing booking and what the new price means for them: credit, extra payment or nothing.

## 2 Main path from start to end

The guest starts on "Booking page" (blue), taps "Change dates" and reaches "Pick new dates". Next comes the decision "Room free on those dates?". If yes the line goes straight to the focused decision "New price?". That decision splits three ways into "Credit difference", "Pay difference" or a direct line. All three meet at the grey join dot before the green goal "Updated booking".

## 3 Every decision and where each exit goes

"Room free on those dates?" has two exits. Yes goes over the top to "New price?". No goes to "See other rooms", whose "Pick a room" line also enters "New price?".

"New price?" has three exits. Lower goes to "Credit difference". Higher goes to "Pay difference". "Same, nothing charged" goes along the bottom straight to the join dot. Both difference steps also go to the join dot, which leads to "Updated booking".

## 4 What the key says

Blue rounded box is where it starts. White rounded box with grey outline is a screen or step. Yellow diamond is a decision. Grey dot is where paths join. Green rounded box with green outline is goal reached. A thin arrow means leads to. A yellow diamond with a thick pink outline is in focus. Every mark on the board appears in the key. The labels on the lines (Tap Change dates, Yes, No, Pick a room, Lower, Higher, Same, nothing charged) are not keyed but read clearly as conditions or actions on the arrows.

## 5 Anything unreadable, hard to follow or ambiguous

All text was readable at 100%. Two small points. The "Pick a room" line and the "Yes" line enter "New price?" at slightly different spots on its left side, which reads fine but looks a little crowded at the diamond's left tip. The board does not say whether "See other rooms" can end without a room being picked (for example the guest gives up), and the footnote says cancellation, payment failure, change-window and fee rules are left out, so "Pay difference" has no failure exit by design. It is also not stated whether "credit" means a refund to the card or account credit.

## 6 Could I act on it without asking

Yes for the happy flow and the three price outcomes, which are unambiguous, though I would ask what "credit" means in money terms and what happens if the guest leaves "See other rooms" without picking one.

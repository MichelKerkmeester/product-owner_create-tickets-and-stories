## Three questions

Q1: Who books the courier pickup?
The warehouse operator books it. "Book a courier" sits in the Warehouse operator lane at bottom right, right after the "Claim queued" arrow. Very sure.

Q2: What happens when the damage is unclear?
The "Unclear" exit of the diamond goes up to the customer, who sends a second photo ("Send the second photo"), and that photo comes back into the same diamond for another check. Sure of the loop. The footnote says the runbook gives no end if the damage is still unclear after the second photo.

Q3: Where does the claim change teams?
It moves from Customer to CS agent when "Damage reported" feeds the diamond, and from CS agent to Warehouse operator on the "Claim queued" arrow out of "Approve the claim" down to "Book a courier". The second handover is the one the board stresses. Very sure, read from the lane boundaries the arrows cross.

## 1 Title and the question this board answers

The title is "Damaged-item refund". It answers who handles a damaged-item claim at each stage from the customer's report until the refund is released.

## 2 Main path from start to end

"Damage reported" (blue start, Customer lane) leads to the diamond "Damage clear in the photo?" (CS agent lane). On "Clear" it goes to "Approve the claim" (pink in-focus step, CS agent lane), which logs to the "Claims table" cylinder. "Approve the claim" then sends "Claim queued" down to "Book a courier" (Warehouse operator lane), then "Inspect the item", then "Refund released" (green goal).

## 3 Every decision or branch and where each exit goes

There is one decision, "Damage clear in the photo?". The "Clear" exit leaves from the bottom and goes right to "Approve the claim". The "Unclear" exit leaves from the top and goes to "Send the second photo" in the Customer lane, which loops back into the right side of the diamond. "Approve the claim" also forks into two outputs, "Logs" to the "Claims table" and "Claim queued" to "Book a courier", but that is a split of work and not a decision.

## 4 What the key says and whether every mark is covered

Blue rounded box means where it starts. White rounded box with grey outline means step. Cyan parallelogram means input or output. Yellow diamond means decision. Green rounded box with green outline means goal reached. Purple cylinder means data store. A thin grey arrow means leads to. Pink box with a thick pink outline means in focus. Every shape on the board matches a key entry. The three lane background colours (peach, mint, cream) are not in the key but each lane carries its own name, so they need no entry. All lines are the same solid grey style so there is no dashed or other line style to explain. The connector text labels are self-explaining.

- The unclear loop has no exit for a claim that stays unclear after the second photo, and there is no rejection or failed inspection path. The footnote states this on purpose, so it is a known gap and not a drawing error, but a reader of the shapes alone could expect an end.
- The footnote mentions pickup skipped under 20 euros as a pilot left out. That is clear but small text at the bottom.
- The "Clear" label sits below the CS agent lane in the gap toward the Warehouse lane, so the line briefly looks like it leaves the CS team before returning to "Approve the claim". Minor.
- Why "Approve the claim" is the in-focus step is not said anywhere. The key says what pink means but not why this step was chosen.
- Who releases the refund is implied as the warehouse operator because "Refund released" sits in that lane, though it is a goal state and not an action.

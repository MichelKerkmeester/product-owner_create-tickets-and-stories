## The three questions

**Q1: Which states end a booking?**
Completed, No-show and Cancelled end a booking. They are grey "Final state" boxes and each has an arrow to an end dot on the right edge of the board. Sure (high). Found on the right column of the board and confirmed by the "Final state" and "End" swatches in the key.

**Q2: From which states can a guest cancel?**
From Pending and from Confirmed. The lower "guest cancels" line leaves Pending and the upper one leaves the bottom edge of the Confirmed group, which I read as meaning both Upcoming and In stay. Fairly sure for Pending (high) and moderately sure for Confirmed (medium), because the upper line drops from the group border just under In stay and could be misread as "from In stay only". Found in the lower middle of the board on the two lines that end at Cancelled.

**Q3: What moves a booking from Upcoming to In stay?**
The "check in" event. Sure (high). Found on the arrow between Upcoming and In stay inside the Confirmed group.

## 1 Title and the question this board answers

The title is "Booking status". The board answers which statuses a Roamstay booking passes through and what event moves it from one to the next, written for the support team.

## 2 The main path or structure from start to end

A booking starts at the start dot on the left and enters Pending. When the card is authorised the "card authorised" line carries it into the Confirmed group, where a second start dot leads to Upcoming. "check in" moves it from Upcoming to In stay. From In stay "check out [from In stay]" runs over the top of the board to Completed, which then goes to an end dot. Confirmed is a group holding Upcoming and In stay, and inside it In stay also has a plain arrow to an end dot at the right of the group.

## 3 Every decision or branch and where each exit goes

- Pending has two exits. "card authorised" goes into Confirmed (to Upcoming). "guest cancels" goes to Cancelled.
- Upcoming has three exits. "check in" goes to In stay. "missed check-in [from Upcoming]" leaves the right edge of Confirmed and goes to No-show. "guest cancels" (from the Confirmed group) goes to Cancelled.
- In stay has two or three exits. "check out [from In stay]" goes to Completed. "guest cancels" (from the Confirmed group) goes to Cancelled, if the group line covers In stay. A plain unlabelled arrow goes to the end dot inside Confirmed.
- Completed, No-show and Cancelled each have one exit, to an end dot.

There are no diamond decision shapes. The branches are all shown as several labelled lines leaving one state.

## 4 What the key says

- Black dot: Start.
- Light blue rounded box with blue outline: First state.
- White rounded box with grey outline: State.
- Grey rounded box with thicker grey outline: Final state.
- Black dot with a grey ring: End.
- Thin arrow: Leads to.
- Grey box with thick pink outline: "In focus: two moves reach it".

Not every mark is in the key. Missing are the pale blue "Confirmed" group container and what a line leaving its border means (it applies to every state inside), the square bracket guard text such as "[from In stay]" and "[from Upcoming]" and the plain text labels on lines (they are self explanatory but unexplained). The end dot inside the Confirmed group matches the End swatch, but the key does not say what ending the Confirmed group means compared with ending the booking.

## 5 Anything I could not read, could not follow or found ambiguous

- The end dot inside Confirmed at the far right of the group, reached by an unlabelled arrow from In stay. It is unclear whether this means the stay is over, which is already shown by "check out" to Completed. It reads as a second, unexplained way out of In stay.
- The "check out [from In stay]" line starts on the top border of Confirmed above In stay and the "missed check-in [from Upcoming]" line starts on the right border of Confirmed. Both leave the group, not the state, so the bracket guards carry the real meaning. I had to read the brackets to know which inner state they belong to.
- The upper "guest cancels" line drops from the bottom border of Confirmed directly under In stay with no bracket guard. By the same convention it should mean both Upcoming and In stay, but its position makes it look like an In stay only exit. Whether a guest can cancel mid stay is a real support question and the board leaves it to interpretation.
- The pink "In focus" outline on Cancelled says "two moves reach it". Completed and No-show each have one move, so this is accurate, but it is not clear why Cancelled is the focus for a support reader.
- The footnote names a gap: a Pending booking whose card is never authorised has no path. Pending can therefore get stuck, which the board admits rather than resolves. The footnote also says "Source: your request", which reads oddly to someone who did not make the request.
- All text is readable at 100 percent.

## 6 Could I act on this board without asking anyone

Mostly yes for the happy path and the end states, but no for cancellation during a stay and the meaning of the inner end dot in Confirmed, so I would need to ask before telling a guest whether they can cancel once checked in.

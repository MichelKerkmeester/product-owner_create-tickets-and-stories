## 4 What the key says and whether every mark is in it

Both keys are the same. Blue rounded box means where it starts. White rounded box with a grey outline means a screen or step. Yellow diamond means a decision. Green rounded box with a green outline means goal reached. A plain grey arrow means leads to. A green box with a thick pink outline means in focus. Every shape and colour on the boards is in the key. Not in the key: the word "(assumed)" on two connector labels in board 1, which is never explained, and the goal boxes on the board carry a pink outline rather than the green outline the "Goal reached" swatch shows, so a reader has to combine two entries. All lines are solid grey arrows, so no line style is left unexplained.

## 5 What I could not read, follow or found ambiguous

- "In focus" (pink outline) sits on the end box of each board. The key does not say what "in focus" means beyond the label, and since it only marks the endpoints it reads the same as "goal reached". I could not tell what extra the pink adds.
- "Google (assumed)" (board 1, middle) sends a Google sign up to "Enter the email code". It is unclear whether Google users really get an email code, and the label says this is assumed. The sources line also says no Google account screen is shown.
- "Resend (assumed)" (board 1, bottom) has no retry limit, lockout or way to change the email, so the "Error with resend" step can only loop back.
- "Template or blank?" (board 2, right) sends both answers to the same end box, so the board does not show what differs between a template and a blank start.
- The "Add emails" line (board 2, under the step) leaves from the bottom of "Ask for teammates' emails". It is clear it reaches "Name the workspace", but the sources line says what happens to those emails is left out.
- "Continue to setup" is green with a pink outline on board 1 and blue on board 2. That reads as a hand-off between boards, but no arrow or note links the two boards, only the shared label and the numbers in the titles.

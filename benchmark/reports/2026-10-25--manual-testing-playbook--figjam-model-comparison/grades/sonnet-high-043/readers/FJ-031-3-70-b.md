- "Yes, Start a return" mixes the branch answer with what looks like a button name, so I am unsure whether "Start a return" is a tap on screen or just a label (line between the first diamond and "Return reasons").
- "Order detail" to the diamond: the check is not a screen, so I cannot tell where the customer actually sees a start button or is told they are outside 30 days.
- Both delivery methods share one confirmation with a QR code. It is unclear whether a courier pickup also needs a QR code or whether the confirmation differs by method (right side).
- "Drop-off point" may be read as choosing a specific location, but no screen for picking a point is shown.
- The footnote says free pickup over 100 euros is left out because it is a proposal under review, so pickup cost is not covered.

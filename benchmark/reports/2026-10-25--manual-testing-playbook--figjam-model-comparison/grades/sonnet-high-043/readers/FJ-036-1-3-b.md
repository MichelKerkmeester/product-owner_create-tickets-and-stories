## 1 Title and the question this board answers

The title is "Change booking dates". The board answers how a Roamstay guest moves a booking to new dates, what happens if the room is taken on those dates and how the price difference is settled.

## 2 The main path or structure from start to end

The board has two sections read left to right. In "Find a room" the guest starts on "Booking page" (blue start shape), taps Change dates (the line label) to reach "Pick new dates", then hits the decision "Room free on those dates?". From there the flow crosses into "Settle the price" to the decision "New price?", which fans out to one of three steps ("Pay the difference", "Difference back as credit" or "Nothing charged"). All three join at the green goal "Updated booking".

## 3 Every decision or branch and where each exit goes

"Room free on those dates?" has two exits. Yes goes along a line over the top straight to "New price?". No goes to "See other rooms", and from there "Pick a room" leads to "New price?" as well.

"New price?" has three exits. Higher goes to "Pay the difference". Lower goes to "Difference back as credit". Same goes to "Nothing charged". Each of the three steps then leads to "Updated booking".

## 1 Title and the question this board answers

The title is "Booking status". It answers: what statuses does a Roamstay booking pass through and what moves it from one to the next, written for the support team.

## 2 The main path or structure from start to end

A booking starts at the start dot on the left and enters Pending (the light blue first state). When the card is authorised ("card authorised") it moves into the Confirmed group. Inside Confirmed it starts at Upcoming, moves to In stay on "check in", and In stay then runs to an end dot inside the group. From the Confirmed group a "check out [from In stay]" line goes over the top to Completed, which ends at an end dot. The happy path is therefore Pending, Upcoming, In stay, Completed.

## 3 Every decision or branch and where each exit goes

- Pending: "card authorised" goes to Confirmed (entering at Upcoming). "guest cancels" goes to Cancelled.
- Upcoming: "check in" goes to In stay. "missed check-in [from Upcoming]" goes to No-show (the line leaves the right edge of the Confirmed group).
- In stay: "check out [from In stay]" goes to Completed (the line leaves the top edge of the Confirmed group). In stay also has an arrow to the end dot inside Confirmed.
- Confirmed as a whole: "guest cancels" goes to Cancelled.
- Completed, No-show and Cancelled each go to their own end dot.

There are no diamond decision shapes. The branches are shown as several labelled arrows leaving a state or the group.

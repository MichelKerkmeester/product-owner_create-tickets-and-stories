## 1 Title and the question this board answers

The title is "Change booking dates". The board answers how a Roamstay guest changes the dates on an existing booking, from finding a room on the new dates to settling the price difference.

## 2 Main path from start to end

The flow starts at the blue "Booking page". The arrow labelled "Tap Change dates" leads to "Pick new dates", which leads to the decision "Room free on those dates?". On Yes the line runs over the top into the "Settle the price" section and reaches the decision "New price?". From "New price?" one of three steps follows ("Pay the difference" or "Difference back as credit" or "Nothing charged") and each of them leads to the green "Updated booking", which is the end goal.

## 3 Every decision and where each exit goes

"Room free on those dates?" has two exits. Yes goes straight to "New price?". No goes to "See other rooms", and from there the "Pick a room" line goes to "New price?".

"New price?" has three exits. Higher goes to "Pay the difference". Lower goes to "Difference back as credit". Same goes to "Nothing charged". All three then go to "Updated booking".

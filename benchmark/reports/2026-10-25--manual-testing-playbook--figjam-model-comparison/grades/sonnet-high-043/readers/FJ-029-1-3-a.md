## 4 What the key says and whether every mark is in it

Both keys say the same: a blue pill is where it starts, a white pill with a grey outline is a screen or step, a yellow diamond is a decision, a green pill with a green outline is goal reached, a thin grey arrow means leads to and a green pill with a thick pink outline means in focus.

Not every mark is in the key. The "(assumed)" tag on line labels ("Google (assumed)" and "Resend (assumed)") is not explained in the key, although the word is plain enough to guess. The words written on lines (Yes, No, Email, Team and so on) are not keyed but read naturally as exit labels. The plain green goal reached style (green outline without pink) never appears on either board, since both goals are drawn in the pink in focus style, so the key shows a style that is not used. The key does not say whether in focus also means goal reached. I read the two pink pills as goals that are also in focus, but that is my guess. The colour change of "Continue to setup" from green on board 1 to blue on board 2 is not explained in the key, though it follows from the start and goal meanings.

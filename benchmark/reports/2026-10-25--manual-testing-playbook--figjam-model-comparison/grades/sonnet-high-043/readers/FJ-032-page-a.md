## 1 Title and the question this board answers

The title is "Booking status". It answers what statuses a Roamstay booking passes through and what event moves it from one to the next.

## 2 Main path from start to end

A booking starts at the start dot on the left and enters "Pending" (the blue first state). On "card authorised" it enters the "Confirmed" group, where it starts in "Upcoming". On "check in" it moves to "In stay". On "check out [from In stay]" it leaves the group for "Completed", which then goes to its end dot. That is the happy path from start to end.

## 3 Every decision or branch and where each exit goes

- From Pending: "card authorised" goes to Confirmed (into Upcoming). "guest cancels" goes along the bottom to Cancelled.
- From Upcoming: "check in" goes to In stay. "missed check-in [from Upcoming]" goes to No-show. The guest can also cancel while Confirmed (see below).
- From In stay: "check out [from In stay]" goes to Completed.
- From the Confirmed group as a whole: "guest cancels" leaves the bottom of the group and goes to Cancelled.
- Completed, No-show and Cancelled each go only to an end dot.

There are no diamond decision shapes. The branches are just several labelled arrows leaving one state.

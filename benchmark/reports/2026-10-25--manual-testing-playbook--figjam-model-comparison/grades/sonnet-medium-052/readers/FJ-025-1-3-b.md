## 1. Title and the question this board answers
Two boards. Board 1, "Checkout payment, card authorised", shows the calls from the booking post to the 201 for an authorised card, for engineers on checkout and payments. Board 2, "Checkout payment, card declined", shows the calls from the booking post to the 402 for a declined card. Together they answer what happens in the API when a guest pays at checkout and the card is accepted or declined.

## 2. Main path
Board 1: GuestApp posts the booking to BookingApi (1). BookingApi holds the room for 15 minutes (2), a self-call. BookingApi sends "Authorise card" to PaymentProvider (3), highlighted in pink. PaymentProvider replies "Authorised" (4). BookingApi confirms the hold (5), a self-call. BookingApi sends "Booking id and dates" to PartnerHub as an async message (6). BookingApi replies "201" to GuestApp (7).
Board 2: steps 1 to 3 are the same, then PaymentProvider replies "Declined" (4) in pink dashed, BookingApi releases the hold (5), a self-call, and replies "402 Card declined" to GuestApp (6). PartnerHub is not on board 2 and nothing is sent to it.

## 3. Decisions and branches
There are no decision shapes. The only branch is the card result, which is split across the two boards. Authorised leads to board 1 and ends in 201. Declined leads to board 2 and ends in 402. Neither board shows the choice on itself.

## 4. The key
Board 1 key: a rounded pill means participant, a solid arrow means request, a dashed arrow means reply, a thin blue open arrow means async message, a purple circle means step number and a thick pink arrow means in focus, it decides the checkout. Board 2 key has the same participant, request, reply and step number entries and a pink arrow meaning in focus, a decline ends checkout. Every mark is in a key. The grey dashed vertical lifelines under each participant and the repeated participant pills at the bottom are not in the key but are standard. The self-call loops use the solid request style.

1. Title and the question this board answers
"Booking status", every status a Roamstay booking passes through, from Pending to Completed, Cancelled or No-show, and what moves it on. It answers which statuses exist and what triggers each change.

2. Main path
The black start dot leads to the blue "Pending" box (stays at most 15 minutes while the card is checked). The "card authorised" line goes to the "Confirmed" container, which has its own start dot leading to "Upcoming" (guest can still change dates). "check in" leads to "In stay" (hotel can add charges to the room), which leads to the container's end dot. "check out [from In stay]" goes to "Completed", which leads to an end dot.

3. Decisions and exits
There are no decision shapes, so the branches are the labelled exits. Pending has two, "card authorised" to Confirmed and "guest cancels" to Cancelled. Upcoming has "check in" to In stay and "missed check-in [from Upcoming]" to No-show. In stay has "check out [from In stay]" to Completed. Confirmed as a whole has "guest cancels" to Cancelled. Cancelled (cancel within 48 hours of check-in keeps the first night's price), No-show and Completed each go to an end dot.

4. The key
Black dot means start. Blue rounded box means first state. White rounded box means state. Grey box means final state. Black dot with grey ring means end. Thin arrow means leads to. Hot pink outline means in focus, a late cancel keeps a night. Every mark is in the key, with two small gaps. The large pale "Confirmed" container is not in the key, and Completed is a pill while No-show and Cancelled are squarer boxes, which the key does not distinguish.

5. Unreadable, unclear or ambiguous
All text was readable. The "check out [from In stay]" line starts from the left edge of the Confirmed container rather than from In stay, and the "guest cancels" line starts from its right edge, so the label does the work of naming the source. The footnote says what happens when Pending passes 15 minutes is not in the doc.

6. Could I act without asking anyone
Yes for the statuses and triggers, but I would ask what happens when Pending runs past 15 minutes because the board says that is left out.

Order: Cancelled > Pending > Confirmed > Upcoming > In stay > No-show > Completed

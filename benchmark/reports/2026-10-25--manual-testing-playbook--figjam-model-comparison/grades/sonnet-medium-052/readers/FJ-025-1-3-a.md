1. Title and the question this board answers
Board 1 is "Checkout payment, card authorised", the calls from the booking post to the 201 for a card that is authorised, for engineers on checkout and payments. Board 2 is "Checkout payment, card declined", the calls from the booking post to the 402 for a card that is declined. Together they answer what calls happen in checkout and what the app gets back in each case.

2. Main path
Board 1 runs top to bottom between GuestApp, BookingApi, PaymentProvider and PartnerHub. Step 1 GuestApp posts the booking to BookingApi. Step 2 BookingApi holds the room for 15 minutes (a call to itself). Step 3 it sends Authorise card to PaymentProvider. Step 4 PaymentProvider replies Authorised. Step 5 BookingApi confirms the hold. Step 6 it sends Booking id and dates to PartnerHub as an async message. Step 7 it replies 201 to GuestApp. Board 2 follows the same steps 1 to 3, then step 4 Declined, step 5 Release the hold, step 6 402 Card declined to GuestApp. There is no PartnerHub on board 2.

3. Decisions and branches
There are no decision shapes. The split between authorised and declined is shown by the two separate boards, which diverge at step 4 (Authorised against Declined). The footnotes say the declined card is drawn on board 2 and board 1 leaves it out, and board 2 leaves out the partner hub message.

4. The key
Rounded pill means participant. Solid arrow means request. Dashed arrow means reply. Thin blue arrow means async message. Purple numbered circle means step number. Thick pink arrow means in focus, on board 1 "it decides the checkout" and on board 2 "a decline ends checkout". Every mark is in the key. The self-calls on BookingApi (steps 2 and 5) are drawn as solid loops, which fits request but is not called out as a self-call. Dashed vertical lifelines are not in the key.

5. Unreadable, unclear or ambiguous
Everything was readable. The pink emphasis sits on different steps on each board, step 3 on board 1 and step 4 on board 2, so the key text differs by board. Hold expiry and retries are stated as not in the brief, so a retry after a decline and the 15 minute expiry are unknown. The participant pills are repeated at the bottom of each diagram.

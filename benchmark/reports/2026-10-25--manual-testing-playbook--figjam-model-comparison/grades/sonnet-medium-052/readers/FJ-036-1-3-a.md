1. Title and the question this board answers
"Change booking dates", from the booking page to the updated booking, for a teammate learning the feature. It answers what a guest does and what happens next when they change the dates of a booking, including money.

2. Main path
The blue "Booking page" leads, on "Tap Change dates", to "Pick new dates" (shows only nights the hotel has open). That leads to the diamond "Room free?". On Yes it goes to "New price?". The price then sets the result, and every price outcome ends at the green "Updated booking, also emailed".

3. Decisions and exits
"Room free?" has Yes, which goes straight to "New price?", and No, which goes to "See other rooms" (up to 3 rooms at the same hotel, price shown for the new dates). From there "Pick a room" goes back to "New price?". "New price?" has three exits. Higher goes to "Pay the difference" (charged to the card on file). Lower goes to "Get credit" (kept for 12 months). Same goes to "Nothing charged". All three lead to "Updated booking, also emailed".

4. The key
Blue pill means where it starts. White rounded box means screen or step. Yellow diamond means decision. Green pill means goal reached. Thin arrow means leads to. Pink-outlined diamond means in focus, the price sets what happens. Every shape on the board is in the key. The "Nothing charged" shape is a rounded pill in white, a little rounder than the other white cards, but it is still a step.

5. Unreadable, unclear or ambiguous
Everything was readable at 100%. The "Yes" line from "Room free?" runs over the top of "See other rooms" and arrives at "New price?" on the left, close to where "Pick a room" also arrives, so the two inbound lines sit close together. The footnote says cancellation or refund, a failed card charge, hotel-side steps and screen layouts are left out. The board does not say what happens if the guest finds no suitable other room.

6. Could I act without asking anyone
Yes, because the steps, both decisions, the room limit and the credit period are all printed, though I would ask what the guest does when none of the other rooms suit.

## 1. Title and the question this board answers
"Start a return", subtitled "From the order list to a confirmed return, for the product and design team". It answers how a customer gets from their order list to a confirmed return in the app.

## 2. Main path
The path starts at the blue pill "Order list" and goes to the card "Order detail", which shows each item with price and delivery date and a Start a return button. The arrow then enters the diamond "Within 30 days of delivery?". On Yes it goes to the card "Return reason" (Damaged, Wrong size, Not as shown, Changed my mind), then to the diamond "Drop-off or courier pickup?". The drop-off exit goes to the card "Pick a drop-off point" (map of the 5 nearest points) and then to the green pill "Confirmation with QR code". The courier exit goes to the card "Pick a pickup date" (date within the next 3 working days) and then up into the same green pill.

## 3. Decisions and branches
"Within 30 days of delivery?" has Yes, which goes to "Return reason", and No, which goes to the dashed pill "Contact support" where the flow ends. "Drop-off or courier pickup?" has Drop-off point, which goes to "Pick a drop-off point", and Courier pickup, which goes to "Pick a pickup date". Both of those then meet at "Confirmation with QR code".

## 4. The key
Blue pill means where it starts. White rounded card means screen or step. Yellow diamond means decision. Green pill means goal reached. Dashed orange pill means leaves the flow. A thin arrow means leads to. A thick pink outline on a diamond means in focus, late returns go to support. Every shape and line on the board matches the key. The three tinted section backgrounds and their small Starts/Ends notes are not in the key but are labelled by their own text. The decision exit labels (Yes, No, Drop-off point, Courier pickup) sit on the arrows and need no key entry.

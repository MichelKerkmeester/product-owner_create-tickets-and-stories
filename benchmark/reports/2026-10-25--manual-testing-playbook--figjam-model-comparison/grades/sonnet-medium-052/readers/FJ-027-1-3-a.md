1. Title and the question this board answers
"Stays model", the tables behind a stay, split into the guest side and the partner side, for Guest Support. It answers which tables exist, how they link and which columns the key ones hold.

2. Main structure
This is a data model, not a flow, so there is no start or end. The guest side holds "Guest", "Booking" and "Payment". The partner side holds "Property", "Room" and "Rate Plan". "Booking" is the hub. Guest makes Booking, Room is reserved by Booking, Rate Plan prices Booking, and Booking holds Payment. On the partner side Property offers Room and Property publishes Rate Plan.

3. Decisions and branches
There are no decisions. The relationships are Guest to Booking "makes (1 to 0..n)", Room to Booking "is reserved by (1 to 0..n)", Rate Plan to Booking "prices (1 to 0..n)", Booking to Payment "holds (1 to 0..n)", Property to Room "offers (1 to 0..n)", and Property to Rate Plan "publishes (1 to 0..n)". In each the first named table is the one side. The line ends also show small crow's foot and bar marks.

4. The key
White box means entity. Plain line means identifying. Hot pink outlined box means in focus, every other table joins it. The pink Booking box and the plain lines match the key. The crow's foot and bar end marks, the "PK" and "FK" column tags and the "1 to 0..n" labels are not in the key. The tinted Guest side and Partner side zones are labelled but not in the key.

5. Unreadable, unclear or ambiguous
All text was readable. The "prices" label sits in the middle of a long vertical line from Rate Plan up to Booking and is easy to read as belonging to either end. Payment status values are not given. The footnote says columns for guests, properties and rooms are left out. The key calls every line identifying even though Booking holds foreign keys to Guest, Room and Rate Plan, which I cannot judge further.

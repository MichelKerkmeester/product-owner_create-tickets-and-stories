## 1. Title and the question this board answers
"Booking status", subtitled "Every status a Roamstay booking passes through, from Pending to Completed, Cancelled or No-show, and what moves it on". It answers which statuses a booking goes through and what triggers each move.

## 2. Main path
A start dot leads to the blue "Pending" box (stays at most 15 minutes while the card is checked). The line labelled "card authorised" runs over the top into the "Confirmed" container, where a second start dot leads to "Upcoming" (guest can still change dates). The arrow "check in" leads to "In stay" (hotel can add charges to the room), which leads to an end dot inside the container. The line "check out [from In stay]" runs down the left side of the container to "Completed", which leads to an end dot.

## 3. Decisions and branches
There are no decision shapes, only labelled moves. From "Pending", "card authorised" goes to "Confirmed" and "guest cancels" goes to "Cancelled". From "Confirmed", "guest cancels" goes to "Cancelled", "missed check-in [from Upcoming]" goes to "No-show" and "check out [from In stay]" goes to "Completed". Inside Confirmed, "check in" takes "Upcoming" to "In stay". "Completed", "Cancelled" and "No-show" each lead to an end dot.

## 4. The key
Black dot means start. Blue rounded box means first state. White box means state. Grey box means final state. Black dot in a grey ring means end. A thin arrow means leads to. A thick pink outline means in focus, a late cancel keeps a night. Every shape is in the key. The Confirmed container is a grouping and has no key entry, though its pale blue colour is not explained. The "Completed" box has more rounded corners than "No-show" and "Cancelled", which the key does not distinguish. The bracketed text in two line labels ([from In stay] and [from Upcoming]) is not explained but reads clearly as where the move starts.

## 5. Unreadable, unclear or ambiguous
All readable at 100%. The lines for "check out [from In stay]", "missed check-in [from Upcoming]" and the right-hand "guest cancels" leave the edge of the Confirmed container and not the state they name, so the bracket text has to be used to tell which state they belong to. The right-hand "guest cancels" has no bracket so it is unclear whether it applies from both Upcoming and In stay. The footnote says what happens when Pending passes 15 minutes is left out, so that exit is missing.

## 6. Could I act without asking anyone
Yes for the normal statuses and their triggers, but I would ask what happens to a Pending booking after 15 minutes and whether guests can cancel during In stay.

Order: Cancelled > Pending > Confirmed > Upcoming > In stay > No-show > Completed

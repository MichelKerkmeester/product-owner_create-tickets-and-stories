1. Title and the question this board answers
The board is "Start a return", from the order list to a confirmed return, for the product and design team. It answers how a customer gets from their orders to a return that is confirmed, and what the customer sees along the way.

2. Main path
The path starts at the blue pill "Order list" and goes to the "Order detail" card, which shows each item with price and delivery date and a Start a return button. From there it enters the diamond "Within 30 days of delivery?". On Yes it goes to the "Return reason" card, then to the diamond "Drop-off or courier pickup?". Drop-off point leads to "Pick a drop-off point" (map of the 5 nearest points), and courier pickup leads to "Pick a pickup date". Both end at the green pill "Confirmation with QR code". The Hand over note says the QR code is also sent by email and can be scanned or handed to the courier.

3. Decisions and exits
"Within 30 days of delivery?" has Yes to "Return reason" and No to the dashed "Contact support", which leaves the flow. "Drop-off or courier pickup?" has Drop-off point to "Pick a drop-off point" and Courier pickup to "Pick a pickup date". Return reason is a card that lists four reasons and has one exit, onward to the second diamond.

4. The key
Blue pill means where it starts. White rounded rectangle means a screen or step. Yellow diamond means a decision. Green pill means goal reached. Dashed orange pill means leaves the flow. Thin arrow means leads to. Pink-outlined diamond means in focus, late returns go to support. Every shape on the board matches a key entry. The three tinted section blocks are not in the key but each carries its own label and notes. The section colours (orange, cyan, yellow) are not explained and seem only to group.

5. Could not read or ambiguous
Nothing was unreadable at 100%. It is not stated whether the customer must pick a reason from the list or can skip it, and the "Return reason" card has no label saying it is a choice. It is not stated what Contact support does next. The pickup date card does not say who books the courier.

6. Could I act without asking anyone
Yes, because the path, both branches, the reasons, the 3 working day window and the excluded proposal are all printed on the board.

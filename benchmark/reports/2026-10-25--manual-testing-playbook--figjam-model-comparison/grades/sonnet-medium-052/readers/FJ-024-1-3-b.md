## 1. Title and the question this board answers
"Block sync", subtitled "How a block change travels from a client to the other devices, ending in an iOS wake-up, for Loomlist Engineering". It answers how a block edit travels through the system and wakes a sleeping iOS device.

## 2. Main path
A client (Desktop client, Web client or iOS client) holds a live socket with the "API gateway", which checks the session token and keeps each client socket open. The gateway routes sync requests to "sync-service", which merges edits to the same block. The sync-service writes blocks to "Blocks database" (solid line over the top) and publishes changes to "Change stream" (dashed line), which keeps events for 7 days. The Change stream feeds events to "Push worker", which sends an Apple push that wakes the device through "Apple push".

## 3. Decisions and branches
There are no decision shapes. The only split is at "sync-service", which has two outputs, a write to "Blocks database" and a publish to "Change stream". The Change stream then goes only to "Push worker", and the Push worker goes only to "Apple push".

## 4. The key
Teal pill means app or client. Blue rounded box means edge or gateway. White rounded card means service. Orange cylinder lying on its side means queue or stream. Purple upright cylinder means data store. Grey dashed pill means outside system. A solid arrow means leads to. A dashed arrow means async or outside call. A double-headed arrow means both ways. A thick pink outline means in focus, every change is written here. Every shape and line on the board is in the key. The group boxes carry their own titles and have no key entry.

1. Title and the question this board answers
"Block sync", how a block change travels from a client to the other devices, ending in an iOS wake-up, for Loomlist Engineering.

2. Main path
A client (Desktop, Web or iOS) talks to the "API gateway" over a live socket. The gateway checks the session token and sends "Routes sync requests" to "sync-service", which merges edits to the same block. sync-service "Writes blocks" to the "Blocks database" and "Publishes changes" to the "Change stream" (keeps events for 7 days). The change stream "Feeds events" to the "Push worker", which sends the Apple push call to "Apple push" that wakes the device.

3. Decisions and branches
There are no decision shapes. The only split is at sync-service, which has two outputs, a solid Writes blocks line to the Blocks database and a dashed Publishes changes line to the Change stream. The Change stream has one output, to the Push worker.

4. The key
Teal pill means app or client. Blue rounded box means edge or gateway. White rounded box means service. Orange cylinder on its side means queue or stream. Purple upright cylinder means data store. Grey dashed pill means outside system. Solid single arrow means leads to. Dashed arrow means async or outside call. Double-headed arrow means both ways. Hot pink outline means in focus, every change is written here. Every mark I see is in the key. The tinted group boxes are labelled but their colours are not in the key.

5. Unreadable, unclear or ambiguous
Everything was readable at 100%. The Desktop client line shows "Live socket" with arrowheads at both ends and the gateway end enters from the top, while Web and iOS enter at the left and bottom, so the routing is a little hard to follow but the meaning is clear. The "Writes blocks" line runs a long way around the top and is the only solid arrow into a data store. The Apple push line ends at the Apple push pill and does not show which device is woken. The footnote says file storage is left out.

6. Could I act without asking anyone
Yes for understanding the path, limits and owners of each step, though I would ask how the woken iOS device then fetches the change because the board stops at Apple push.

## 1. Title and the question this board answers
"Change booking dates", subtitled "From the booking page to the updated booking, for a teammate learning the feature". It answers how a guest changes the dates on a booking and what happens to the price.

## 2. Main path
It starts at the blue pill "Booking page". The arrow "Tap Change dates" leads to the card "Pick new dates" (shows only nights the hotel has open). That leads to the diamond "Room free?". On Yes the line runs over the top into the diamond "New price?". From "New price?" the three exits lead to "Pay the difference", "Get credit" or "Nothing charged", and all three lead to the green pill "Updated booking, also emailed".

## 3. Decisions and branches
"Room free?" has Yes, which goes straight to "New price?", and No, which goes to "See other rooms" (up to 3 rooms at the same hotel, price shown for the new dates). From there the line "Pick a room" runs back into "New price?". "New price?" has three exits. Higher goes to "Pay the difference" (charged to the card on file). Lower goes to "Get credit" (kept for 12 months). Same goes to "Nothing charged". All three then go to "Updated booking, also emailed".

## 4. The key
Blue pill means where it starts. White rounded card or pill means screen or step. Yellow diamond means decision. Green pill means goal reached. A thin arrow means leads to. A thick pink outline on a diamond means in focus, the price sets what happens. Every shape and line is in the key. "Nothing charged" is a white pill and "Pay the difference" and "Get credit" are square-cornered cards, which the key lumps into one screen or step style. The exit labels sit on the arrows and need no key entry.

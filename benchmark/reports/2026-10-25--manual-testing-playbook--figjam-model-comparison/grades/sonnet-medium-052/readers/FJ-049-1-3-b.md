## 1. Title and the question this board answers
"Roamstay damage claim", subtitled "From the hotel's report to a closed claim, for support". It answers how a hotel's damage report moves through support and the guest to a closed claim.

## 2. Main path
It starts at the blue pill "Guest checks out" in the Hotel lane. That leads to "Report the damage" (status Reported, sends photos and a repair quote, quote must be from a listed supplier). The line drops into the Roamstay support lane to "Send the claim to the guest" (status With the guest, asks the hotel for more photos if they do not show the damage). A line drops into the Guest lane to the diamond "Accept the claim or dispute it?" (the guest has 7 days to accept or dispute). From there the claim goes either to "Charge the card on file" or to "Review the dispute", and every route ends at the green pill "Closed".

## 3. Decisions and branches
"Accept the claim or dispute it?" has Accept, which goes up to "Charge the card on file" (accepted claim, charged to the card on file, then the claim is Closed), and Dispute with a reply, which goes up to "Review the dispute" (status Under review, reads the photos and the guest's reply, decides within 5 working days). "Review the dispute" leads to "Uphold the claim or drop it?". Uphold goes to "Charge the card" (upheld claim, charged within 2 working days, then the claim is Closed). Drop goes to "Drop the claim" (then the claim is Closed). "Charge the card on file", "Charge the card" and "Drop the claim" all lead to "Closed".

## 4. The key
Blue pill means where it starts. White rounded card means step. Yellow diamond means decision. Green pill means goal reached. A thin arrow means leads to. A thick pink outline on a diamond means in focus, the guest decides here. Every shape and line is in the key. The lane tints are labelled by the lane names. The exit labels (Accept, Dispute with a reply, Uphold, Drop) sit on the arrows and need no key entry. The status lines inside cards (Reported, With the guest, Under review) are plain text and not in the key.

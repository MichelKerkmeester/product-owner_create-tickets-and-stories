## 1. Title and the question this board answers
"Stays model", subtitled "The tables behind a stay, split into the guest side and the partner side, for Guest Support". It answers which tables make up a stay and how they join.

## 2. Main path or structure
This is a table diagram and not a flow. The guest side has "Guest", "Booking" and "Payment". The partner side has "Property", "Room" and "Rate Plan". "Guest" makes "Booking". "Booking" holds "Payment". "Booking" is reserved by "Room" (the line crosses from the guest side down to the partner side). "Rate Plan" prices "Booking" (a second line crossing from the partner side up to "Booking"). On the partner side "Property" offers "Room" and "Property" publishes "Rate Plan". "Booking" sits in the middle of the web and has the most columns (id, guestId, roomId, ratePlanId, checkIn, checkOut, status).

## 3. Decisions and branches
There are none, since this is a data model. The only things to read are the six relations, all labelled "(1 to 0..n)". The line from "Guest" to "Booking" reads one guest makes zero or more bookings. The line from "Property" to "Room" reads one property offers zero or more rooms. The line from "Property" to "Rate Plan" reads one property publishes zero or more rate plans. The line from "Rate Plan" to "Booking" reads one rate plan prices zero or more bookings. The line from "Room" to "Booking" reads one room is reserved by zero or more bookings. The line from "Booking" to "Payment" reads one booking holds zero or more payments.

## 4. The key
A white rounded rectangle means entity, a plain line means identifying, and a thick pink outline means in focus, every other table joins it. Every table is in the key as an entity. The key shows only a plain line, but the lines also carry end marks (a single bar and a crow's foot with a circle) that the key does not explain. The coloured sides are labelled by their own titles. The "(1 to 0..n)" in each relation label is not in the key but is readable.

## 5. Unreadable, unclear or ambiguous

The long "prices" line from "Rate Plan" goes a long way around to the bottom of "Booking" and the "is reserved by" line comes into "Booking" from the left, so it takes a moment to find which end belongs to which table. The values of the Payment "status" column are not given. Whether "Guest" and "Payment" really are tied by a relation is not drawn, only through "Booking".

1. Title and the question this board answers
"Damaged-item refund", from a damage report to a released refund, for the product team. It answers who does what, in which order, from the moment a customer reports damage until the refund is released.

2. Main path
"Damage reported" (customer, the help form asks for order number, photo and short description) goes to "Check the photo" in the CS agent lane, then to the diamond "Damage clear in the photo?". Clear goes to "Approve the claim". Claim queued then passes to the Warehouse operator lane, to "Book courier pickup" (next working day), then "Inspect on arrival" (held against the customer's photo), then the green "Refund released".

3. Decisions and branches
"Damage clear in the photo?" has three labelled exits. Unclear goes to "Ask the customer for a second photo", which goes to "Send second photo" in the Customer lane, which loops back to "Check the photo". Clear goes to "Approve the claim". Decision logged goes to the "Claims table" cylinder. The CS agent note says every decision is logged there. There is no exit for a claim that is rejected, and no limit on how many times the second photo loop can run.

4. The key
Blue pill means where it starts. White rounded shape means a step. Yellow diamond means a decision. Green pill means goal reached. Purple cylinder means data store. Thin arrow means leads to. Hot pink outlined pill means in focus, claim leaves CS for warehouse. Every shape type on the board is in the key. The two white cards at the bottom (Book courier pickup, Inspect on arrival) are square-cornered and carry a second line of detail, while the key shows a rounded step, so the card shape is not exactly in the key. The tinted lane colours are not explained but each lane is labelled.

5. Unreadable, unclear or ambiguous
All text was readable at 100%. The edge label "Claim queued" sits on a long vertical line over the CS agent and Warehouse operator border, which is slightly easy to miss. The "Decision logged" exit leaves the diamond alongside the real choices, so it reads as a third outcome rather than a side effect. It does not say what the refund amount is or what happens if the inspection finds no damage.

6. Could I act without asking anyone
Mostly yes, the owners, loop, handoff and timings are printed, but I would ask what happens when the inspection does not match the photo because the board stops at Refund released.

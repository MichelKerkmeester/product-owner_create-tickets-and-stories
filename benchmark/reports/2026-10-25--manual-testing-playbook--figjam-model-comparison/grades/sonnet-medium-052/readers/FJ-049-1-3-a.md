1. Title and the question this board answers
"Roamstay damage claim", from the hotel's report to a closed claim, for support. It answers who does what, in which order, when a hotel reports damage after a guest checks out.

2. Main path
The blue "Guest checks out" leads to the hotel's "Report the damage" card (status Reported, sends photos and a repair quote, quote must be from a listed supplier). That goes down to "Send the claim to the guest" in the support lane (status With the guest, sends the photos and the quote, guest sees them and the amount in the app, asks the hotel for more photos if they do not show the damage). That goes to the guest's diamond "Accept the claim or dispute it?". Every route ends at the green "Closed".

3. Decisions and exits
"Accept the claim or dispute it?" has two exits. Accept goes to "Charge the card on file" (accepted claim, charged to the card on file, then the claim is Closed), which goes to Closed. Dispute with a reply goes to "Review the dispute" (status Under review, reads the photos and the guest's reply, decides within 5 working days). That goes to "Uphold the claim or drop it?". Uphold goes to "Charge the card" (upheld claim, charged within 2 working days, then the claim is Closed), which goes to Closed. Drop goes to "Drop the claim" (then the claim is Closed), which goes to Closed.

4. The key
Blue pill means where it starts. White rounded box means step. Yellow diamond means decision. Green pill means goal reached. Thin arrow means leads to. Pink-outlined diamond means in focus, the guest decides here. Every shape on the board is in the key. The three tinted lanes are labelled but their colours are not in the key.

5. Unreadable, unclear or ambiguous
The card text is very small and I read it by zooming, but all of it was legible. The labels "Dispute with a reply" and "Accept" sit on long lines in the middle of the board, so it takes some tracing to see which exit they belong to. "Charge the card on file" and "Charge the card" have near identical names and do the same thing for different outcomes. The footnote says the waiver under 25 euros and a guest who does not reply in 7 days are left out, so the no-reply case is not drawn. The Hotel lane has no note of its own.

6. Could I act without asking anyone
Yes for the path, owners, statuses and time limits, but I would ask what happens when the guest does not reply within 7 days because the board says that is not stated.

# Tidy the FigJam board labels: questions before drafting

I'll create your task. I read the FigJam file `<test-file-key>` as the board whose labels the task covers. Please answer in one message:

**Labels in scope:** Which labels need tidying, such as section titles, sticky notes, connector labels or shape text? Is it the whole board or named sections?
- Recommended: the whole board, so the task does not miss labels nobody listed

**What tidy means:** Which problems should the task fix? Examples are inconsistent casing, typos, duplicate or outdated names, overlapping or misaligned text, or mixed font sizes
- Recommended: name the two or three problems you see most, since each one becomes a checklist item QA can verify

**Naming rule:** Is there a casing or naming convention the labels must follow, such as sentence case or the names used in the app?
- Recommended: sentence case with screen names matching the app, because that keeps the board readable next to the product

**Why it matters:** Who reads this board and what goes wrong for them today?
- Recommended: designers and engineers who use the board as a reference, where unclear labels slow handoff

**Done when:** What does success look like, and who signs it off?
- Recommended: every label in scope follows the rule and the board owner approves it

**Owner and links:** Who does the work, and are there related tickets or a parent task?

**Validation:** What am I likely to misunderstand, for example whether labels may be renamed or only reformatted?

Outline, in drawing order:
1. Hotel lane: Report the damage (start). Photos and a repair quote from a listed supplier. Moves the claim to Reported
2. Support lane: Send claim to guest. The guest sees the photos, the quote and the amount in the app. Moves the claim to With the guest
3. Guest lane: Accept or dispute? (the highlighted decision, 7 days to answer)
4. Accept: Support lane, Charge the card on file
5. Dispute: Support lane, Review photos and reply. Reads the photos and the guest's reply, and asks the hotel for more photos if they do not show the damage. Moves the claim to Under review
6. Decision: Uphold or drop? Support decides within 5 working days
7. Uphold: Charge the card on file, within 2 working days
8. Drop, and both charge steps, end at Claim closed. That end moves the claim to Closed
9. Status lane on top: Reported, With the guest, Under review, Closed, with a dotted line from each step that moves the claim

That is 12 nodes and 16 connectors in four lanes, inside the Faithful budget.

Two framings:
- Lanes plus a status lane, which shows the hand-offs and the status at each step. It costs a wider board. I recommend it
- A state map of the claim alone, which shows every status exit. It loses who acts at each step

The more-photos request sits in the review step's detail and not as a loop of its own. The policy does not say what the hotel does next or what status the claim holds meanwhile, and a loop would invent both.

# Pause a pending payout

### About

---

Brands need to hold a payout while they confirm amounts or resolve a question with a creator. A pause control on the pending payout, with a required reason, keeps the delay explainable instead of silent. The pause lasts 24 hours, after which the payout resumes its normal schedule.

### Requirements

---

1.  **Pause a pending payout**

---

Brands can pause a payout while it is pending. The payout stays pending for the duration of the pause.

**Checklist**

- [ ] A brand can pause a payout while it is pending
- [ ] A paused payout stays in the pending state for 24 hours
- [ ] A paused payout resumes its normal schedule after 24 hours

2.  **Required reason**

---

Every pause carries a reason so the payout history shows why a payout was held.

**Checklist**

- [ ] A reason is required before a payout can be paused
- [ ] The saved reason stays visible on the payout while the pause is active

3.  **Creator banner**

---

The creator sees a banner on the payout while the pause is active, so they know the payment is delayed and why.

**Checklist**

- [ ] The payout shows a pause banner to the creator while the pause is active
- [ ] The banner names the reason the payout was paused
- [ ] The banner clears when the 24 hour pause ends

## The FJ-027 route of 'is booked under'

The edge leaves the top of Booking, runs right across the Guest side zone at y 384, exits through the zone's right border, runs down at x 1520 (24 px outside both zones, so outside the composer's 16 px rim) and enters Rate Plan from its right side. It is about 1,600 px long against about 460 px for the 'reserves' edge from the same card. It crosses no box and no edge, shares no magnet and rides no border, so it costs no scorecard point. It is a poor route all the same.

Cause: Booking has four relationships and four magnets. Makes uses the left, holds the right and reserves the bottom, so the second edge toward the Partner side must leave from the top. The composer prices a magnet shared between two departing edges at 10,000 and a long detour at its length, so it takes the detour.

Would a different model shorten it? Only partly. I composed variants locally with the composer and the trace tool, without calling Figma. Swapping the declaration order and `direction TB` changed nothing (1,684 and 1,680 px in the trace against 1,684 for the original). Writing the two relationships the other way round, as the worked example does (`RatePlan ||--o{ Booking`), shortened that edge to about 1,010 px but pushed a roughly 1,280 px detour onto 'makes' from Guest to Booking and widened the board from 1,736 to 2,200 px. The model also loses the user's wording on the label. So the long run moves rather than disappears.

Would a composer change? Yes, and it is the real fix. Letting two departing edges that head to the same zone share the bottom magnet for a stub of at most 56 px, or pricing detour length above the 10,000 shared-magnet cost, would give a route of about 700 px. I did not run that, it is inference. Confirming test: compose the unchanged model after the change and read the connector legs of `edge:Booking->RatePlan` in the trace.

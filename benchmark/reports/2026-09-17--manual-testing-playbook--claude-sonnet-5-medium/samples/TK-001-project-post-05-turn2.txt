```markdown
# Creator Payout Pause Indicator

### About

---

Creators currently have no visibility when their payout is paused, and there's no record of why. This task adds a pause indicator to the payout row and requires a reason whenever a payout is paused, so creators can see the paused state at a glance and QA can confirm both the state and the reason in payout history.

### Requirements

---

**Payout pause indicator and reason capture**

---

When a payout is paused, the system must capture why and surface that state directly on the payout, so support and QA can trace the pause without digging through separate tooling.

**Checklist**

- [ ] Reason field is required when a payout is paused, and the pause cannot be saved without it
- [ ] A pause indicator appears in the payout row for any payout currently paused
- [ ] The paused state and its reason are visible in payout history for QA to verify
```

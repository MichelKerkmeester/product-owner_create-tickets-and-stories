```markdown
# Creator payout pause indicator and reason tracking

### About

---

Creators can have a pending payout paused, but there is currently no visible indicator on the payout row and no captured reason for why the pause happened. This task adds a pause indicator to the payout row and requires a reason whenever a payout is paused, so creators see pause status at a glance and support or finance can trace the cause through payout history.

### Requirements

---

1.  **Pause indicator on payout row**

---

Show a visible indicator on the payout row whenever a pending payout is paused, so creators can tell at a glance which payouts are on hold.

**Checklist**

- [ ] Payout row displays a pause indicator when a pending payout's status is paused
- [ ] Indicator is not shown when the payout is not paused
- [ ] Indicator updates without a page reload when a payout is paused or unpaused

---

2.  **Required pause reason**

---

Capture a reason whenever a payout is paused, and block the pause action until a reason is entered, so every pause carries an auditable cause.

**Checklist**

- [ ] Pause action requires a non-empty reason before it can be submitted
- [ ] Reason is stored against the specific pause event
- [ ] Attempting to pause without a reason shows a validation error and does not pause the payout

---

3.  **Pause visibility in payout history**

---

Surface the pause and its reason in payout history so QA can verify a pause occurred and why, without inspecting the database.

**Checklist**

- [ ] Payout history shows the pause event, its timestamp and its reason
- [ ] QA can verify pause state and reason directly from payout history
- [ ] Unpausing a payout is also recorded in payout history
```

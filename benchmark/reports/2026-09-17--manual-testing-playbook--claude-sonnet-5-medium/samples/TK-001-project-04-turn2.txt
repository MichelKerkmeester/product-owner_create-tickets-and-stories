```markdown
# Creator payout pause indicator and reason capture

### About

---

Creators can currently have their payouts paused, but nothing records why or shows the pause on the payout itself. Support and QA have no way to confirm a pause is active or trace it back to a cause, so a paused payout looks identical to one still processing normally. Store a required reason whenever a payout is paused, show a pause indicator on the payout row, and make the pause and its reason visible in payout history so QA can verify it without escalating.

### Requirements

---

1.  **Store the pause reason**

---

A payout cannot be paused without a reason. The reason is captured at the moment of pause and stays attached to that payout for as long as the pause exists.

**Checklist**

- [ ] Add a required reason field to the pause action, block the action until a reason is entered
- [ ] Store the reason, the timestamp and who triggered the pause against the payout record
- [ ] Reject any pause request submitted without a reason

---

2.  **Show a pause indicator on the payout row**

---

Creators and internal users need to see at a glance which payouts are paused without opening the payout detail.

**Checklist**

- [ ] Add a pause indicator to the payout row when a payout's status is paused
- [ ] Indicator is visible in the same row list creators use to review pending payouts
- [ ] Indicator disappears once the payout is resumed or resolved

---

3.  **Surface the pause in payout history for QA verification**

---

QA needs to confirm a pause happened and read its reason directly from payout history, without database access.

**Checklist**

- [ ] Add a pause entry to the payout's history log, including the stored reason and timestamp
- [ ] QA can view the pause entry from the payout history view for that payout
- [ ] History entry persists after the payout is resumed
```

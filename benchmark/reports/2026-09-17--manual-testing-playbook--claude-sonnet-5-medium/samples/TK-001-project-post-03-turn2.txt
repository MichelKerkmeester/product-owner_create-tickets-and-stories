# Creator payout pause indicator and reason tracking

### About

---

Creators and support teams currently have no way to tell whether a pending payout has been paused or why. This task adds a visible pause indicator to the payout row and requires a reason whenever a payout is paused, so the pause state and its cause are traceable through payout history for QA and support review.

### Requirements

---

1.  **Require a reason when pausing a payout**

---

Pausing a pending payout must capture why, so the reason is available for support and QA review later.

**Checklist**

- [ ] Pausing a payout requires a reason to be entered before the pause is saved
- [ ] A pause cannot be saved with an empty or missing reason
- [ ] The reason is stored against the payout record

---

2.  **Show a pause indicator in the payout row**

---

Creators and internal users need to see at a glance which pending payouts are paused.

**Checklist**

- [ ] A paused payout shows a visible indicator in the payout row
- [ ] The indicator is distinguishable from active and completed payout states

---

3.  **Surface the pause reason in payout history**

---

QA and support need to verify a pause and its reason without querying the backend directly.

**Checklist**

- [ ] The stored pause reason is visible in the payout history entry for the paused payout
- [ ] QA can confirm both the pause state and the reason from the payout history view

**# Creator payout pause with reason and indicator**

### About
---
Admins need a way to pause a creator's pending payout and record why, so support and finance can hold funds during a dispute or review without losing context on the reason. Right now a pause has no required reason and no visible signal on the payout row, so anyone reviewing payouts can't tell a pause happened or why. This adds a required pause reason at the point of pause, a visible indicator on the payout row, and a verifiable record in payout history.

### Requirements
---

1. **Require a reason when pausing a payout**

---

An admin cannot pause a payout without entering a reason. The reason is stored against the payout record so it survives after the pause is lifted.

**Checklist**
- [ ] Pause action blocks submission until a reason is entered
- [ ] Reason is stored on the payout record at the time of pause
- [ ] Reason remains attached to the payout record after the pause is lifted

2. **Show a pause indicator on the payout row**

---

Anyone viewing the payout list can tell at a glance which payouts are paused, without opening each record.

**Checklist**
- [ ] Paused payouts display a visible indicator on the payout row
- [ ] Indicator is visible only while the payout is paused
- [ ] Indicator disappears once the pause is lifted

3. **Surface the pause in payout history for QA verification**

---

QA can confirm a pause happened, when it happened and why, by checking payout history rather than a live-only state.

**Checklist**
- [ ] Payout history shows the pause event with its timestamp
- [ ] Payout history shows the stored reason for that pause
- [ ] Payout history shows when the pause was lifted, if applicable

```markdown
# Payout pause toggle silently reverts to off after refresh

*   **Environment:** Web, Chrome 126
*   **Scope:** Brand wallets with a pending payout
*   **Consistency:** Reproduces every time
*   **Source:** Reported directly by michel.k@getbarter.com, not yet corroborated with screenshot or logs

* * *

### Steps to reproduce

1.  Open a brand wallet that has a pending payout.
2.  Switch the payout pause toggle on.
3.  Refresh the page.

* * *

### 1. Observed Behavior

The toggle displays as off after the page refresh, even though the payout itself still shows as paused. The toggle's displayed state no longer matches the actual payout state.

### 2. Expected Behavior

The toggle stays on after refresh and continues to reflect the actual pause state. It should only turn off when the brand explicitly resumes the payout.

* * *

### Impact

A brand or internal user checking the wallet after a refresh sees an off toggle and may reasonably conclude the payout is not paused, when it still is. That mismatch invites an unnecessary re-toggle or an incorrect assumption about payout status.

* * *

### Acceptance criteria

1\. After toggling payout pause on and refreshing the page, the toggle displays as on.
- [ ] _Mark as done, if the criteria are met_

2\. The toggle's displayed state matches the actual payout pause state after refresh, after navigating away and back, and after reopening the wallet.
- [ ] _Mark as done, if the criteria are met_

3\. Toggling pause off still resumes the payout and the toggle correctly displays as off, with no regression to the existing resume flow.
- [ ] _Mark as done, if the criteria are met_

* * *
```

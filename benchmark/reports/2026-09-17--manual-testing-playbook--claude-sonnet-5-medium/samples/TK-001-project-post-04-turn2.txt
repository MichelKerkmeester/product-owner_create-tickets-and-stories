```markdown
# Creator Payout Pause Indicator

### About

---

Creators and admins need clear visibility when a pending payout is paused, and the reason for the pause must be captured for accountability and QA verification. This task adds a pause indicator to the payout row and requires a reason whenever a payout is paused, with the pause reflected in payout history for QA review.

### Requirements

---

1.  **Pause reason capture**

---

When a payout is paused, the user pausing it must supply a reason before the pause is applied. The reason is stored against the payout record for later review.

**Checklist**

- [ ] Reason field is required and cannot be left blank when pausing a payout
- [ ] Submitted reason is saved and associated with the paused payout
- [ ] Attempting to pause without a reason blocks the action with a clear message

---

2.  **Pause indicator on payout row**

---

The payout row shows a visible pause indicator whenever a payout is paused, so creators and admins can identify paused payouts at a glance.

**Checklist**

- [ ] Paused payouts display a pause indicator in the payout row
- [ ] Indicator is visible only while the payout remains paused
- [ ] Indicator does not appear on payouts that are not paused

---

3.  **Pause visibility in payout history**

---

QA and support need to verify that a pause occurred, including its reason, by reviewing payout history.

**Checklist**

- [ ] Payout history shows the pause event, including the stored reason
- [ ] QA can confirm the pause state and reason from payout history without additional tooling
```

### Bug

**1. Observed Behavior**

---

After a page refresh, the payout pause toggle displays off even though the underlying payout is still paused
- The toggle visually reverts to off
- The payout status stays paused, so the toggle and the payout state disagree
- No error message appears

Steps to Reproduce:
1. Open a brand wallet with a pending payout
2. Switch the payout pause toggle on
3. Refresh the page
4. Observe the toggle shows off while the payout still shows paused

```markdown
# Creator payout pause indicator

### About

---

Creators can have a pending payout paused, but the system has no way to record why or to show that status to the creator or to support. This task adds a required pause reason on the payout record and a visible pause indicator on the payout row, so anyone reviewing a payout can see it is paused and why, and QA can confirm the state from payout history.

### Requirements

---

1.  **Pause reason capture**

---

When a payout is paused, the reason must be captured and stored with that payout record so the pause can be explained and audited later.

**Checklist**

- [ ] Pause reason field is required when a payout is paused
- [ ] Pause reason is stored on the payout record
- [ ] Payout cannot be paused without a reason submitted

---

2.  **Pause indicator and history visibility**

---

A paused payout needs a clear visual indicator in the payout row, and the pause status with its reason must carry through to payout history so QA can verify it after the fact.

**Checklist**

- [ ] Pause indicator displays in the payout row for a paused payout
- [ ] Pause status and reason are visible in payout history
- [ ] QA can verify a paused payout's status and reason from payout history alone
```

# Creator payout pause indicator and reason tracking

### About

---

Creators and QA currently have no way to see or verify why a payout is paused. A pause carries no stored reason and no visible signal on the payout row, so support cannot explain a stalled payout and QA cannot confirm the pause after the fact. This task adds a required pause reason captured at the point of pause, a pause indicator on the payout row, and a verifiable record in payout history.

### Requirements

---

1.  **Require a pause reason**

---

A payout cannot be paused without a reason, giving support and QA a source of truth for why a payout stopped moving.

**Checklist**

- [ ] Pausing a payout requires a non-empty reason before the pause is applied
- [ ] Attempting to pause without a reason blocks the action and prompts for one
- [ ] The reason is stored against the paused payout record

---

2.  **Show a pause indicator on the payout row**

---

Creators need to see at a glance that a payout is paused, without opening it for detail.

**Checklist**

- [ ] A paused payout displays a visible pause indicator in its row
- [ ] The indicator shows only while the payout is in the paused state
- [ ] The indicator clears once the payout is resumed

---

3.  **Make the pause verifiable in payout history**

---

QA and support need to confirm a pause occurred and why, after the fact, without relying on current row state alone.

**Checklist**

- [ ] Payout history records the pause event, its reason and the timestamp it was applied
- [ ] Payout history records when and how the pause was lifted
- [ ] QA can locate the pause record for a given payout from history alone

# Pause creator payouts with a required reason

### About

---

Support and creators need a way to pause a pending payout while a review is in progress, such as a fraud check or an open dispute, without releasing funds prematurely. Every pause must carry a reason so the payout history stays auditable and QA can confirm the pause happened for the right cause.

### Requirements

---

1.  **Require a reason when pausing a payout**

---

Pausing blocks the payout from progressing further until it is resumed. The reason is mandatory so support, creators and QA can always tell why a payout stopped.

**Checklist**

- [ ] Pausing a pending payout requires a reason before the pause is saved
- [ ] The reason is stored with the payout record
- [ ] Attempting to pause without a reason blocks the action and shows an error

---

2.  **Show a pause indicator in the payout row**

---

Anyone scanning the payout list needs to see at a glance which payouts are paused, without opening each one individually.

**Checklist**

- [ ] A paused payout shows a visible pause indicator in its payout row
- [ ] The indicator appears wherever that payout row is listed

---

3.  **Surface the pause and reason in payout history for QA verification**

---

QA needs to confirm a pause and its reason from the payout history alone, without inspecting the underlying data.

**Checklist**

- [ ] Payout history shows the pause event, its timestamp and the stored reason
- [ ] QA can verify the pause and reason from payout history without a database query

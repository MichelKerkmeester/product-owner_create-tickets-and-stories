```markdown
# Creator payout pause indicator

### About

---

Creators pause pending payouts, and support and QA need to see that a payout is paused and why. This task adds a pause reason capture, a visible pause indicator on the payout row and a durable record in payout history so the pause state can be verified after the fact.

### Requirements

---

1.  **Pause reason capture**

---

When a payout is paused, the reason for the pause is captured and stored with the payout record.

**Checklist**

- [ ] Pause reason field is required to complete a pause action
- [ ] Pause action is blocked until a reason is provided
- [ ] Reason is stored with the payout record

---

2.  **Pause indicator on payout row**

---

A pending payout that is paused is visibly distinct from an active pending payout wherever payout rows are listed.

**Checklist**

- [ ] Paused payouts show an indicator on the payout row
- [ ] Indicator is visually distinguishable from the active pending state

---

3.  **Pause visibility in payout history**

---

QA and support can confirm a payout's pause state and reason without relying on the live row alone.

**Checklist**

- [ ] Paused payout appears in payout history with its pause status
- [ ] Stored pause reason is visible in payout history
- [ ] QA can verify the pause from payout history alone
```

After refreshing the page, the payout pause toggle displays as off, while the payout still shows a paused status
- Toggle UI state does not match the actual payout pause state
- No error message displayed
- Reproduces on every attempt (per reporter)

Steps to Reproduce:
1. Open a brand wallet with a pending payout
2. Switch the payout pause toggle on

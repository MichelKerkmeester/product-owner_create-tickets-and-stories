```markdown
# Pause and resume payouts for a creator

### About

---

Trust & Safety and Finance need to stop a specific creator's payouts without disabling their account, so they can hold funds during a dispute, an active investigation or a compliance review. This task adds a pause control on the creator's account that blocks new payout runs while the creator keeps trading normally, and a resume control that releases the hold once the review closes.

### Requirements

---

### **Pause and resume control**

---

1.  **Pause a creator's payouts**

---

An authorized admin can pause payouts for one creator from their account page, with a required reason captured at the time of the pause.

**Checklist**

- [ ] Pause action available only to roles with the payout-hold permission
- [ ] Pause requires a reason selected from a fixed list plus an optional free-text note
- [ ] Paused state and reason are visible on the creator's account page
- [ ] Pausing a creator does not affect their ability to log in, list items or complete trades

---

2.  **Resume a creator's payouts**

---

An authorized admin can resume a paused creator's payouts, which releases the hold for the next payout run.

**Checklist**

- [ ] Resume action available only to roles with the payout-hold permission
- [ ] Resume requires a reason or a reference to the review outcome
- [ ] Resume timestamp and actor are recorded

---

### **Payout processing behavior**

---

3.  **Hold payouts while a creator is paused**

---

Payouts due to a paused creator are held rather than processed, and resume automatically once the pause is lifted.

**Checklist**

- [ ] Scheduled payout runs skip any creator currently paused
- [ ] Held amounts remain visible to the creator as pending, not failed
- [ ] Lifting the pause queues the held amount for the next scheduled run
- [ ] A payout already submitted to the payment processor before the pause was applied is not clawed back by this feature

---

4.  **Notify on pause and resume**

---

The creator and the internal team receive a notification when a pause or resume happens, so no one has to check the account page to find out.

**Checklist**

- [ ] Creator receives a notification when their payouts are paused, with no internal reason disclosed
- [ ] Creator receives a notification when their payouts resume
- [ ] Internal team receives a notification identifying the actor, reason and timestamp

---

5.  **Audit trail**

---

Every pause and resume action is logged for compliance review.

**Checklist**

- [ ] Log entry includes actor, timestamp, reason and creator ID
- [ ] Log is visible to Trust & Safety and Finance roles
- [ ] Log entries cannot be edited or deleted
```

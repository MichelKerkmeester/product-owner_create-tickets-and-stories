```markdown
# Notification retry pipeline

* * *

## Overview

* * *

This guide explains how the notification retry pipeline behaves so support can diagnose a failed notification and requeue it correctly. It reflects current system behavior.

### Retry attempts and backoff

* * *

*   A notification retries on a 30 second backoff
*   The pipeline makes five retry attempts before succeeding or giving up
*   There is no sixth retry attempt. Instead, the notification moves to the failed queue

### Diagnosing a failed notification

* * *

A notification in the failed queue has already used all five retry attempts on the 30 second backoff without succeeding. Finding it there confirms it reached that state rather than being newly stuck or still retrying.

*   **Failed queue** — holds notifications that exhausted all five retry attempts
*   **Requeue action** — the control support uses to restart delivery for a notification sitting in the failed queue

### Requeuing a notification

* * *

1.  Locate the notification in the failed queue
2.  Select the Requeue action on that notification
3.  Confirm the notification leaves the failed queue and re-enters the retry cycle

### What to expect after requeue

* * *

*   Requeuing restarts the backoff from the beginning
*   The notification gets a fresh set of five attempts on the 30 second backoff
*   If all five fail again, the notification returns to the failed queue and can be requeued again
```

```markdown
# Show a pause indicator and reason for paused creator payouts

### About

---

Creators currently have no way to tell that a pending payout is on hold, and support has no stored explanation to point to when a creator asks why. Add a pause indicator to the payout row and require a reason whenever a payout is paused, so creators get a visible signal and QA and support can confirm the pause and its reason from the payout history.

### Requirements

---

1.  **Require and store a pause reason**

---

Every paused payout must carry a reason so the pause can be explained later without guesswork.

**Checklist**

- [ ] Pausing a pending payout requires a non-empty reason before the pause is saved
- [ ] The reason is stored against that specific payout record
- [ ] Attempting to pause without a reason blocks the action and shows an inline error

2.  **Show a pause indicator on the payout row**

---

Creators need to recognize a paused payout at a glance in their payout list.

**Checklist**

- [ ] A paused payout displays a visible pause indicator on its row
- [ ] The indicator is visible only while the payout is paused
- [ ] A payout with no pause shows no indicator

3.  **Make the pause verifiable in payout history**

---

QA and support need to confirm a pause and its reason after the fact, not just at the moment it happens.

**Checklist**

- [ ] The payout history view shows the paused status for the affected payout
- [ ] The stored pause reason is visible from the payout history view
- [ ] The paused state and reason persist across sessions and remain visible after the pause is lifted
```

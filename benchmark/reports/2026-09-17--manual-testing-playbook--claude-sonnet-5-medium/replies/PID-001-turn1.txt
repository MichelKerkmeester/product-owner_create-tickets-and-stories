```markdown
# Add a pause toggle for pending payouts

### About

---

Brands currently have no way to hold a pending payout once it is queued, so a mistaken or disputed payout runs to completion before anyone can intervene. Add a toggle that lets a brand pause a pending payout for 24 hours, with a required reason so the creator and support have context for the hold.

### Requirements

---

1.  **Pause toggle on the pending payout**

---

Brands need a visible, reversible way to pause a payout before it settles. The pause applies only to payouts still in a pending state and automatically expires after 24 hours if no further action is taken.

**Checklist**

- [ ] Toggle is available on each pending payout and disabled or hidden once the payout is no longer pending
- [ ] Enabling the toggle pauses the payout for exactly 24 hours from the time it is switched on
- [ ] Payout automatically resumes and proceeds normally when the 24-hour hold expires
- [ ] Brand can turn the toggle off before the 24 hours elapse to resume the payout early
- [ ] Payout status reflects "Paused" state distinctly from "Pending" while the hold is active

---

2.  **Required reason field**

---

A pause cannot be submitted without a reason, so support and the creator always have context for why a payout was held.

**Checklist**

- [ ] Reason field is required before the pause toggle can be confirmed
- [ ] Attempting to pause without a reason blocks submission and shows a validation message
- [ ] Submitted reason is stored with the pause event and visible wherever the pause is reviewed
- [ ] Reason field enforces a minimum length so a single character cannot satisfy the requirement

---

3.  **Creator banner**

---

The creator whose payout is paused needs a visible notice so the hold is not silent, without exposing internal-only detail.

**Checklist**

- [ ] Creator sees a banner on the affected payout while the pause is active
- [ ] Banner states that the payout is paused and shows the time remaining or expected resume time
- [ ] Banner does not display the brand's internal reason text
- [ ] Banner is removed automatically once the pause expires or is turned off early
```

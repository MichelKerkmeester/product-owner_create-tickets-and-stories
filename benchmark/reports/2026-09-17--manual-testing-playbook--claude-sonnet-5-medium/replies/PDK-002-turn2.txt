### Glossary
* * *
#### Terms and states
* * *
*   **`paused`** — The payout is held and not yet sent to the brand
*   **`released`** — The hold has ended and the payout proceeds through normal payout processing
* * *

## Behavior rules
* * *
**1. Automatic release after 24 hours**
* * *
*   **When** — A payout enters the `paused` state
*   **Then** — The pause holds for 24 hours, then the system releases the payout automatically, moving it to `released`
*   **Status** — Current behavior
* * *

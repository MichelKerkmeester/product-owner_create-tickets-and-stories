```markdown
# Show payout pause reason and date on the payout detail screen

### About

---

Creators can't currently tell why a payout was paused or when it happened, which pushes them to open a support ticket for an answer they should be able to see themselves. Showing the pause reason and pause date on the payout detail screen lets creators self-serve that context.

### Requirements

---

**Show the pause reason and pause date for a paused payout**

---

When a payout is in a paused state, the payout detail screen must show why it was paused and when the pause happened, in language a creator can understand without contacting support.

**Checklist**

- [ ] Show the pause reason on the payout detail screen for any payout in a paused state
- [ ] Show the date the payout was paused, alongside the reason
- [ ] Reason and date are visible only when the payout is paused; a payout that isn't paused shows neither
- [ ] Pause reason text is plain language a creator can act on, with no internal codes or jargon

**User Story**

- **Given:** a creator opens the detail screen for a payout that is currently paused
- **When:** the screen loads
- **Then:** the creator sees both the reason the payout was paused and the date it was paused
```

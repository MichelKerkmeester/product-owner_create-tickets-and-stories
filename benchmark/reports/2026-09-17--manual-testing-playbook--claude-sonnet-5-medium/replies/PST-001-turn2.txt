# Brand - Payouts - Pause payout

* * *
_A story is used to define product requirements, acceptance criteria, etc._
_Use it as the foundation for Tasks that work towards fulfilling the acceptance criteria._

## About
* * *
A brand can hold a pending payout for a fixed window instead of letting it process on schedule. This story adds a pause action on the payout row, a required reason and a status badge, then releases the payout automatically once the hold ends.

### Problem
* * *
A pending payout processes on schedule with no way for a brand to stop it:
*   There is no way to hold a payout once it is pending
*   A brand with a reason to stop a payout, such as a dispute or a check still in progress, has no lever
* * *

### Solution
* * *
Add a pause action to the pending payout row that puts the payout on a fixed 24-hour hold and records why. The row reflects the paused state so the brand can see it at a glance, and the hold lifts on its own once the window ends, so nobody has to remember to resume it.
* * *

#### **Expected outcomes**
* * *
*   A brand can stop a pending payout from processing without contacting support
*   Every pause carries a recorded reason
*   A paused payout resumes on its own, with no manual step to release it
* * *

## Requirements
* * *
**Payout row**
* * *
*   The pause action is labelled `Pause payout`
*   The payout row shows a `Paused` badge while the hold is active

**Pause hold**
* * *
*   The reason field is required to confirm a pause
*   The hold lasts exactly `24 hours` from confirmation, then the payout releases automatically
* * *
##   

## Acceptance criteria
* * *
All acceptance criteria below must be met, or discuss and rescope any that cannot be met.

1\. **A brand pauses a pending payout with a reason**
* * *
*   **Given** a brand is viewing a pending payout
*   **When** the brand selects `Pause payout` and submits a reason
*   **Then** the payout stops processing and the row shows the `Paused` badge
*   **And** the brand cannot confirm the pause without entering a reason
* * *
- [ ] _Mark as done, if the criteria are met_

2\. **A paused payout releases on its own once the hold ends**
* * *
*   **Given** a payout has been paused
*   **When** the `24-hour` hold ends
*   **Then** the payout resumes processing with no action from the brand
*   **And** the `Paused` badge no longer shows on the row
* * *
- [ ] _Mark as done, if the criteria are met_
* * *

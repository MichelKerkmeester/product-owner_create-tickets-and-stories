# Creator - Wallet - Payout Alerts

* * *

## Acceptance criteria

* * *

1 ) **The creator turns on payout alerts**

* * *
*   **Given** a creator in the wallet
*   **When** they turn on payout alerts
*   **Then** each payout sends an alert
* * *
- [] _Mark as done, if the criteria are met_

2 ) **The creator sees a failed payout**

* * *
*   **Given** a payout that failed
*   **When** the creator opens the wallet
*   **Then** the failed payout shows an alert
* * *
- [] _Mark as done, if the criteria are met_

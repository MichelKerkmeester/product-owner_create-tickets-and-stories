# Iteration 003 — What the corpus proves a board can steer, and in whose numbers

Lineage: `deepseek`. Builds on: iteration 001 findings 3, 4, 5 and 7 (spacing, titles, grouping and multi-diagram composition), and iteration 002 findings 2, 3, 8, 10 and 11 (proximity, zoning, the title-and-description contract, the missing size dial, status tints). Iterations 001 and 002 reasoned from the packet and the comparator; this one widens the source set to the 22-repo corpus to find what a board can actually carry, stated by someone who builds boards for a living.

## Focus

What layout, spacing, grouping, connector-routing, density and section behaviour do the 22-repo corpus and Figma's own published skills demonstrate that a Mermaid model or the generator can steer?

## Findings

1. **Figma's own board skill states a reading order for a board, and the packet has none.** It opens with "**Reading direction and grouping**: Left-to-right, top-to-bottom. Context on the left, evidence in the middle, proposal/asks on the right. Supporting detail and appendix below." That is a three-zone narrative order with an appendix band — a rule a reader can be walked through. `sk-create-figjam` has per-type direction (iteration 001, finding 2) and no statement about how a reader traverses the finished board. [SOURCE: research/corpus/lightspeedwp__.github/plugins/figma/figma-use-figjam/references/plan-board-content.md:38]

2. **Figma states the board's spacing language as three named distances, and they are an order of magnitude larger than the comparator's node gap.** "**Tight clustering** (60-92px) = same thought. **Loose spacing** (200-400px) = different topics. **Zone breaks** (1000px+) = different part of the board." A board's smallest unit of meaning is therefore 60px, not the 24px minimum gap `output-spec.md` gives between nodes. This is the decisive fact for spacing in this lineage: the FigJam packet cannot adopt the comparator's diagram-side spacing scale, and the board-side scale it should adopt instead is this one. [SOURCE: research/corpus/lightspeedwp__.github/plugins/figma/figma-use-figjam/references/plan-board-content.md:42] [SOURCE: .skilled/skills/sk-design/sk-design-diagram/references/foundations/output-spec.md:79]

3. **Figma requires every board to have an entry point, in a form the packet already almost has.** "**Every board needs an entry point**: Board title (60-96px) at top-left, clearly visible at overview zoom. For templates, add a meta/instructions section. For meetings, a colored agenda sticky." The packet's title recipe — a text node 64px above the diagram in charcoal — matches the *position* but not the *scale* (a FigJam text node at default size is a label, not a 60-96px title) and not the *default* (it is an optional extension). Iteration 002, finding 8 identified the missing title contract; this finding supplies its numbers. [SOURCE: research/corpus/lightspeedwp__.github/plugins/figma/figma-use-figjam/references/plan-board-content.md:82] [SOURCE: AI Systems/Product Owner/sk-create-figjam/references/hybrid-workflow.md:150]

4. **Sections nest on a FigJam board by design, so the packet's "a section never wraps a generated node" rule is a policy about moving nodes, not a platform limit.** Figma's guidance is "**Always wrap the entire board in one top-level white section**" and "Sections nest. A board is a section that contains zone sections, which contain card sections." The packet's caution is real — `position-figjam-nodes.md` confirms a node's coordinates become parent-relative after `appendChild`, and that "After adding nodes to a section, you MUST check that the section encompasses its children" — but the prohibition is protecting the generated layout from a side effect, not reporting an impossibility. A board could still be sectioned *if* the sectioning is done without moving the generated geometry, or if the whole diagram is generated inside a section from the start. [SOURCE: research/corpus/lightspeedwp__.github/plugins/figma/figma-use-figjam/references/plan-board-content.md:92] [SOURCE: research/corpus/lightspeedwp__.github/plugins/figma/figma-use-figjam/references/position-figjam-nodes.md:85] [SOURCE: AI Systems/Product Owner/sk-create-figjam/references/hybrid-workflow.md:148]

5. **Figma's own diagram skill says the fix for a cramped or tangled generated diagram is better Mermaid, and stops there.** "**Don't reposition nodes.** ELK's layout is what it is for now. If the diagram looks cramped or tangled, the fix is better Mermaid, not manual repositioning via `use_figma`." This confirms the packet's layer choice independently (iteration 001, finding 1) and identifies the missing artifact precisely: nobody in either source says *what* "better Mermaid" is. That is the gap the packet should close, because it is the only lever the generator leaves. [SOURCE: research/corpus/lightspeedwp__.github/plugins/figma/figma-generate-diagram/references/workflow.md:148]

6. **Figma treats a title block and a legend as ordinary extension work, not as exceptional requests.** Its hybrid-workflow guidance lists them among the tasks that need no inspection at all: "**Inspection is optional.** Skip `get_figjam` when your extensions don't need precise anchoring (e.g., adding a title text block above the diagram, adding a legend off to one side)." The packet instead routes every extension through "Extend when the request asks for something the model cannot carry", which makes a title conditional on a request. Two different default postures, and Figma's is the one that produces a board a stranger can read. [SOURCE: research/corpus/lightspeedwp__.github/plugins/figma/figma-generate-diagram/references/workflow.md:70] [SOURCE: AI Systems/Product Owner/sk-create-figjam/references/hybrid-workflow.md:29]

7. **The corpus contains a formal glance-order method that maps exactly onto the research question's "where the eye lands first".** "Before placing nodes, write three short lines: 1. First glance: the one claim, decision, or object that should win within two seconds. 2. Second glance: the evidence or relationship that advances the argument. 3. Last glance: risks, metadata, appendix, or other material that should remain available without competing." It also warns against the packet's own likely failure: "Repeating the primary sentence in both a board title and a hero card creates two focal objects for one idea." The packet has one accent rule at node level and nothing at board level. [SOURCE: research/corpus/prasantloki__figjam-builder/references/composition-grammar.md:5]

8. **The corpus states a focus budget per section, which is the board-level analogue of the packet's one-accent rule.** "One primary focal object per section. Two or three secondary objects may support it. Everything else should be quieter through smaller scale, lower contrast, simpler containers, or later position", with the ordering "position and visual mass first, typography second, and color as reinforcement". The packet's accent rule says "at most one accent, on the node a reader would keep if they could keep only one" — the same instinct, scoped to a diagram rather than a board, and without the "everything else should be quieter" half that does the actual work. [SOURCE: research/corpus/prasantloki__figjam-builder/references/composition-grammar.md:17] [SOURCE: AI Systems/Product Owner/sk-create-figjam/references/quality-rubric.md:49]

9. **The corpus gives spacing a semantic scale keyed to relationship, which is the missing half of the packet's proximity rule.** "Spacing communicates grouping before labels do. Use a clear progression: 16-32px between title, body, and metadata within one text stack. 32-48px of card padding for normal narrative content. 60-92px between peer objects in the same thought. 160-240px between distinct groups inside one section." The closing rule is the one the packet needs most: "Unrelated groups should never be closer than the text blocks inside a card." The packet's only grouping affordance is a tint expanded 24px on every side — a constant, where the corpus asks for a ratio between *within-group* and *between-group* distance. [SOURCE: research/corpus/prasantloki__figjam-builder/references/composition-grammar.md:25] [SOURCE: AI Systems/Product Owner/sk-create-figjam/references/hybrid-workflow.md:118]

10. **The corpus makes text size a computable function of viewing distance, which resolves the packet's missing size dial into something measurable.** The method is "Normalize the overview review to a maximum dimension of 1200px. Before building, estimate `reviewScale = min(1, 1200 / max(wrapperWidth, wrapperHeight))`. Divide the rendered-size targets below by `reviewScale` to get starting canvas font sizes", with rendered targets at the 1200px overview — board title 44-56px, section heading 32-44px, body 13-18px, metadata 10-13px — and two governing sentences: "Viewing distance overrides the nominal preset", and "The floor is a legibility requirement, not a recommendation to make every role equally loud". Because the generated diagram's font cannot be changed (iteration 001, finding 9), this is the one usable route to the comparator's reading-distance insight from iteration 002, finding 10: compute the review scale, then decide node count and label length from the rendered size the board will actually be read at. [SOURCE: research/corpus/prasantloki__figjam-builder/references/typography-grammar.md:24] [SOURCE: research/corpus/prasantloki__figjam-builder/references/typography-grammar.md:36] [SOURCE: research/corpus/prasantloki__figjam-builder/references/typography-grammar.md:38] [SOURCE: AI Systems/Product Owner/sk-create-figjam/references/style-guide.md:128]

11. **The corpus supplies the two-pass review method the scorecard's Layout and Clarity dimensions are missing.** "Run the two-zoom review: 1. Overview zoom: identify what actually wins first, second, and last. If that order differs from the plan, revise scale, position, contrast, or grouping. 2. Reading zoom: verify comfortable line length, clean phrase-boundary wraps, breathing room, connector labels, and absence of clipping or overlap", followed by four questions including "Would removing one card, tint, label, or connector make the argument clearer?" and the verdict "A structurally valid board can still be compositionally unresolved. Ship only when both zoom levels work." The playbook currently screenshots at 100% zoom only and scores Layout on crossings, so the overview pass — the one that would catch a board with no first glance — never happens. [SOURCE: research/corpus/prasantloki__figjam-builder/references/composition-grammar.md:106] [SOURCE: research/corpus/prasantloki__figjam-builder/references/composition-grammar.md:118] [SOURCE: AI Systems/Product Owner/sk-create-figjam/manual-testing-playbook/manual-testing-playbook.md:80]

12. **The corpus's geometry audit defines the collision tests a board review can actually run, including one the packet's read-back cannot.** It requires "at least 12px and prefer the 16-32px text-stack range" between vertically consecutive text blocks whose horizontal spans overlap, checks every non-connector child against its intended parent bounds, and — the important one — "Judge connector crossings from the rendered screenshot; a connector bounding box does not describe its path." The packet's read-back compares node and connector counts from a node list and explicitly refuses to read position; the corpus says the crossing judgment must come from the image, which the packet already captures but never uses for that purpose. [SOURCE: research/corpus/prasantloki__figjam-builder/references/composition-grammar.md:98] [SOURCE: AI Systems/Product Owner/sk-create-figjam/references/flow-mode.md:223]

13. **Figma's own sticky geometry contradicts the packet's legend column, by the platform's own fixed numbers.** Stickies "are 240x240 (square) or 416x240 (wide, for longer text). These sizes are fixed; stickies cannot be resized", and "**Always lay out stickies in a grid.** Rows and columns, consistent 64px gap". The packet stacks legend stickies in a single column at a 32-pixel gap. A 32px gap between 240px-tall fixed-size stickies puts the legend column at half the platform's own grid rhythm, which is why the packet's legend reads as a list rather than as a FigJam object. [SOURCE: research/corpus/lightspeedwp__.github/plugins/figma/figma-use-figjam/references/plan-board-content.md:254] [SOURCE: AI Systems/Product Owner/sk-create-figjam/references/hybrid-workflow.md:103]

14. **The packet already matches or beats Figma's own guidance on the parts that constrain content, and that should be said plainly so nothing good is traded away.** The packet's shared rules are stricter than Figma's universal constraints in three places — a 40-character label cap where Figma caps nothing, a per-type node budget where Figma states none, and a named shape vocabulary per type where Figma's own fallback archetype only says "distinct fills per node type". The corpus adds layout and measurement vocabulary; it does not add content discipline. [SOURCE: research/corpus/lightspeedwp__.github/plugins/figma/figma-generate-diagram/SKILL.md:49] [SOURCE: research/corpus/lightspeedwp__.github/plugins/figma/figma-use-figjam/references/plan-board-content.md:339] [SOURCE: AI Systems/Product Owner/sk-create-figjam/references/flow-mode.md:160]

## Questions Answered

- "What layout, spacing, grouping, connector-routing, density and section behaviour do the 22-repo corpus and Figma's own published skills demonstrate that a Mermaid model or the generator can steer?" — answered. Direction and grouping are steerable only through the model, and the board's spacing language is three fixed bands (60-92, 200-400, 1000+) that a model cannot set and an extension can express. Titles, glance order, focus budget, section nesting and the legend are extension-layer work with published numbers. Connector crossings must be judged from a screenshot, not a node list. Figma's own answer to a cramped diagram is "better Mermaid", which the packet has not yet defined.

## Ruled Out This Iteration

- **Adopting the corpus's 1200px overview normalisation as a literal board rule.** It assumes a wrapper the packet does not own. The transferable part is the review-scale *method* (finding 10), not the constant.
- **Using the importer repos (`tmcw__figjam-mermaid`, `memi-design__mermaid-jam`, `lauragalera__figjam-erd-plugin`) as style sources.** Read and dismissed: they document Mermaid-to-FigJam import mechanics, and none states a composition or legibility rule. Direction for later iterations: do not re-open them for design claims.
- **Treating Figma's board-content skill as a competitor to `sk-create-figjam`.** It explicitly excludes flowcharts, architecture, sequence, state and ER diagrams and routes them to `generate_diagram`, so it is the board layer the packet lacks, not an alternative to it.

## Findings

1. Every visual value on every board is set in one file's constants block: the `SHAPE` fill/stroke/text pairs, `TINT` zone palette plus `TINT_CYCLE`, the `KINDS` records, the `TYPE` scale, and the gap constants `GRID/CROSS_GAP/RANK_GAP/ZONE_GAP/ZONE_HEAD/ZONE_PAD/BOARD_PAD` — `compose-board.js` is the entire theme surface and therefore the natural extraction target for any design spec file. [SOURCE: AI Systems/Product Owner/sk-create-figjam/assets/compose-board.js:27-97]
2. Colour-by-kind is already enforced semantically, which is the packet's biggest consistency strength: each of ~30 kinds fixes shape and colour together, so a colour means the same thing on every board, and the observed boards confirm it — start/start-ish nodes are light blue, decisions light yellow, goals light green, exits light orange, data stores light violet across all seven types. [SOURCE: AI Systems/Product Owner/sk-create-figjam/references/style-guide.md:32; AI Systems/Product Owner/sk-create-figjam/assets/compose-board.js:51-83]
3. The accent system lands the first glance on every board viewed: the pink `#F849C1` 8px outline (4px on a sequence message) is a hue no kind uses, measures 3.13:1 on white, and outweighs every other mark (25.0 vs 12.6 weight×contrast) — confirmed visually on `user-flow-board.png` (Return confirmed), `sequence-board.png` (Authorise the card), `gantt-board.png` (critical bar), `er-board.png` (Booking), `architecture-board.png` (sync-service) and `process-board.png` (Approve the claim). [SOURCE: AI Systems/Product Owner/sk-create-figjam/references/style-guide.md:135-152]
4. The palette is deliberately FigJam-native: each kind uses a swatch pair the check accepts and the self-test re-measures, because a colour FigJam recognises as a palette swatch stays recolourable from the picker; a design spec that introduces custom hexes would silently break that property unless it keeps to pair-shaped tokens. [SOURCE: AI Systems/Product Owner/sk-create-figjam/references/style-guide.md:103-120; AI Systems/Product Owner/sk-create-figjam/references/style-guide.md:156]
5. Sequence is the weakest rendered board at 19/22, and the image shows why: `ROW` is a fixed 72 px so seven messages stretch a tall, airy column whose right side (PartnerHub) is nearly empty, participant heads and feet duplicate every label's visual weight, and the self-message loops are cramped at `SELF_DROP` 40 px with labels tight to the lifeline. [SOURCE: AI Systems/Product Owner/sk-create-figjam/assets/screenshots/sequence-board.png; AI Systems/Product Owner/sk-create-figjam/assets/compose-board.js:933-961]
6. Gantt `planned` bars are white fill with a thin gray stroke, so at overview they read as unfilled outlines rather than scheduled work; on `gantt-board.png` only planned/critical/milestone appear, leaving the chart visually hollow except for the one accent bar — bar fill is the weakest hierarchy channel on this type. [SOURCE: AI Systems/Product Owner/sk-create-figjam/assets/screenshots/gantt-board.png; AI Systems/Product Owner/sk-create-figjam/references/style-guide.md:78]
7. Zone tints are near-white (`#F5FBFF`, `#FFF7F0` etc.) and barely separate at overview zoom; grouping is actually carried by the 48 px zone gap, the border and the 24 px semi-bold zone label — on `architecture-board.png` all six cycle tints are present and only just distinguishable, and on `state-board.png` the composite "Confirmed" zone reads mostly through its label, not its tint. [SOURCE: AI Systems/Product Owner/sk-create-figjam/assets/compose-board.js:42-48; AI Systems/Product Owner/sk-create-figjam/assets/screenshots/architecture-board.png; AI Systems/Product Owner/sk-create-figjam/assets/screenshots/state-board.png]
8. The connector solver, not the colour constants, owns label legibility: a port-pricing search (STUB/CLEAR/BLOCKED/SHARED/CLASH/LAP…, 6 seeded restarts, pair-wise sweeps) picks routes and magnets, and every viewed board shows mid-connector labels clear of lines and nodes — this layer must stay algorithmic and outside any spec file's parametrisation. [SOURCE: AI Systems/Product Owner/sk-create-figjam/assets/compose-board.js:600-840; AI Systems/Product Owner/sk-create-figjam/assets/screenshots/user-flow-board.png]
9. The key is generated from the kinds, lines and arrows the board actually uses ("one sample per kind and line the board uses"), so it names every mark and nothing else by construction — the strongest piece of self-consistent furniture; a spec may restyle samples but should never parametrise membership. [SOURCE: AI Systems/Product Owner/sk-create-figjam/assets/compose-board.js:1142-1194; AI Systems/Product Owner/sk-create-figjam/references/quality-rubric.md:136]
10. Board furniture is identical across all seven types — title 64 Bold top-left, subtitle 28 Regular wrapped to the board's width band, gray `Key` section beneath the diagram, 16 px source line, white board section — which is why the family resemblance holds even where kind vocabularies diverge. [SOURCE: AI Systems/Product Owner/sk-create-figjam/assets/compose-board.js:1123-1202; AI Systems/Product Owner/sk-create-figjam/references/style-guide.md:193-200]
11. The type scale is one family at one ramp everywhere: Inter at 64/28/24/20/16 and 14-16 for fields, pins and bar lengths, with weight (Bold/Semi Bold/Medium/Regular) doing the hierarchy work — confirmed constant across all seven screenshots. [SOURCE: AI Systems/Product Owner/sk-create-figjam/references/style-guide.md:191-200]
12. Spacing has two layers that a spec file must not conflate: the documented judgment bands (60-92 px one-thought / 200-400 px same-topic / 1000+ px separate-content) govern what humans and notes place, while the composer enforces its own fixed steps (80 cross, 136 rank, 48/96 zone pad/head, 80 board pad, 64 above key) — only the second layer is tokenisable. [SOURCE: AI Systems/Product Owner/sk-create-figjam/references/style-guide.md:221-234; AI Systems/Product Owner/sk-create-figjam/assets/compose-board.js:91-97]
13. ER is the chromatically flattest type by design: entity cards are all white with gray strokes and hierarchy rests on the semi-bold card title, the PK/FK field list and the accent alone — "colour never carries meaning alone" is satisfied, but the type contributes the least colour information of the seven. [SOURCE: AI Systems/Product Owner/sk-create-figjam/assets/screenshots/er-board.png; AI Systems/Product Owner/sk-create-figjam/references/style-guide.md:76]
14. The packet's current stance is explicit refusal of skinning: "Skins and brand onboarding still stay behind… a brand palette would turn every swatch into a custom colour", so adopting a DESIGN.md-style spec means reversing a recorded decision — the spec must therefore keep every value a FigJam-native pair or accept losing picker-recolourability. [SOURCE: AI Systems/Product Owner/sk-create-figjam/references/style-guide.md:206]
15. Deliberate divergences are per-type identity, not inconsistency: violet step pins and head/foot participants on sequence, 1 px grid rules and section bands on gantt, ringed final dots and composite-state zones on state, lane-ordering on architecture — what makes the types differ is kind vocabulary and layout mode, not furniture, palette grammar or type scale. [SOURCE: AI Systems/Product Owner/sk-create-figjam/assets/compose-board.js:921-927,1008-1013,1065-1084; AI Systems/Product Owner/sk-create-figjam/references/style-guide.md:68-81]

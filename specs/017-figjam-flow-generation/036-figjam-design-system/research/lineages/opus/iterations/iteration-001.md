## Findings

1. The zone tint cycle includes pink, so an architecture lane is tinted in the accent's hue, which breaks the style guide's promise that pink belongs to the accent alone. `TINT_CYCLE` ends with `'pink'` [SOURCE: AI Systems/Product Owner/sk-create-figjam/assets/compose-board.js:48] while the guide says "The only pink and the only heavy line on a board" [SOURCE: AI Systems/Product Owner/sk-create-figjam/references/style-guide.md:91] and "The light pink pair belongs to the accent alone" [SOURCE: AI Systems/Product Owner/sk-create-figjam/references/style-guide.md:139]. Visible on `architecture-board.png`: the External lane is pink-tinted with a pink border, on the same board as the pink sync-service accent.
2. Light blue carries five different meanings across types (start, gateway, first state, participant, gantt in-progress), so the style guide's claim that "a colour means the same thing on every board" holds within a type but not across them. [SOURCE: AI Systems/Product Owner/sk-create-figjam/assets/compose-board.js:52] [SOURCE: AI Systems/Product Owner/sk-create-figjam/assets/compose-board.js:65] [SOURCE: AI Systems/Product Owner/sk-create-figjam/assets/compose-board.js:70] [SOURCE: AI Systems/Product Owner/sk-create-figjam/assets/compose-board.js:77] [SOURCE: AI Systems/Product Owner/sk-create-figjam/assets/compose-board.js:79] against [SOURCE: AI Systems/Product Owner/sk-create-figjam/references/style-guide.md:32]. On `sequence-board.png` every participant, head and foot, is light blue, so eight blue pills dominate the first glance ahead of the pink focus message.
3. Zone tints collide with kind fills of the same hue inside them. The tint cycle is positional (blue, orange, teal, ...) [SOURCE: AI Systems/Product Owner/sk-create-figjam/assets/compose-board.js:593] while kinds have fixed hues, so on `user-flow-board.png` the orange exit "Contact support" sits in the orange-tinted "Request the return" zone, on `architecture-board.png` the violet Blocks Database sits in a violet-tinted Stores lane and the orange Change Stream in a yellow Events lane. Hue then says two things at once: "this zone" and "this kind". The rubric asks that colour name a kind the key explains and nothing be coloured by position [SOURCE: AI Systems/Product Owner/sk-create-figjam/references/quality-rubric.md:132], yet the zone tint is positional by construction.
4. Zone tops are ragged on group-mode boards, because each zone box is fit to its own members [SOURCE: AI Systems/Product Owner/sk-create-figjam/assets/compose-board.js:496-509] with no shared top line. `user-flow-board.png`: "Request the return" starts about 30 px above its two neighbours. `dense-flow-board.png`: "Settle the price" starts about 90 px above "Find a room". `architecture-board.png`: five lanes start at five different heights (Edge, Services, Events, Stores, External). Ragged tops read as accidental and weaken the grid the rest of the board keeps.
5. Edge labels and key labels are 16 px against 20 px node labels and a 64 px title [SOURCE: AI Systems/Product Owner/sk-create-figjam/assets/compose-board.js:90], so at overview zoom on wide boards they fall to the edge of legibility. On `user-flow-board.png` and `dense-flow-board.png` (both 2048 px wide renders) "Yes", "No", "Courier pickup", "Tap Change dates" and the key entries read noticeably smaller than any other text, and the title-to-label ratio is 4:1. The rubric's legibility criterion is judged at overview zoom [SOURCE: AI Systems/Product Owner/sk-create-figjam/references/quality-rubric.md:134].
6. The key's width is whatever its samples need and it is always left-aligned [SOURCE: AI Systems/Product Owner/sk-create-figjam/assets/compose-board.js:1147-1148] [SOURCE: AI Systems/Product Owner/sk-create-figjam/assets/compose-board.js:1194], so on `state-board.png`, `er-board.png`, `gantt-board.png` and `dense-flow-board.png` a large empty region sits to its right, and its right edge aligns with nothing above it. The rubric's proportion criterion penalises "a large empty region" [SOURCE: AI Systems/Product Owner/sk-create-figjam/references/quality-rubric.md:137].
7. Key entries follow first-use order of node kinds [SOURCE: AI Systems/Product Owner/sk-create-figjam/assets/compose-board.js:851-853], which is model order rather than any fixed per-type order. On `state-board.png` the key reads "Final state, State, Start, First state, End", while `state-on-existing-board.png` of nearly the same model reads "Start, First state, State, End, Final state". Two boards of one type thus have different keys; gantt alone uses a fixed order [SOURCE: AI Systems/Product Owner/sk-create-figjam/assets/compose-board.js:1059].
8. The sequence board uses a different line language from the graph boards: requests are charcoal with a closed equilateral head [SOURCE: AI Systems/Product Owner/sk-create-figjam/assets/compose-board.js:921-927], while every other type draws gray 2 px lines with open ARROW_LINES heads [SOURCE: AI Systems/Product Owner/sk-create-figjam/assets/compose-board.js:218-220]. The difference is semantic within sequence (request vs async) but no cross-type rule records it, and side by side `sequence-board.png` reads darker and heavier than `user-flow-board.png`.
9. Long edges detour around zone boxes and over the top of the board, landing close to the subtitle. On `state-board.png` "check out" runs above the Confirmed zone at about 50 px under the subtitle; on `architecture-board.png` "Writes Blocks" rises above Events and drops into Stores; on `dense-flow-board.png` "Yes" climbs out over the decision and back down. The spacing rules forbid riding a zone border and running through foreign zones [SOURCE: AI Systems/Product Owner/sk-create-figjam/references/style-guide.md:234] but set no clearance from the title block, so the 64 px gap between subtitle and diagram [SOURCE: AI Systems/Product Owner/sk-create-figjam/assets/compose-board.js:1139] is the only buffer.
10. Entity cards in one rank are centre-aligned although their heights differ, so tops are ragged and an edge into a tall card jogs. `er-board.png`: Guest, Booking and Payment sit at three different top lines, and "makes (1 to 0..n)" bends up and into Booking's top rather than running straight. Card height comes from field count [SOURCE: AI Systems/Product Owner/sk-create-figjam/assets/compose-board.js:547-555] and placement centres every box on its rank [SOURCE: AI Systems/Product Owner/sk-create-figjam/assets/compose-board.js:451-456].
11. The subtitle wraps at the diagram width clamped to 960-1440 px [SOURCE: AI Systems/Product Owner/sk-create-figjam/assets/compose-board.js:1131], which leaves single-word orphans: "Engineering" alone on line 2 of `architecture-board.png`, "engineers" on `sequence-board.png`. The style guide asks for one sentence [SOURCE: AI Systems/Product Owner/sk-create-figjam/references/style-guide.md:196] but sets no measure that avoids a widow.

## Ruled out

- Inter at four weights is not a problem; the hierarchy title Bold / zone Semi Bold / node Medium / body Regular reads cleanly on every board.
- The 8 px accent outline is not too heavy; it is the board's one loud mark by design and works.

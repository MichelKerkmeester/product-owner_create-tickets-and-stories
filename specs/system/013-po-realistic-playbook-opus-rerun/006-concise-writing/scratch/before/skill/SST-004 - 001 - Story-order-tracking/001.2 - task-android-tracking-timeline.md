# FE - Android - TRACK - Tracking timeline and delivery estimate

### About

---

Show the tracking timeline and the delivery estimate on the order page in the Android app, one timeline per parcel, so a customer can see where the parcel is and when it should arrive without going to the parcel carrier's site. The layout follows the `Order page / Tracking timeline` design frame.

### Requirements

---

1.  **Tracking timeline**

---

The timeline replaces today's fixed `Order placed` and `Shipped` display for parcel items. `Packed` comes from orders-service, which already receives it from the warehouse system, and the carrier statuses come from the tracking webhook.

**Checklist**

- [ ] One timeline per parcel, with that parcel's items under it
- [ ] Statuses in order: `Order placed`, `Packed`, `Shipped`, `Out for delivery`, `Delivered`, `Delivery failed`
- [ ] Every step that happened shows with its date and time
- [ ] `Delivery failed` shows no reason, even when the carrier sends one
- [ ] After `Delivery failed`, a new attempt shows `Out for delivery` as current and keeps the failed attempt in the timeline
- [ ] Layout matches the `Order page / Tracking timeline` design frame

---

2.  **Delivery estimate**

---

**Checklist**

- [ ] The day and window show under the current status, in the format `Arriving Thursday 1 October` and `Between 10:00 and 14:00`
- [ ] No estimate shows when the carrier has sent no window

---

3.  **Pallet items and older orders**

---

**Checklist**

- [ ] Pallet items keep today's page plus the line `The delivery company will call you to book a delivery slot`
- [ ] An order delivered more than `90 days` ago shows the last status only

---

4.  **Translation**

---

**Checklist**

- [ ] Status and estimate text is translated for `nl-NL`, `nl-BE`, `fr-BE`, `de-DE`, `fr-FR` and `en-GB`

# v1.0.16.0, Status Lanes and Detail Cards

A spec board drawn by hand outside Flow Mode showed five gaps. Two parties moved one shared record through a series of statuses, and the skill had no lane for the statuses. A spec reader needed what the source said about each step, and a step could only carry a one-line label. A screenshot taken after a later write showed the board as it stood before that write. The subtitle said who reads the board, which helped less than where the flow starts and ends. No example showed that board shape. This release closes each one.

## What's New at a Glance

- **A status lane on a laned process.** `%% status: <lane id>` names one lane as the record's statuses. It is declared first and drawn on top, its statuses run in one `-->` chain and are drawn as white chevrons, and a step moves the record with a dotted gray `-.->` the key names `Moves the status`. Every status after the first is reached by a move, and no status takes the accent. A status move is no way out of a step, so an end may move the last status and stay an end
- **Detail cards at the Faithful level.** `%% level: faithful` and `%% detail: <step id> | <line> | <line>` give a step a body of at most 10 lines of 44 characters or fewer. The label stays on one line in Bold, a 10 pixel spacer follows and the body takes the new `body` type token, 16 Regular at 145% line height. A laned process with detail cards reads at 18 nodes and 24 connectors, and a body below Faithful is refused
- **A read-back judges a fresh view.** A view after any write but the composing call is taken with `node.screenshot()` inside a `use_figma` call, and a view that still shows the board before the change is reported `stale` and taken again
- **The subtitle names the start and the end.** It names where the flow starts and where it ends, and the reader when the brief names one. An ER model names what it covers instead
- **A worked example of that board.** `assets/examples/process-fernhouse-commission.md` walks a client and a maker moving one planter commission through five statuses, with detail cards on three steps, and `assets/templates/process.md` gains a status lane pattern

## Changed

- The check reads the status lane, the detail lines and the level, and holds the path, the ways in and out and the goal on the work alone, without the status lane
- The composer draws the status kind, the dotted status move and the detail card, and its design gains the `status` kind, the `body` type token with a floor from `small` to `node` and the `statusMove` key entry
- The compact code drops a single arrow parameter's brackets and a semicolon or comma before a closing brace, so the whole code is 47,390 characters and the Project keeps 2,628 for its model, more than before this release
- The wrap advice skips a card, whose lines are set one by one
- An architecture refuses `%% status` and `%% detail` lines, which belong to a laned process
- The state, sequence, gantt and architecture examples name where their flow starts and ends in their subtitle
- The playbook gains FJ-046, a status lane checked and drawn, FJ-047, detail cards only at Faithful, and FJ-048, a read-back that judges a view taken after the write

# v1.0.14.0, Row Decisions and Closing Questions

Claude Sonnet 5.5 high reran FJ-017, FJ-022, FJ-023 and FJ-036 on v1.0.13.0. On FJ-023 both readers read the decision beside the accent before the entry the `Glance:` line put second. FJ-036's first intake round ended on a `Why:` line. FJ-017's scenario asked for a `Finding:` line the skill never gives. The trace drew decision diamonds 24 px wider than FigJam does and read their text box narrower than FigJam fits it, so the check passed a label whose last word FigJam stranded. FJ-022 ran the check before its playback with the reader assumed. This release fixes each one.

## What's New at a Glance

- **A decision beside the accent reads second.** The `Glance:` line puts a decision joined to the accent by one line, on the accent's own row (its own column on a top-down flow) with no node between them, right after the accent. A second node in the accent fill is still named once. The quality rubric plans the glance lines the same way
- **Every intake round ends on one closing question.** It comes after the round's last block, such as `Take the recommended answers, or correct any of them?`, so each `Why:` line stays inside its block
- **Diamond labels are read at FigJam's size.** The trace sets each line at a whole-pixel height, so a 26-character decision traces at the 336 by 216 diamond FigJam draws. The check reads a diamond's label at half its width less 8 px, the text box a live render measured
- **The playback names the dials.** It names the detail level, the audience and the distance by their words before the model is written, and the gate need not repeat them

## Changed

- The composer keeps every edge label 4 px clear of a zone title, the clearance the check measures. A whole-pixel title line had left one planted flow's label on the last pixel of a zone title at the default and the largest type sizes
- `references/hybrid-workflow.md` Section 3 says the check gives the second of two stacked stickies the side below or to the left of its node and names it on its `Sides:` line, so the gate shows it
- The user flow example reads `Within 30 days after delivery?` and `Drop-off or courier pickup?`, in the source's words, so the check prints no `Label:` line at the measured diamond width. The process example's `Glance:` line and second glance put `Damage clear in the photo?` right after the accent
- New self-tests hold the lifted decision on the FJ-023 shape, the plain order on the FJ-036 shape where a node stands between, a second node in the accent fill named once, a diamond label read at the measured width and the 336 px diamond
- The playbook's Composition row scores 2 when the planned order holds with the requested element first, 1 for one part out of its planned place or one unplanned part at or before the second glance and 0 for two or more or a requested element out of its planned place. A swapped pair costs only the part read earlier than its place
- Playbook Section 5 fails an intake round whose last line is not its closing question and a playback that leaves out a dial. Under Standard energy, when the sources leave the reader unsettled or more than two rows assumed, a run that skips the turn 1 intake round or models before the playback's answer misses the playback signal
- FJ-017 asks for a `Finding:` line only for a sticky that left the side its `Sides:` line named. FJ-022 expects the turn 1 intake round its prompt calls for, since it leaves the reader unstated, and answers it with its own scripted turn

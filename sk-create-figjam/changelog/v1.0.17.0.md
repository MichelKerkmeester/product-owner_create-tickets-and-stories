# v1.0.17.0, Boards That Teach

The gallery boards were tidy but told a reader little more than the request did. A card held one short line, so "Pick a reason" never said which reasons there are. A zone held only a name such as "Done". The key took about a fifth of the board, and one node was marked "In focus" with no reason. A board drawn by hand for a chat prompt spec read far better because each card carried its button, its steps and its result, and each section said where it started and ended. This release brings those habits into the skill.

## What's New at a Glance

- **Notes on zones and lanes.** `%% about: <zone label or lane id> | <line> | <line>` puts up to three note lines of 56 characters or fewer under a zone's or a lane's title, such as `Starts:`, `Ends:` and `Good to know:`. A user flow or a process with two or more zones gives every zone notes unless it runs at `Simplified`, and a zone too narrow for its notes widens to them
- **Detail cards at Balanced too, and on more types.** A body under a one-line label is allowed at `Balanced`, up to 4 lines, and at `Faithful`, up to 10, on a user flow, a process, an architecture and a state diagram. `Simplified` keeps one-line labels
- **Every accent names its reason.** A styled step or decision needs `%% focus: <id> because <reason>`, and every focus line on any type needs its reason, which the key draws as `In focus: <reason>`
- **A compact key.** The key is one band: its title leads the row and the samples sit beside it at small type, 88 pixels tall for one row where it took 176

## Changed

- The check refuses a body at `Simplified`, a body over its level's line count, a zone with no notes on a Balanced or Faithful zoned flow, an `%% about:` line that names no zone or lane or runs long, and an accent with no reason
- The composer grows every zone's head by the most note lines any zone carries, so zones side by side stay level, and gives a stage zone the room its notes need in the gaps beside it
- `compose_board.py --template` takes `graph` or `sequence,gantt`, and the Project's composer carries those two code blocks instead of one, so a graph model keeps 8,188 characters of room in one call where it kept 2,628
- The quality rubric's remove test adds a line: the board teaches what its source says beyond the request
- The worked examples, the templates and their Project copies carry focus reasons, and the user flow example carries zone notes
- The playbook adds Substance, scored where a scenario names source questions, so a board that names them totals 24 and passes at 20. FJ-022 to FJ-028, FJ-032, FJ-036 and FJ-049 give the request a source excerpt and two source questions, and FJ-047 tests detail cards at Balanced and Faithful against a refusal at Simplified

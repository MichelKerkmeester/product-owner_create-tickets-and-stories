# v1.0.6.0, One Question First and Boards That Read in Greyscale

Claude Sonnet 5.5 high ran all 54 FigJam scenarios on v1.0.5.0 and closed at 23 PASS, 29 FAIL and 2 SKIP once skeptics and an adjudicator had checked its grades. Its 20 boards scored 411 of 440 points. The failures traced to rules that contradicted each other, boards that lost meaning in greyscale and playbook lines that asked for something the skill never set. This release fixes the skill and the composer.

## What's New at a Glance

- **The type question goes alone.** When two types fit, or a type the user names does not fit what the brief describes, the first reply asks that one question, naming both types and the recommended one, and the reader round follows its answer. It is the one question outside the round count
- **One board means one board.** When the user asked for one board, the lowest-priority part is cut and named under `Left out:`, and the split is offered in the playback as the alternative. The ladder, the budget rule, the cut order and all seven type references agree
- **Quick reaches the playback.** Under Quick energy the draw-ready threshold is met once every row is filled and each inferred row is marked assumed, however many rows that is. A recommendation the user accepts stays assumed, and the next reply is the playback
- **Kinds read in greyscale.** Kinds that share a shape and sit close in greyscale also differ by outline: a goal, a final state, a gateway and a critical bar take a 4 pixel outline, an exit, an outside system and a bar in progress a dashed one and an error both. `--design-check` fails a design where two such kinds differ by colour alone
- **The key explains every line and the focus.** A plain one-way connector takes a `Leads to` entry, and `%% focus: <id> because <reason>` puts the reason in the key as `In focus: <reason>`, 30 characters at most. A user flow or a process takes that line on the node its `style` line marks

## Changed

- Edges that arrive at one side of a node each take their own point 24 pixels apart, and a gantt arrow runs from a bar's right end onto the top of the next bar's left end
- A zone never takes the tint of a zone it faces along its row or its column, however wide a label made the gap between them
- A label the router would have hidden under a split connector now sits on a leg long enough to hold it
- A brief that spans several flows is read back as one recommended scope, with the other flows named under `Left out:` as later boards
- On a revision the changed element takes the accent, and on an extension that pins several problems the accent stays on the goal. The rubric also asks the board to say why the accent is there
- A sequence message the caller sends without waiting for an answer is async even when the brief does not say fire-and-forget. A process log write that runs today is drawn solid, and the step in focus on a handoff is the sending step
- A note pin on a diamond sits on its top vertex
- A file the user named is read once before the gate, and the gate names any section on it that shares the board's title and recommends a numbered title for the new board
- A composite state section reads first in the overview, so it is planned as the first glance or holds the focus
- Gantt arrows into a short bar or a milestone space out to fit it, and a choice or a milestone is where arrivals meet. `flow_check.py` refuses a flow focus line the composer refuses
- With no Figma route the reply prints no `Read-back:` line, and the no-read-tool line applies only once a board was written
- The scratch folder is made with `mktemp -d`, and its path stays in the conversation
- The compact composer joins short lines, so `--template` prints 155 lines and 47,545 characters, up from 47,148. The Project's whole-composer call leaves about 2,470 characters for the model and the `THEME` line. `compose_board.py --self-test` runs 1,240 checks and `flow_check.py --self-test` runs 163
- The playbook's eight routing scenarios grep a `TYPE_SIGNALS` row that exists, and FJ-005, FJ-011, FJ-033 and FJ-037 now ask for what the skill sets. Section 3 defines meaning lost in greyscale and keeps the title block out of the glance order. A `driver:` key marks seven scenarios `operator-only` or `new-file`. FJ-030 accepts a pin on a diamond's top vertex, and FJ-041 expects the reader round after the type answer when the run opens one

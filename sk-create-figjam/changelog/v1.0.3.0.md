# v1.0.3.0, Board Design and One Family

The board's look lived in constants inside the composer, so a team that wanted its goals teal had to edit code, and the seven diagram types had drifted apart: a key in a different order on each type, sequence requests in a colour no other connector used and zones that took the colour of the steps inside them. The look now lives in one design file, and the composer draws every type from it.

## What's New at a Glance

- **A design a team can edit.** `assets/figjam-design.md` holds every board's style as one json token block: the FigJam pair each of 17 colour roles draws, the role and key words of each kind, the first-glance accent and its weights, a type family with seven sizes, the line colours and weight, the zone tint cycle and the key's words. Edit it and every board composed after it follows. `scripts/compose_board.py` reads it over the composer's built-in design and sends only the tokens that differ, so the shipped design costs 2 characters
- **A bad edit never stops a board.** A token that misses a floor, such as a pink zone tint, a role on the accent's own pair or a type size under 14, falls back to its shipped value with a named `DESIGN` line, one token at a time until every floor holds, and the `COMPOSED` line counts the warnings. A block that does not parse, or a file the reader cannot handle for any other reason, falls back whole. Flow Mode turns each `DESIGN` line into a `Finding:` line in the reply
- **`--design-check`.** `compose_board.py --design-check` prints the resolved design, each role's measured contrast, the accent's weights and nearest hue, the zone tints, the `THEME` it sends and the room left for the model, and exits 1 on any design warning
- **One family across seven types.** The key lists its entries in one order on every board of a type with the focus last, spans the diagram's width and wraps. Every connector is gray but an async message and the accent. Zone and gantt band tints skip pink, any hue a node inside them draws and the tint of any zone beside them. Edge labels, key entries and the source line are 18 pixels, and bar text and step pins share a 14 pixel step

## Changed

- Sequence participants are white, the participant feet are smaller, requests are gray with a closed head and each step pin sits beside its sender's lifeline in the new `marker` role
- Zones that share a row line up at the top unless an edge skips a zone in that row, and so do the cards of an ER diagram
- An edge routed outside the nodes widens the diagram instead of running into the title or the board's margin
- The subtitle wraps into even lines, so its last line no longer holds one word
- A planned gantt bar takes the connector gray outline, and gantt section names are measured at the size they are drawn
- The board's tag records the design that drew it
- `references/quality-rubric.md` gains a ninth criterion, Matches the active design, for a total of 18, and two Section 6 lines on the design. The scorecard's Signal reads the design file and its tint list drops pink
- Two playbook scenarios: FJ-044 composes the seven examples side by side and compares their furniture, and FJ-045 breaks the design on purpose and expects three named findings
- The seven type references name colour roles and point at the design file rather than restating colours, and the style guide records the shipped design
- `flow_check.py --self-test` no longer reads the design file as a model
- The floors cover what an edit could otherwise make unreadable: async messages and lifelines keep 2:1 on white, the unlabelled start, end, merge and fork dots keep 3:1, the key's tint stays off the accent's hue, edge labels and fields stay no larger than node type, zone names stay at 24 pixels or under, the subtitle no larger than the title, the zone cycle never holds the key's tint and the design's name stays within 40 characters of text. A zone the cycle cannot fit repeats a member's hue with a warning rather than taking the key's gray. A design file saved with Windows line endings reads as it would with Unix ones
- A family whose third weight FigJam names `SemiBold` loads as it is, where it fell back to Inter before
- The compact composer grew from 43,623 to 46,569 characters, so the model JSON and the `THEME` line have about 3,400 characters of room in one `use_figma` call, down from about 6,400. The largest shipped example model runs about 2,300. A compose that runs over the limit says how much of it a team design's `THEME` takes
- `compose_board.py --self-test` also proves the Project's design file carries the skill's token block and the `THEME` line the design check prints, both font fallbacks and every new floor

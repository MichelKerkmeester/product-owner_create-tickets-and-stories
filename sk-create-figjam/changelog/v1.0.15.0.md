# v1.0.15.0, Reading Order and Reply Lead

Claude Sonnet 5.5 high reran FJ-017, FJ-022, FJ-023 and FJ-036 on v1.0.14.0 and 2 of 4 passed. On FJ-023 both readers read the entry before the decision the `Glance:` line had lifted beside the accent, and across the 18 reader orders of three runs that lift fit the minority. FJ-022 opened its delivery reply on a sentence announcing itself above the board URL. FJ-023 skipped its turn 1 intake round and drew the runbook's queue as a ninth node, while its scenario still expected a turn 1 playback and 8 nodes. Five of the seven examples left the reader unnamed and opened on a playback. The label trace counted characters, so it passed `New price compared to old?`, whose last word FigJam stranded. Two grade records misread reader orders. This release fixes each one.

## What's New at a Glance

- **The glance order is plain.** The `Glance:` line puts the accent first and then goes column by column along the flow and down each column. The quality rubric says the printed order is the majority read, never a promise, and charges a part only when both readers misplace it
- **The reply opens on the board URL.** Everything written after the last tool call is the reply and its first characters are the board URL, with no status line and no sentence announcing the reply. The flow templates carry a wrong and a right first line, and the run harness flags a delivery whose first line is not the URL
- **A queue between two lanes is the handoff.** The process type draws a queue that only carries work from one lane to the next as the crossing connector's label, and keeps the cylinder for a store that steps read and write on their own
- **Diamond labels are measured by glyph width.** The check wraps a decision's label by Inter Medium advance widths at half the diamond's width less 4 px and reports a stranded last word as `leaves`
- **Dials are not rows.** The detail level, the audience and the reading distance take their recommended default and never count toward the two assumed rows, while a reader no source names stays unsettled

## Changed

- FJ-023 scripts the turn 1 intake round its prompt calls for, as FJ-022 does, and keeps 8 of 8 nodes and 8 of 8 connectors
- The process and user flow examples show an intake round before their playback, and their playbacks name all three dials. The gantt, ER and sequence examples name their reader in the request. The process example's `Glance:` line and second glance follow the plain order
- The playbook's Composition row marks a part out of place when it falls outside a longest common subsequence of the plan and the reader's order, and a tie goes to the part read earlier than its planned place. Playbook Section 5 states that dials are not rows
- The run harness gains `run/composition_check.py`, which scores Composition from the plan's `Glance:` line and the two readers' `Order:` lines, and `grades/reader-brief.md`, which asks each reader to end the notes on that line. The completeness check reruns the script and reads no reader prose
- New self-tests hold a decision in the accent's lane read in its column after an entry to its left, a decision joined to the accent keeping its column place, the stranded labels FigJam drew in 336 px diamonds and a 27-character label that clears at the glyph measure

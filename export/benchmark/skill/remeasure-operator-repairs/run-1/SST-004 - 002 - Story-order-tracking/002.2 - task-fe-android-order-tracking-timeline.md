# FE - Android - TRACK - Order tracking timeline

### About

---

The order page in the Android app gets a status timeline for each parcel. The timeline moves as the parcel moves and shows the expected delivery day when the carrier sends one. Today the page shows `Order placed` until dispatch and `Shipped` after it, and never changes after that.

Customers who cannot see when a parcel will arrive copy the tracking number into the carrier's site, and the ones who get lost there contact us. In August that made up 31% of all contacts.

The app shows the statuses, dates and delivery windows the BE tracking webhook task provides, and maps no carrier codes itself.

### Requirements

---

### **Timeline**

---

1.  **Status timeline**

---

Each parcel shows where it is, so the customer does not need the carrier's site to find out.

**Checklist**

- [ ] Statuses run in this order: `Order placed`, `Packed`, `Shipped`, `Out for delivery`, `Delivered`, `Delivery failed`
- [ ] Every step that happened shows with its date and time
- [ ] `Delivery failed` shows no reason
- [ ] After `Delivery failed`, a new `Out for delivery` becomes the current status and the failed step stays in the timeline
- [ ] The layout follows the `Order page / Tracking timeline` frame

**User Story**

- **Given:** a customer has a shipped order
- **When:** the carrier scans the parcel and the customer opens the order page
- **Then:** the page shows the new status, with every earlier step and its date and time

---

2.  **Delivery estimate**

---

The customer sees when the parcel arrives, but only when the carrier has said so.

**Checklist**

- [ ] With a delivery window, the day and the window show under the current status, as `Arriving Thursday 1 October` and `Between 10:00 and 14:00` in the design mock
- [ ] With no delivery window, no estimate shows, and none is worked out from the dispatch date
- [ ] The estimate follows the newest window the BE tracking webhook task provides

---

3.  **Orders with several parcels**

---

**Checklist**

- [ ] The order page shows one timeline per parcel, with that parcel's items under it

---

### **Edge cases**

---

4.  **Pallet items**

---

The pallet carrier sends no tracking events, so pallet items keep today's page.

**Checklist**

- [ ] Items over `30 kg` or `120 cm` keep today's page plus the line `The delivery company will call you to book a delivery slot`

---

5.  **Tracking history**

---

**Checklist**

- [ ] After tracking events expire, `90 days` after delivery, the order page shows the last status only

---

6.  **Translations**

---

**Checklist**

- [ ] Status text and the two estimate lines show in all six locales: `nl-NL`, `nl-BE`, `fr-BE`, `de-DE`, `fr-FR` and `en-GB`

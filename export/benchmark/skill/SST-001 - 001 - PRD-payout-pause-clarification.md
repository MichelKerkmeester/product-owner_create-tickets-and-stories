# Payout pause - clarification

The PRD routes as a Story in the house format. To draft it I need:

1. The notes themselves, pasted into the reply or named as a file
2. Who the pause feature is for, and what they can do once it ships (for example: a creator pauses payouts while away)
3. Whether you want a Delivery section, which adds Estimation, Rabbit holes and No-gos to the artifact

Answer in one reply and drafting starts immediately. If the feature spans several feature areas rather than one, say so in the same reply and the artifact becomes an Epic with a Scope instead

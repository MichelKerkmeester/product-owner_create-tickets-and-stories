### Requirements

---

1.  **Tracking timeline**

---

It replaces the fixed `Order placed` and `Shipped` for parcel items, with `Packed` from orders-service and the rest from the tracking webhook.

**Checklist**

- [] One timeline per parcel, with its items under it
- [] Statuses in order: `Order placed`, `Packed`, `Shipped`, `Out for delivery`, `Delivered`, `Delivery failed`
- [] Past steps show date and time
- [] `Delivery failed` never shows a reason
- [] A new attempt after `Delivery failed` shows `Out for delivery`, keeping the failed one
- [] Layout matches the `Order page / Tracking timeline` design frame

---

2.  **Delivery estimate**

---

**Checklist**

- [] Day and window sit under the current status as `Arriving Thursday 1 October` and `Between 10:00 and 14:00`
- [] No window, no estimate

---

3.  **Pallet items and older orders**

---

**Checklist**

- [] Pallet items keep today's page plus `The delivery company will call you to book a delivery slot`
- [] Over `90 days` after delivery, only the last status shows

---

4.  **Translation**

---

**Checklist**

- [] Status and estimate text translated for `nl-NL`, `nl-BE`, `fr-BE`, `de-DE`, `fr-FR` and `en-GB`

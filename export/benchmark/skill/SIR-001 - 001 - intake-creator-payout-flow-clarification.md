Welcome! Let's create exactly what you need for the creator payout flow.

Please provide the following information at once:

**0. How should I work this?**
- Quick - lean pass with smart defaults, minimal back-and-forth
- Deeper - take more time, read more context and apply the full phase flow

**1. Deliverable type:**
- Task - Development task with QA checklist
- Bug - Defect report with evidence and reproduction steps
- Story - Product requirements document in the Barter house format, new or refined, with numbered Given/When/Then acceptance criteria
- Doc - Product or engineering guide, behavior reference, runbook, technical reference or proposal

**2. Scope & complexity:**
- For a task: Backend/Frontend/Mobile/Full-stack/DevOps/QA, and where the payout flow touches which surfaces
- For a bug: Platform, affected payout step, severity and reproduction context
- For a story: Create or refine, the user role and value, how many requirements, and whether they share one mechanism
- For a doc: Create or refine, product/engineering/mixed domain, purpose, audience and required technical depth

**3. Requirements:**
- What needs to be built, fixed or documented in the payout flow
- Why does this matter? (problem being solved for creators or the business)
- What does success look like?

**4. Sources, authority & status:**
- For a doc: Source files or links, which source controls any conflict, and whether claims are current behavior, approved direction, proposal, retired material or unknown
- For tasks and bugs: References, evidence, dependencies and relevant constraints

**5. Additional context:**
- Figma references? Platforms? (iOS / Android / Web / All)
- Related or dependent tickets?

**6. Assumptions to challenge:**
- What am I likely to assume incorrectly about the payout flow?
- What "impossible" options should I consider?

The creator payout flow fits a bug, a task, a story, an epic or a doc, and each one reads differently in the backlog. Please answer the points below in one reply and I will draft from there.

**0. How should I work this?**

- Quick: lean pass with smart defaults and minimal back-and-forth
- Deeper: more time, more context read and the full phase flow

**1. What is the situation?**

Pick the closest one:

- Something in the payout flow is broken or behaves unexpectedly (Bug)
- One piece of work with a clear outcome, such as a single change or screen (Task)
- A behaviour change or new capability for a role, written as requirements with acceptance criteria (Story)
- A larger initiative split across several child stories, with a goal (Epic)
- A write-up of how payouts work today, or a proposal for how they should work (Doc)

**2. Scope and value:**

- Which part of the payout flow does this cover, and on which platforms?
- Who is affected, and what problem does it cause them today?
- What does success look like once it ships?

**3. Evidence and sources:**

- For a bug: what happens, what should happen, steps to reproduce, and any screenshots or logs
- For a task, story or epic: Figma links, governing docs and exact values such as amounts, limits and timings
- For a doc: which files or links I may use, which source controls if they disagree, and whether each claim is current behavior, approved direction, proposal, retired material or unknown

**4. Constraints and dependencies:**

- Related or blocking tickets
- Deadlines, compliance or finance rules that shape the flow

**5. Assumptions to challenge:**

- What am I likely to assume incorrectly about the payout flow?
- Is there an option that looks off the table but deserves a look?

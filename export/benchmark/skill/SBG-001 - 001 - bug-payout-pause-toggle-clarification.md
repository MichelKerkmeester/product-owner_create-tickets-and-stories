### Questions before the bug report

To write the payout pause toggle bug so QA can reproduce and verify it, please answer these together:

1. **Where does it happen?** Which screen or flow holds the toggle, and who uses it (creator, brand or admin)?
2. **Steps to reproduce.** What do you do, in order, from opening the screen to seeing the toggle return to off?
3. **When does it revert?** Immediately after you switch it on, after a page refresh, after leaving and returning, or after some time passes?
4. **Was the change saved?** Did you see any confirmation, error or loading state, and does the payout actually stay active or pause?
5. **Environment.** Platform (web, iOS or Android), device, OS, browser and version, and the app version if known.
6. **Frequency.** Does it happen every time, only sometimes, or was it seen once? Does it happen on more than one account?
7. **Expected behavior.** What should the toggle do after it is switched on, and what should happen to payouts while it is on?
8. **Evidence.** Any screen recording, screenshots, logs or design reference, plus what worked before if this used to behave correctly.

# Guest loyalty points - clarification

Leadership keeps asking whether guests could earn loyalty points on their stays. Before I write anything for the backlog, please answer the points below in one reply.

**0. How should I work this?**
- Quick: lean pass with smart defaults and little back-and-forth
- Deeper: more time, more context and the full review

**1. What leadership is asking for**

The Roamstay context file says there is no loyalty or points scheme today: guests earn no points, and there are no member tiers or member-only prices. Is leadership still deciding whether to build one, or has it decided and now wants the work planned? Pick the closest fit:
- Still deciding: a Doc in the Proposal shape, setting out options and open questions for leadership to decide on
- Decided, whole programme: an Epic, `Epic - Guest - Loyalty`, with a Goal and a list of child stories
- Decided, first slice only: a Story, such as earning points on a completed stay
- Smaller than any of these, such as a discovery task to size the idea

**2. What points should achieve**
- Which problem should points solve: repeat bookings, more signed-in guests or something else?
- How would you know it worked, and is there a target number?

**3. Scope of the first version**
- Earning only, or spending points too, and if spending, on money off at checkout or member-only prices?
- Are member tiers in scope?
- When do points land: at booking, after check-out or once the free cancellation deadline has passed?
- Guests can book with an email address only, without signing in: are they in or out?
- Who funds the points: Roamstay out of its commission, partners or both, given that partner funding brings Partner Hub and payouts into scope?
- All five currencies and every market, or a subset to start?

**4. Sources and constraints**
- Any leadership notes or numbers I should use, competitor examples included
- Known legal or finance constraints, tax treatment included, and who owns that call
- The owning squad, if one has already been agreed

**5. What I should not assume**
- For example, whether points are earned per night, per unit of currency or per booking

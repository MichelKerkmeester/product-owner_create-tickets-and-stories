# Show payout pause reason and date on the payout detail screen

### About

---

Creators need to understand why a payout was paused. The payout detail screen should show the reason for the pause and the date it was paused, so a creator can see what happened to their payout on the screen where they already check it.

### Requirements

---

**Pause reason and pause date**

---

When a payout is paused, its detail screen tells the creator why and since when. A payout that is not paused stays as it is today, because there is nothing to explain.

**Checklist**

- [ ] A paused payout shows its pause reason on the payout detail screen
- [ ] A paused payout shows its pause date on the payout detail screen
- [ ] A payout that is not paused shows no pause reason and no pause date
- [ ] A paused payout with no recorded reason or date never shows a blank field

# Show payout pause reason and date on the payout detail screen

### About

---

Creators who open a payout that has been paused have no way to see why. They cannot tell what went wrong, whether the hold relates to something they can fix, or how long the payout has been sitting. This task adds the pause reason and the pause date to the payout detail screen so a creator can understand the hold on their own.

### Requirements

---

**Payout pause details**

---

When a payout is paused, the payout detail screen shows the reason it was paused and the date the pause started. The two details appear on the payout detail screen itself, so the creator answers "why is this payout paused" without leaving the screen or asking support.

**Checklist**

- [ ] The payout detail screen shows the reason a payout was paused whenever the payout is in the paused state
- [ ] The payout detail screen shows the date the payout was paused, displayed alongside the reason
- [ ] A payout that is not paused shows neither the reason nor the pause date

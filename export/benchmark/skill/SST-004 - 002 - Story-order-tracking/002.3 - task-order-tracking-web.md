# FE - Web - TRACK - Order page tracking timeline

## About

---

The web order page shows the tracking timeline the Story describes: one timeline per parcel, moving as the parcel moves, with the expected delivery day and window under the current status when the carrier gives one. Customers stop copying the tracking number into the carrier's site to learn when a parcel arrives.

The web layout is in the same `Order page / Tracking timeline` frame as the apps. The status data comes from the carrier tracking webhook task, so this task depends on it.

### Requirements

---

1.  **Timeline**

---

The timeline lists each step the parcel has reached, in the order the Story sets.

**Checklist**

- [] The timeline shows `Order placed`, `Packed`, `Shipped`, `Out for delivery`, `Delivered` and `Delivery failed` in that order
- [] Every step that has happened shows its date and time
- [] A multi-parcel order shows one timeline per parcel, with that parcel's items under it
- [] A pallet item keeps today's page with the line `The delivery company will call you to book a delivery slot`

2.  **Delivery estimate**

---

The estimate sits under the current status and follows the window rules in the Story.

**Checklist**

- [] When the carrier sends a window, the day and the window show under the current status, as `Arriving Thursday 1 October` and `Between 10:00 and 14:00`
- [] When the carrier sends no window, no estimate shows
- [] The time zone of the window follows the Story's open question, which is not yet settled

3.  **Platform and copy**

---

**Checklist**

- [] The timeline runs in the one responsive web build on desktop and mobile browsers
- [] The timeline works in the two latest versions of each major browser
- [] Status text appears in all six locales the Story lists

4.  **Analytics**

---

**Checklist**

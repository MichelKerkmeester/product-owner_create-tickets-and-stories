# Guest loyalty points: intake questions

Roamstay has no loyalty or points scheme today: guests earn no points, and there are no member tiers or member-only prices. Anything written for this is new scope, and it stays a proposal until someone approves it. Before I draft, please answer the questions below in one reply.

**0. How should I work this?**
- Quick: a lean pass with smart defaults and little back-and-forth
- Deeper: read more context and run the full phase flow

**1. What is leadership asking for?**
- A decision document that sets out options, trade-offs and open decisions so leadership can say yes or no (Doc, proposal shape)
- An approved initiative that needs splitting into child stories (Epic)
- One first slice to build, such as earning points on completed stays (Story)
- Something else

**2. Status and authority:**
- Has anyone approved a loyalty scheme, or is it still an open question?
- Which leader or forum decides, and is there a brief, deck or message I can use as a source?
- Is `context/roamstay-context.md` the only source, and if you send more, which one wins when they disagree?

**3. Problem and value:**
- What problem should points solve, for example repeat bookings, guest retention or something else?
- What does success look like, and does leadership have a number in mind?

**4. Scope:**
- Earning only, or earning and redeeming?
- Are member tiers or member-only prices in or out?
- Which surfaces: the Guest app only, or also Partner Hub (partners seeing or funding points) and Back office (Guest Support adjusting a balance)?

**5. Rules I must not guess:**
- Who can earn: signed-in guests only, or also guests who book with just an email address?
- What earns points: nights, the amount paid or a flat amount per stay?
- Prices come in five currencies with no conversion, so a spend-based rule needs a stated basis
- When do points land (booking, check-out or after the free cancellation deadline), and what happens on a cancellation, refund or Pay at property no-show?
- Should city tax count toward a spend-based rule?
- Who pays for the points: Roamstay, the partner through commission, or both?

**6. Ownership and constraints:**
- No feature code or squad covers loyalty today, so which squad should own it, or should the artifact list ownership as an open decision?
- Are there any limits on date, budget, legal requirements or regions that I should respect?

**7. Assumptions to challenge:**
- Why does leadership keep bringing this up, and what might I get wrong about their reason?
- Is "points" the actual ask, or shorthand for a wider goal such as rewarding repeat guests?
